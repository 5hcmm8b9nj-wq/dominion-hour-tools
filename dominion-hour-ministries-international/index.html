Meet Prophet Prince

Fire Brand Prophet · Teacher of the Nations

Prophet Prince Amo David

Prophet Prince Amo David is a Fire Brand Prophet and Teacher of the Nations, called and ordained by God before the foundation of the world (Jeremiah 1:5 KJV). Based in Accra, Ghana 🇬🇭, he carries a prophetic and teaching anointing of over 20 years, raising a generation of believers who walk in power, authority, and dominion through Jesus Christ.

He is not only a prophet — he is an equipping voice, set apart to train and activate believers in the gifts of the Spirit, in prophetic prayer, and in declarations that enforce the Kingdom of God on earth. Through his ministry, thousands have been equipped to stay delivered, reign in life, and live as more than conquerors in Christ Jesus.
