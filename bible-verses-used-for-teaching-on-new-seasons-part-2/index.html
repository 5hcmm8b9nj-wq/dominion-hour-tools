Know Before You Pray

The Three Words

David uses all three in Psalm 51. Each one is different. Each one needs its own repentance.

1

Sin

chatta’ah (Hebrew)

Missing the mark. Falling short of God’s standard.

Romans 3:23 (KJV)

For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God.

2

Iniquity

avon (Hebrew)

A twisted, crooked, bent inner condition. A rooted pattern. Often generational.

Psalm 51:5 (KJV)

Behold, I was shapen in iniquity; and in sin did my mother conceive me.

3

Transgression

pesha (Hebrew)

Rebellion. Willfully crossing a known boundary.

1 Samuel 15:23 (KJV)

For rebellion is as the sin of witchcraft, and stubbornness is as iniquity and idolatry, Because thou hast rejected the word of the LORD, he hath also rejected thee from being king.

    Repeat After Me

    Closing Confession Prayer

    Merciful Father, I confess my sins and turn away from them.
    I acknowledge every sin, every iniquity, and every transgression named and unnamed.
    I renounce every participation, agreement, dedication, vow, or practice that is contrary to Your Word.
    Wash me throughly. Cleanse me. Create in me a clean heart. Renew a right spirit within me.
    Blot out my transgressions according to the multitude of Thy tender mercies.
    I plead the blood of Jesus over my life — my mind, my heart, my body, my mouth, my will, my emotions, my past, my present, and my future.
    I receive Your mercy through Jesus Christ.
    Amen.
