How to use this tool

  1. Pray walks you through nine steps in order: Before the Service, Guard My Mind Holy Spirit, When My Mind Strays, a practical readiness check, prayer for Ps Kiky and Prophet Prince, declarations, declarations from the servants who assisted the prophets, your personal declarations, short prayers and the closing prayer.
  2. Voice prompts: the player at the bottom of Pray and Service Mode reads everything aloud, with play, pause, stop, forward and rewind. Forward and rewind move one sentence at a time. Everything is read in the Charon voice. You can also choose the speed and whether the scripture is read too. On a keyboard: Space plays or pauses, the arrow keys move forward and back, and S stops.
  3. My mind is straying: in Service Mode, tap this button for a four-step refocus: pause, pray one line, declare one truth, then check the recording.
  4. Day and night: the button at the top right switches between Day, Night and Auto. Auto turns night mode on from 7 pm to 6 am.
  5. Service Mode shows the short prayers as large, silent cards while you record. It can rotate them on a timer and keeps the screen awake where the device allows.
  6. Scripture holds every verse in full KJV, grouped into eight categories, each with a prompt for writing your own declaration.
  7. My Declarations lets you write and keep personal declarations anchored to a scripture.
