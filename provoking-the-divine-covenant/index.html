Six Movements

The Teaching

Six movements through the covenant of God — each anchored in the full text of scripture, with a short word of understanding.

Movement One

God Is a Covenant-Keeping God

Deuteronomy 7:9-15 (KJV)

Know therefore that the LORD thy God, he is God, the faithful God, which keepeth covenant and mercy with them that love him and keep his commandments to a thousand generations; And repayeth them that hate him to their face, to destroy them: he will not be slack to him that hateth him, he will repay him to his face. Thou shalt therefore keep the commandments, and the statutes, and the judgments, which I command thee this day, to do them. Wherefore it shall come to pass, if ye hearken to these judgments, and keep, and do them, that the LORD thy God shall keep unto thee the covenant and the mercy which he sware unto thy fathers: And he will love thee, and bless thee, and multiply thee: he will also bless the fruit of thy womb, and the fruit of thy land, thy corn, and thy wine, and thine oil, the increase of thy kine, and the flocks of thy sheep, in the land which he sware unto thy fathers to give thee. Thou shalt be blessed above all people: there shall not be male or female barren among you, or among your cattle. And the LORD will take away from thee all sickness, and will put none of the evil diseases of Egypt, which thou knowest, upon thee; but will lay them upon all them that hate thee.

God cannot lie, and His word is exalted above His name. He keeps covenant and mercy to a thousand generations — so those in covenant with Him rest on a faithfulness that never fails.

Movement Two

Covenant Gives Legal Access

The devil operates by legality: a demonic or ancestral covenant gives the enemy legal access to a life. But your covenant with God through Jesus gives you legal access to heaven — and the higher covenant nullifies the lower. This is the ground for every declaration you will make below.

Movement Three

God Remembers His Covenant

Exodus 2:23-25 (KJV)

And it came to pass in process of time, that the king of Egypt died: and the children of Israel sighed by reason of the bondage, and they cried, and their cry came up unto God by reason of the bondage. And God heard their groaning, and God remembered his covenant with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob. And God looked upon the children of Israel, and God had respect unto them.

Israel's deliverance came because God remembered His covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. When we cry out and remind God of His covenant, He is moved to visit us.

Movement Four

Covenant Brings Kindness & Restoration

1 Samuel 18:1-4 (KJV)

And it came to pass, when he had made an end of speaking unto Saul, that the soul of Jonathan was knit with the soul of David, and Jonathan loved him as his own soul. And Saul took him that day, and would let him go no more home to his father's house. Then Jonathan and David made a covenant, because he loved him as his own soul. And Jonathan stripped himself of the robe that was upon him, and gave it to David, and his garments, even to his sword, and to his bow, and to his girdle.

2 Samuel 9:1-7 (KJV)

And David said, Is there yet any that is left of the house of Saul, that I may shew him kindness for Jonathan's sake? And there was of the house of Saul a servant whose name was Ziba. And when they had called him unto David, the king said unto him, Art thou Ziba? And he said, Thy servant is he. And the king said, Is there not yet any of the house of Saul, that I may shew the kindness of God unto him? And Ziba said unto the king, Jonathan hath yet a son, which is lame on his feet. And the king said unto him, Where is he? And Ziba said unto the king, Behold, he is in the house of Machir, the son of Ammiel, in Lodebar. Then king David sent, and fetched him out of the house of Machir, the son of Ammiel, from Lodebar. Now when Mephibosheth, the son of Jonathan, the son of Saul, was come unto David, he fell on his face, and did reverence. And David said, Mephibosheth. And he answered, Behold thy servant! And David said unto him, Fear not: for I will surely shew thee kindness for Jonathan thy father's sake, and will restore thee all the land of Saul thy father; and thou shalt eat bread at my table continually.

David honored his covenant with Jonathan by seeking out Mephibosheth and bringing him out of Lodebar to eat at the king's table continually. Covenant reaches back for the forgotten and restores them.

Movement Five

The Abrahamic Covenant Is Our Portion

Genesis 17:1-13 (KJV)

And when Abram was ninety years old and nine, the LORD appeared to Abram, and said unto him, I am the Almighty God; walk before me, and be thou perfect. And I will make my covenant between me and thee, and will multiply thee exceedingly. And Abram fell on his face: and God talked with him, saying, As for me, behold, my covenant is with thee, and thou shalt be a father of many nations. Neither shall thy name any more be called Abram, but thy name shall be Abraham; for a father of many nations have I made thee. And I will make thee exceeding fruitful, and I will make nations of thee, and kings shall come out of thee. And I will establish my covenant between me and thee and thy seed after thee in their generations for an everlasting covenant, to be a God unto thee, and to thy seed after thee. And I will give unto thee, and to thy seed after thee, the land wherein thou art a stranger, all the land of Canaan, for an everlasting possession; and I will be their God. And God said unto Abraham, Thou shalt keep my covenant therefore, thou, and thy seed after thee in their generations. This is my covenant, which ye shall keep, between me and you and thy seed after thee; Every man child among you shall be circumcised. And ye shall circumcise the flesh of your foreskin; and it shall be a token of the covenant betwixt me and you.

Galatians 3:13-17 (KJV)

Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us: for it is written, Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree: That the blessing of Abraham might come on the Gentiles through Jesus Christ; that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith. Brethren, I speak after the manner of men; Though it be but a man's covenant, yet if it be confirmed, no man disannulleth, or addeth thereto. Now to Abraham and his seed were the promises made. He saith not, And to seeds, as of many; but as of one, And to thy seed, which is Christ. And this I say, that the covenant, that was confirmed before of God in Christ, the law, which was four hundred and thirty years after, cannot disannul, that it should make the promise of none effect.

Through the blood of Jesus and new birth, the blessing of Abraham becomes your portion. This higher, confirmed covenant cannot be disannulled — and it annuls every ancestral and demonic covenant standing against you.

Movement Six

Provoke the Covenant by Sacrifice & Consistency

2 Samuel 24:24 (KJV)

And the king said unto Araunah, Nay; but I will surely buy it of thee at a price: neither will I offer burnt offerings unto the LORD my God of that which doth cost me nothing. So David bought the threshingfloor and the oxen for fifty shekels of silver.

Ephesians 1:13 (KJV)

In whom ye also trusted, after that ye heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation: in whom also after that ye believed, ye were sealed with that holy Spirit of promise,

David refused to offer God what cost him nothing. Prayer, consistency, giving, tithing, and fasting are covenant acts that move the hand of God — and communion and the anointing oil invoke and seal the covenant upon your life.

Word Study

What Does Lodebar Mean?

Lo·de·bar

The place where Mephibosheth was hidden and forgotten. Tap to reveal what the name means.

no word no pasture nothing

From the Hebrew, Lodebar carries the sense of desolation — a place of rejection, no communication, lack, barrenness, and spiritual isolation.

Pray This Aloud

The Renunciation Prayer

Speak this over your generation, your children, and your seed — by your covenant with God through Jesus.

Father, I command my generation, my children, and my seed out of every ancestral covenant by my covenant with You. My covenant with You has the potency and the power to annul, to nullify, to cancel every handwriting of ordinance written against me, contrary to the covenant I have with You. Jesus has taken everything to the cross; therefore I claim my redemption, my freedom, my longevity, my marriage, and my finances. By virtue of covenant I renounce and denounce every ancestral covenant, and I affirm my covenant with Jesus through the Abrahamic covenant. I am out of every satanic covenant. It is nullified in the name of Jesus. Amen.

Communion & the Anointing Oil

The elements are not empty symbols — they invoke and seal the covenant sealed on the cross.

Communion Invokes the Covenant

The wine and the bread remind God of the covenant sealed on the cross through the blood of His Son. As blessings are declared, those with the elements may take them in faith.

Those needing it may take a glass of water in the name of Jesus after a bad dream or an attack.

The Oil — Seal of the Holy Spirit

The anointing oil is a symbol of the seal of the Holy Spirit of promise. Those with oil may anoint themselves as a token of the covenant upon their lives.
