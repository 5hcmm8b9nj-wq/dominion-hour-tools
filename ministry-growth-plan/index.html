Growing Our Clubhouse Ministries

A practical, Bible-grounded plan to move L137 Deliverance Ministry International and Dominion Hour from under 40 attendees back to 80–90 — and beyond.

Understanding What We Are Facing

Before strategy, let us see clearly. The drop from 80–90 attendees to under 40 is not entirely a spiritual attack on our rooms — a large part of it is the platform itself.

What this means for us: the believers still on Clubhouse are the committed remnant. Our strategy is to serve that remnant deeply and pull new people in from other platforms — WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube. Clubhouse can no longer feed itself.
Step 1

Bring Outside People In

The Christian rooms that stayed strong on Clubhouse all did the same thing: they treated Clubhouse as one broadcast point in a wider network, not the whole ministry.

Dr. Francina Norman’s “Prophetic Intercession” room grew to more than 10,000 followers and about 700 daily listeners by running Clubhouse and Facebook Live in parallel, then adding Instagram Live and cross-promoting with other rooms (CBN News).

Practical steps for both ministries

  • Simulcast the audio to Facebook Live and/or YouTube Live so people who no longer open Clubhouse can still join.
  • Post the room link 30–60 minutes before each session on WhatsApp Status, ministry WhatsApp groups, Facebook, Instagram Stories, and the Linktree.
  • Cut 60–90 second clips from each session — a declaration, a prophetic word, a testimony — and post them daily to Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook Reels.
  • Over 90% of social video is watched with the sound off, so add captions to every clip (ChurchSocial).
  • End every clip and post with a clear next step: join the room, join the WhatsApp group, download the workbook.
Step 2

Guest-Moderator Swaps — the Fastest Growth Lever

Dr. Norman’s room had more than 15 moderators from 7 states and 2 countries, brought in guests like Apostle Kimberly Daniels and Apostle John Eckhardt, and a partner fundraiser across multiple rooms raised $180,000 in 10 days (CBN News).

Clubhouse and marketing guides agree: the fastest way to fill a room is to co-host with someone whose followers get pinged into your room (Kajabi), (Social Media Examiner).

Action

  • Identify 5–10 like-minded deliverance and prophetic ministers still active on Clubhouse.
  • Propose a monthly “pulpit exchange” — they moderate a session in your room, you moderate one in theirs.
  • Add 3–5 trusted intercessors from the congregation as permanent co-moderators so the room feels alive even when the pastor is briefly off-stage.
Step 3

Capture Every Visitor — Stop the Leak

The single biggest finding from Dr. Norman’s ministry: they used a Google Form to capture contact information from every online participant, then had a follow-up team pray with them, assign a “battle buddy” prayer partner, and connect them to a local church (CBN News).

Church-leadership research shows that once a regular attender misses six weeks in a row, they rarely return without direct personal outreach (Church Leadership).

Set up now

  • A one-tap intake form (name, WhatsApp, city, prayer request) linked in every room title and on the Linktree.
  • A small follow-up team who sends a personal welcome message within 24 hours.
  • A “we missed you” WhatsApp message to anyone who has not shown up in 3 consecutive sessions — not scolding, just: “praying for you, here is this week’s declaration.”
Step 4

Fix the Room Itself — Retention Beats Acquisition

Clubhouse-specific and prayer-meeting research both point to the same essentials (Litfame), (Kajabi), (Ministry Magazine).

Rock-solid schedule

Same day, same hour, every week. No cancellations, no drift. Consistency is the single biggest predictor of room growth.

Length: 45–90 minutes

Longer than that and casual listeners leave; the “faithful few” stay but no one new joins.

Strong opening hook

State the exact topic, scripture, and takeaway in the first 60 seconds. Do NOT open with 10 minutes of greetings — that is when people leave.

Compelling room titles

Not “L137 Monday Night.” Better: “Breaking Marine-Spirit Attacks on the Family — Live Deliverance Prayer 8pm EST.”

Vary the format

Teaching night, pure intercession night, testimony night, Q&A night, guest-moderator night. Never identical week to week.

Call up testimonies

Dr. Norman’s team calls audience members up to testify of God’s goodness. This pulls silent listeners into the community.

Step 5

Give People Something to Take Home

Our existing pipeline of workbooks, declaration cards, prayer manuals, and visual companion stories is the biggest asset most Clubhouse ministries simply do not have. Let us use it as a growth engine.

  • At the end of each session, announce: “Tonight’s declarations and full scripture pack — free download, link in the room title and WhatsApp group.”
  • The download page asks for their WhatsApp number so they enter our follow-up list.
  • Post the finished pack on WhatsApp and social with: “This came out of last night’s live room. Join us next Monday for Part 2.”
Step 6

Reactivate the 80–90 Who Used to Come

Handwritten-note research from Church Leadership finds that a short, non-judgmental personal message from a peer (not the pastor) brings back the highest percentage of dormant attendees (Church Leadership).

  • Build a list of everyone who used to attend regularly.
  • Split it among 4–6 intercessors.
  • Each sends a short, personal WhatsApp voice note: “We noticed you have not been on the room lately — praying for you. This Monday Pastor Kiki is teaching on [topic]. Would love to hear your voice again.”
  • Follow up with one gentle reminder on the day of the room.

A Simple 30-Day Plan

Week 1

Set up simulcast to Facebook Live. Build Google Form intake. Recruit follow-up team of 3–5 intercessors.

Week 2

Reach out to 8 dormant regulars per ministry with personal WhatsApp voice notes. Line up 2 guest moderators for the month.

Week 3

Launch daily 60–90 second clips on Reels, Shorts, and TikTok — with captions and a clear “join us live” call to action.

Week 4

Review numbers. Keep what worked, drop what didn’t. Schedule the next month’s guest-moderator swaps.
