📜 The Teaching Journey

Eight Movements Through the Word

Tap each stop on the journey — each opens the exact place Prophet Prince took us in the sermon.

  1. 🕊️

    The Impartation of the Holy Ghost

    Every believer is a candidate for the impartation of the Holy Ghost. What God is about to do in your life will not be by human strength or human wisdom — it will be by the Spirit of the Lord.

    “It is not by your strength; it is by God’s grace.”

    “Then he answered and spake unto me, saying, This is the word of the LORD unto Zerubbabel, saying, Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit, saith the LORD of hosts.” Zechariah 4:6 KJV

    The same Spirit that raised Christ from the dead dwells in you. That Spirit is the giver of victory, the giver of blessing, the giver of dominion.

    “But if the Spirit of him that raised up Jesus from the dead dwell in you, he that raised up Christ from the dead shall also quicken your mortal bodies by his Spirit that dwelleth in you.” Romans 8:11 KJV

  2. 👑

    Three Kings and the Light Thing

    Three kings — Israel, Judah, and Edom — went out to war and ran out of water. They came to Elisha. The man of God said, Bring me a minstrel. When the minstrel played, the hand of the Lord came upon him.

    “But now bring me a minstrel. And it came to pass, when the minstrel played, that the hand of the LORD came upon him.” 2 Kings 3:15 KJV

    Prophecy came: Make this valley full of ditches. No wind, no rain — yet the valley shall be filled with water. And then this: This is but a light thing in the sight of the LORD.

    “And he said, Thus saith the LORD, Make this valley full of ditches. For thus saith the LORD, Ye shall not see wind, neither shall ye see rain; yet that valley shall be filled with water… And this is but a light thing in the sight of the LORD: he will deliver the Moabites also into your hand.” 2 Kings 3:16–18 KJV

    What looks impossible to you is a light thing to God. He will give you water without wind and without rain, and He will also give you the enemy into your hand.

  3. 🌿

    The Blessing of the Anointed (Lot & Abraham)

    Lot was not the anointed — Abraham was. But because Lot was with the anointed, Lot became rich. Even the enemy noticed the blessing on Abraham’s life.

    “And Lot also, which went with Abram, had flocks, and herds, and tents.” Genesis 13:5 KJV

    When you attach yourself to the anointing, the overflow reaches you. Position yourself under Kingdom covering, and the blessing follows.

    “And I will bless them that bless thee, and curse him that curseth thee: and in thee shall all families of the earth be blessed.” Genesis 12:3 KJV

  4. 🚫

    Don’t Magnify the Giants

    Ten spies came back and said, “We are in our own sight as grasshoppers.” Their report magnified the giants and shrunk the promise. Joshua and Caleb saw the same land and said, We are well able.

    “And there we saw the giants, the sons of Anak, which come of the giants: and we were in our own sight as grasshoppers, and so we were in their sight.” Numbers 13:33 KJV

    “And Caleb stilled the people before Moses, and said, Let us go up at once, and possess it; for we are well able to overcome it.” Numbers 13:30 KJV

    Whatever you magnify, you empower. Stop giving your problem a bigger microphone than your God. Magnify the Lord — and the giants will shrink.

    “O magnify the LORD with me, and let us exalt his name together.” Psalm 34:3 KJV

  5. 🕯️

    Take Your Anointing Oil

    Take the oil. Put it on your forehead, on your hands, on your feet. Anoint your door. Anoint your workplace. Anoint your children.

    “And they shall take of the blood, and strike it on the two side posts and on the upper door post of the houses… And when I see the blood, I will pass over you, and the plague shall not be upon you to destroy you.” Exodus 12:7,13 KJV

    The anointing breaks the yoke. The anointing marks you as God’s. The oil is not decoration — it is a covenant point of contact.

    “And it shall come to pass in that day, that his burden shall be taken away from off thy shoulder, and his yoke from off thy neck, and the yoke shall be destroyed because of the anointing.” Isaiah 10:27 KJV

  6. 🕊️

    Grace to Do More

    Elisha received a double portion. Where others crawl, you will run. Where others run, you will fly. This is grace — not effort.

    “And Elisha said, I pray thee, let a double portion of thy spirit be upon me.” 2 Kings 2:9 KJV

    God is giving you the grace to do in one year what took others ten. The grace to write. The grace to build. The grace to lead. Receive it.

    “But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.” Isaiah 40:31 KJV

  7. 🌊

    Restoration & the Potter

    You are not a victim. You are the pot in the hand of the Potter. Broken vessels can be re-formed. Nothing you have lost is beyond the reach of the God who calls things that be not as though they were.

    “And the vessel that he made of clay was marred in the hand of the potter: so he made it again another vessel, as seemed good to the potter to make it.” Jeremiah 18:4 KJV

    “…God, who quickeneth the dead, and calleth those things which be not as though they were.” Romans 4:17 KJV

    Say it out loud: I am not a victim. The blood speaks better things. The Potter is not finished with me.

    “And to Jesus the mediator of the new covenant, and to the blood of sprinkling, that speaketh better things than that of Abel.” Hebrews 12:24 KJV

  8. ✝️

    The Resurrection and the Life

    Jesus said, I am the resurrection, and the life. Every dead area of your life bows to that name. Dead dreams. Dead business. Dead marriage. Dead prophecy.

    “Jesus said unto her, I am the resurrection, and the life: he that believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live.” John 11:25 KJV

    If Christ is not risen, our faith is vain — but Christ IS risen. And that same Spirit is in you. Command your morning. Command the dry ground. Fill the ditches.

    “And if Christ be not risen, then is our preaching vain, and your faith is also vain… But now is Christ risen from the dead, and become the firstfruits of them that slept.” 1 Corinthians 15:14,20 KJV

🙏 Prayer Flow

Five Movements of Prayer

Pray this out loud. Prophet Prince taught us: your mouth is a covenant instrument.

1 Enthronement · “Bring me a minstrel”

“Father, I bless Your holy name. You inhabit the praises of Your people. Let the hand of the LORD come upon me as it came upon Elisha. Let the minstrel of my mouth call down the sound of heaven.”

“But thou art holy, O thou that inhabitest the praises of Israel.” Psalm 22:3 KJV

“But now bring me a minstrel. And it came to pass, when the minstrel played, that the hand of the LORD came upon him.” 2 Kings 3:15 KJV

2 Repentance · Ephesians 1:7

“Lord Jesus, forgive every sin of thought, word, and deed. Wash me by Your blood. Let no unconfessed sin block the impartation You have prepared for me today.”

“In whom we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of his grace.” Ephesians 1:7 KJV

“If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.” 1 John 1:9 KJV

3 Impartation · Romans 8:11

“Holy Ghost, the same Spirit that raised Jesus from the dead — quicken every dead thing in me. Impart victory. Impart blessing. Impart dominion. Fall on me now in Jesus’ name.”

“But if the Spirit of him that raised up Jesus from the dead dwell in you, he that raised up Christ from the dead shall also quicken your mortal bodies by his Spirit that dwelleth in you.” Romans 8:11 KJV

4 Warfare · 2 Kings 3:18 / Luke 10:19

“Every Moabite spirit assigned against my destiny — you are delivered into my hand today. I tread on every serpent and scorpion. This is but a light thing in the sight of my God.”

“And this is but a light thing in the sight of the LORD: he will deliver the Moabites also into your hand.” 2 Kings 3:18 KJV

“Behold, I give unto you power to tread on serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy: and nothing shall by any means hurt you.” Luke 10:19 KJV

5 Sealing · Isaiah 40:31

“I mount up with wings as an eagle. I run and I am not weary. I walk and I do not faint. My valley is full of water. My enemies are under my feet. It is done in Jesus’ name. Amen.”

“But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.” Isaiah 40:31 KJV

📝 Reflection

Sit With the Word

These are for your notebook — not for scoring. Write honestly before the Lord.

“Search me, O God, and know my heart: try me, and know my thoughts: And see if there be any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting.” Psalm 139:23–24 KJV

  1. Where in my life am I still calling “impossible” what God calls “a light thing”?
  2. What giant have I been magnifying instead of magnifying the LORD?
  3. Who is my “Abraham” — the anointed I am connected to for covering and overflow?
  4. What “ditches” is God asking me to dig before I see the water?
  5. Where does the enemy still say “victim” over me — and what does the blood of Jesus say?
  6. What area of my life needs the oil today: my head, my hands, my house, or my children?
  7. What does the “grace to do more” look like for me this week?
