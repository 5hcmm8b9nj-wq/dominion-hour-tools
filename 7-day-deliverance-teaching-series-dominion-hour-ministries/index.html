Day 01

Foundations of Deliverance: The Finished Work of the Cross

Objective — Establish that deliverance is rooted in the completed work of Jesus Christ, not human effort.

Key Scriptures

  • Colossians 2:13–15 — “Having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it.”
  • Isaiah 53:4–5 — Healing and wholeness purchased at Calvary.
  • 1 John 3:8 — “For this purpose the Son of God was manifested, that he might destroy the works of the devil.”
  • Luke 4:18 — Jesus’ mission statement: liberty to the captives.

Teaching Points

  1. Deliverance is part of “the children’s bread” (Matthew 15:26) — a covenant benefit for believers.
  2. The cross legally disarmed every accuser; deliverance is enforcing that victory.
  3. We do not fight FOR victory; we fight FROM victory.
  4. The four ministry streams of Jesus: preaching, teaching, healing, deliverance (Matthew 4:23–24).

Prayer Focus

Thank the Lord for the finished work of Calvary. Ask the Holy Spirit to open eyes to see Christ’s victory personally.

Declarations

“Jesus Christ has triumphed over every principality and power on my behalf.”

“I receive the full benefits of the cross — salvation, healing, and deliverance.”

Reflection Assignment

Read Colossians 2 in full. Journal one area where you need to apply Christ’s finished work.

Day 02

Knowing Your Authority in Christ

Objective — Teach the believer’s identity, position, and delegated authority.

Key Scriptures

  • Luke 10:19 — “Behold, I give unto you power to tread on serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy.”
  • Ephesians 1:17–23; 2:6 — Seated with Christ in heavenly places.
  • Matthew 28:18–20 — All authority belongs to Jesus, delegated through the Great Commission.
  • Mark 16:17 — “In my name shall they cast out devils.”

Teaching Points

  1. Authority (exousia) vs. power (dunamis) — both given to the believer.
  2. Authority flows from identity: child of God, ambassador, joint-heir (Romans 8:16–17).
  3. The name of Jesus is the legal seal of our authority.
  4. Submission to God precedes resistance to the enemy (James 4:7).

Prayer Focus

Pray Ephesians 1:17–23 over the group. Ask for the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of Christ.

Declarations

“I am seated with Christ above every principality and power.”

“In the name of Jesus, I have authority over every work of darkness assigned against my life.”

Reflection Assignment

Write out your identity in Christ using 10 Scriptures (e.g., 2 Corinthians 5:17, 1 Peter 2:9, Romans 8:37).

Day 03

The Battlefield of the Mind: Renewing & Casting Down Strongholds

Objective — Equip believers to identify and dismantle mental and emotional strongholds.

Key Scriptures

  • 2 Corinthians 10:3–5 — Weapons mighty through God to the pulling down of strongholds.
  • Romans 12:2 — “Be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind.”
  • Philippians 4:6–8 — Guarding the mind through prayer and right thinking.
  • Isaiah 26:3 — Perfect peace through a mind stayed on God.

Teaching Points

  1. A stronghold is a pattern of thinking that exalts itself against the knowledge of God.
  2. Common strongholds: fear, rejection, shame, unbelief, lust, pride, bitterness.
  3. The renewal process: identify the lie → replace with the Word → repent of agreement → declare truth.
  4. Word-based meditation is a deliverance weapon (Joshua 1:8; Psalm 1).

Prayer Focus

Lead the group in identifying one stronghold and bringing it under the lordship of Christ. Pray for the mind of Christ (1 Corinthians 2:16).

Declarations

“Every imagination that exalts itself against the knowledge of God is cast down in Jesus’ name.”

“I have the mind of Christ and I think His thoughts after Him.”

Reflection Assignment

List 3 recurring negative thought patterns. For each, find a Scripture that counters it and memorize it.

Day 04

Generational Patterns & Bloodline Freedom

Objective — Address inherited patterns and apply the redemptive power of Christ’s blood.

Key Scriptures

  • Exodus 20:5–6 — Iniquity visited to the third and fourth generation; mercy to thousands.
  • Galatians 3:13–14 — “Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law.”
  • Ezekiel 18:19–23 — Personal responsibility under the New Covenant.
  • Hebrews 9:14; 1 Peter 1:18–19 — The cleansing power of the blood of Jesus.

Teaching Points

  1. Generational patterns are real but not stronger than the blood of Jesus.
  2. Common areas: poverty, divorce, addiction, premature death, sickness, anger, idolatry.
  3. The believer renounces, repents on behalf of self and ancestors (Nehemiah 1:6; Daniel 9:5–19), and pleads the blood.
  4. Freedom is sealed by walking in the opposite spirit (e.g., generosity replaces poverty mindset).

Prayer Focus

Lead a corporate prayer renouncing known generational patterns and applying the blood of Jesus.

Declarations

“I am redeemed from the curse of the law by the blood of Jesus Christ.”

“Every generational pattern of [name it] is broken off my life and lineage in Jesus’ name.”

Reflection Assignment

Draft a personal “freedom prayer” listing patterns you’ve observed in your family line and Scripture-based renunciations.

Day 05

Forgiveness, Repentance & Closing Open Doors

Objective — Identify legal entry points the enemy uses and close them through repentance and forgiveness.

Key Scriptures

  • Matthew 6:14–15; 18:21–35 — The link between forgiveness and freedom.
  • 1 John 1:9 — Confession brings cleansing.
  • Ephesians 4:26–27 — Unresolved anger gives “place” (topos) to the devil.
  • Acts 19:18–19 — Renouncing and removing occult/sinful items.

Teaching Points

  1. Common open doors: unforgiveness, unrepented sin, occult involvement, ungodly soul ties, sinful covenants/oaths, trauma agreements.
  2. Forgiveness is a choice, not a feeling; it releases the offender to God and frees you.
  3. Repentance is a change of mind and direction, not just regret (2 Corinthians 7:10).
  4. Practical step: list, renounce, and where appropriate, destroy ungodly objects/agreements.

Prayer Focus

A guided forgiveness prayer (name by name, before the Lord) and a prayer of repentance for known sin.

Declarations

“I forgive [name] as Christ has forgiven me, and I release them to God.”

“Every open door to the enemy is closed and sealed by the blood of Jesus.”

Reflection Assignment

Make a private list of people to forgive and any items/agreements to renounce. Bring it to the Lord this week.

Day 06

The Armor of God & Walking in Daily Victory

Objective — Train believers to put on the full armor and engage in daily spiritual warfare.

Key Scriptures

  • Ephesians 6:10–18 — The full armor.
  • Psalm 91 — The covenant of protection.
  • Revelation 12:11 — Overcoming by the blood of the Lamb and the word of their testimony.
  • James 4:7 — Submit, resist, and the enemy must flee.

Teaching Points

  1. Each piece of armor is a Person and a practice: truth, righteousness, peace, faith, salvation, the Word, prayer.
  2. The armor is “put on” daily by faith and confession.
  3. Watch and pray (Matthew 26:41) — the discipline of a praying believer.
  4. Testimony is a weapon — speak what God has done.

Prayer Focus

Pray through each piece of the armor aloud, applying it to the day ahead.

Declarations

“I am strong in the Lord and in the power of His might.”

“No weapon formed against me shall prosper.” (Isaiah 54:17)

Reflection Assignment

Create a personal morning prayer routine using Ephesians 6 and Psalm 91.

Day 07

Walking in Freedom: Maintaining Deliverance & Discipleship

Objective — Equip the delivered believer to remain free and grow in discipleship.

Key Scriptures

  • John 8:31–36 — “If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed.”
  • Galatians 5:1 — Stand fast in liberty.
  • Matthew 12:43–45 — The danger of an empty, unfilled life after deliverance.
  • 2 Peter 1:3–10 — Adding to your faith for fruitfulness.

Teaching Points

  1. Deliverance without discipleship invites return — the house must be filled with the Spirit and the Word.
  2. Daily disciplines: Word intake, prayer, worship, fellowship, fasting (as led), serving.
  3. Accountability and godly community (Hebrews 10:24–25).
  4. Testimony, then ministry — freed people free people (2 Corinthians 1:3–4).

Prayer Focus

A consecration prayer dedicating the next season to walking in freedom, fruitfulness, and ministry to others.

Declarations

“I stand fast in the liberty wherewith Christ has made me free.”

“I am filled with the Holy Spirit, and my life bears the fruit of freedom.”

Reflection Assignment

Write a one-page testimony of what the Lord did during this 7-day series, and identify one person to share it with.
