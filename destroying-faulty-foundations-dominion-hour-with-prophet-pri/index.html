Teaching Modules

Nine modules drawn directly from the teaching. Click each to expand.

Module 1 · Praise, Thanksgiving, and Preparation

Before any deliverance can take place, the ground must be prepared. Prophet Prince opens the service with corporate praise and thanksgiving — the biblical entry point into God's presence.

Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, and into his courts with praise: be thankful unto him, and bless his name. Psalm 100:4 KJV

Key point: Praise is not decoration — it is the doorway. We do not push our way into deliverance; we sing our way in and God brings us to the altar.

Module 2 · Confession, Forgiveness, and the Holy Spirit

Unconfessed sin and unforgiveness are legal doors that hold demons in place. Before we bind anything, we deal with what gives the enemy a right to speak.

Have mercy upon me, O God, according to thy lovingkindness: according unto the multitude of thy tender mercies blot out my transgressions. Wash me throughly from mine iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin. For I acknowledge my transgressions: and my sin is ever before me. Psalm 51:1-3 KJV
Behold, the Lord's hand is not shortened, that it cannot save; neither his ear heavy, that it cannot hear: But your iniquities have separated between you and your God, and your sins have hid his face from you, that he will not hear. Isaiah 59:1-2 KJV

Practical: Confess by name. Forgive by name. Then invite the Holy Spirit — never operate a deliverance in your own strength.

Module 3 · What Is a Foundation?

If the foundations be destroyed, what can the righteous do? Psalm 11:3 KJV

Every building has a foundation. The visible glory of the superstructure is sustained by what has been buried underground. Our lives are the same. Your foundation is your background, your ancestry, your lineage, your bloodline, the family you were born into.

For we are labourers together with God: ye are God's husbandry, ye are God's building. 1 Corinthians 3:9 KJV

Key point: The foundation determines the height. You cannot lay the foundation of a one-story building and put a ten-story building on it — it will collapse.

Module 4 · How Foundations Become Faulty

Before Christianity reached most bloodlines, forefathers were serving idols — worshipping the sun, trees, rivers, consulting mediums and fetish priests. In every one of these transactions, covenants were made and the family line was handed to demonic spirits in exchange for wealth, protection, or victory.

As long as that covenant remains unrenounced, the foundation is faulty. That is why a brilliant young man cannot find or keep a job. That is why a beautiful, God-fearing young woman cannot marry, or marries and the marriage collapses. That is why premature death repeats at the same age across generations. That is why barrenness runs through a family. These are not coincidence — they are the voice of a faulty foundation.

Module 5 · Jericho — The Curse of a Rebuilt Foundation

And Joshua adjured them at that time, saying, Cursed be the man before the Lord, that riseth up and buildeth this city Jericho: he shall lay the foundation thereof in his firstborn, and in his youngest son shall he set up the gates of it. So the Lord was with Joshua; and his fame was noised throughout all the country. Joshua 6:26-27 KJV
In his days did Hiel the Bethelite build Jericho: he laid the foundation thereof in Abiram his firstborn, and set up the gates thereof in his youngest son Segub, according to the word of the Lord, which he spake by the son of Nun. 1 Kings 16:34 KJV

Key point: The curse Joshua spoke waited over 500 years — and it still killed Hiel's sons the moment he rebuilt on that foundation. Cursed foundations do not expire with time. They wait to be either renounced or activated.

Module 6 · Elisha at Jericho — Healing a Bad Source

And the men of the city said unto Elisha, Behold, I pray thee, the situation of this city is pleasant, as my lord seeth: but the water is naught, and the ground barren. And he said, Bring me a new cruse, and put salt therein. And they brought it to him. And he went forth unto the spring of the waters, and cast the salt in there, and said, Thus saith the Lord, I have healed these waters; there shall not be from thence any more death or barren land. So the waters were healed unto this day, according to the saying of Elisha which he spake. 2 Kings 2:19-22 KJV

Key point: Elisha did not treat the leaves or the fruit — he went to the spring. Every barren womb, every early death, every failed harvest downstream came from one bad source. In Christ, the true "new cruse" is the finished work of Calvary and the true "salt" that heals the source is the blood of Jesus. We do not put salt in bathwater — we plead the blood over the source of the bloodline.

Module 7 · Built on the Rock — Jesus the Chief Cornerstone

Now therefore ye are no more strangers and foreigners, but fellowcitizens with the saints, and of the household of God; And are built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone. Ephesians 2:19-20 KJV
Therefore whosoever heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them, I will liken him unto a wise man, which built his house upon a rock: And the rain descended, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house; and it fell not: for it was founded upon a rock. And every one that heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them not, shall be likened unto a foolish man, which built his house upon the sand: And the rain descended, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house; and it fell: and great was the fall of it. Matthew 7:24-27 KJV

Key point: Salvation gives you a new spirit and a new lineage. Jesus becomes your new foundation and your chief cornerstone. But the physical consequences of the altars raised over your bloodline must be consciously renounced and cast down in prayer.

Module 8 · Deliverance — Enforcing Victory From the End Point

Lord, thou hast been our dwelling place in all generations. Before the mountains were brought forth, or ever thou hadst formed the earth and the world, even from everlasting to everlasting, thou art God. Psalm 90:1-2 KJV

Key point: Your victory was declared from the end point. Revelation calls Jesus the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world — before there was a curse, there was a Cross. Deliverance is not begging God to defeat the enemy; it is enforcing a victory Christ already accomplished.

Deal with faulty foundations in three steps:

  1. Get a revelation of the problem. Stop calling generational patterns "normal."
  2. Raise a superior altar. The blood of Jesus — the only altar that overrules every other altar.
  3. Engage in aggressive prayer. Uproot every plant your heavenly Father did not plant (Matthew 15:13).

Module 9 · Sealing and Aftercare

For he looked for a city which hath foundations, whose builder and maker is God. Hebrews 11:10 KJV

Every deliverance service closes by sealing what God has done under the blood of Jesus. But sealing is not the end — it is the doorway into aftercare. What God does in one hour of prayer must be maintained in daily walk.

Aftercare pillars: daily Bible study, personal prayer, worship, Christian fellowship, renewing the mind, cutting off the former sinful patterns, and seeking qualified pastoral and medical support when appropriate.

Prayer Library

Deliverance prayer points drawn from the teaching, organized by section. Pray them slowly in your private altar.

1 Praise and Worship

Father, I bless Your name. Every good thing in my life came from You. I lift up worship as my first act, before any other prayer, in the name of Jesus.

2 Thanksgiving

Thank You, Lord, for the breath in my lungs. Thank You for salvation. Thank You for keeping me and my household. I enter Your gates with thanksgiving, and Your courts with praise, in the name of Jesus.

3 Confession of Sins

Father, according to Psalm 51, have mercy upon me. Blot out my transgressions. Wash me throughly from mine iniquity. I confess every known sin, and I ask for the cleansing of every hidden thing, in Jesus' name.

4 Forgiveness of Others

Father, I release every person who has hurt me, offended me, or wounded my family. I forgive them by name and by memory. I refuse to let unforgiveness give the enemy a legal door in my life, in Jesus' name.

5 Inviting the Holy Spirit

Holy Spirit, I welcome You. Come and lead this prayer. Come and lead this deliverance. I refuse to operate in my own strength. Move by Your power, in Jesus' name.

6 Renouncing Foundation Covenants

Father, in the name of Jesus, I renounce every covenant my forefathers made with idols, shrines, mediums, fetish priests, rivers, trees, or any demonic source. I renounce every exchange made for wealth, protection, or victory. I disconnect my life and my bloodline from those covenants by the blood of Jesus.
Every altar raised over my bloodline that is speaking against my destiny, I command you to be silent by the blood of Jesus. I overrule you by the superior altar of Calvary, in the mighty name of Jesus.

7 Breaking Faulty Foundations

Every faulty foundation in my life, catch fire right now. Every foundation not laid by my heavenly Father, be uprooted, in the name of Jesus.
By the finished work of Christ, every pattern of premature death in my family — I break you today. Every pattern of barrenness, poverty, marital collapse, sickness, or failure at the edge of breakthrough — I break you, in the mighty name of Jesus.
Every plant my heavenly Father hath not planted in my life, be rooted up. Matthew 15:13 — I enforce it now, in the name of Jesus.

8 Rebuilding on the Rock

Lord Jesus, You are my chief cornerstone. I lay my life on Your finished work. Build me on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, with You as the chief corner stone. Let every future thing You do in me stand on this Rock, in Jesus' name.

9 Repossession

Every good thing the enemy has held back from my life — my finances, my health, my marriage, my ministry, my open doors — I take possession today, in the mighty name of Jesus.
I release the anointing on every genuine door of provision God has appointed for me. Divine connections, locate me now. Divine helpers, locate me now, in Jesus' name.

10 Healing and Restoration

Father, every place the enemy has wounded — in my body, in my soul, in my mind, in my emotions — send Your healing hand. Reverse every unreversible report. Let the presence of God bring comfort and restoration, in Jesus' name.

11 Sealing by the Blood

Father, I seal everything You have done in this prayer under the blood of Jesus. Every declaration is sealed. Every deliverance is sealed. Every prophetic word is sealed. Let no witchcraft, no monitoring spirit, no ancestral voice reverse what God has done. In the mighty name of Jesus, amen.
