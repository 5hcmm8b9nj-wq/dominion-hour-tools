Prophet Prince opens with worship and welcomes the altar: “Open the floodgates of heaven. Let it rain. Let it rain.” He reminds us there are levels in God, and the tormented soul must be brought back to the secret place.

Teaching

2. Empty the Vessel First — Consecration

“Before God can fill you, you must consecrate, you must purify yourself, you must prepare the vessel in the name of Jesus.”

The Lord told Moses, Tell my people to consecrate themselves for three days and let them come to that mount. I will speak to them. I will reveal my glory to them (see Exodus 19:10–11). The people could not stand because they were full of the flesh, full of themselves, full of worldly things. Moses could stand, receive, and distribute — because he consecrated himself, he purged himself, he cleaned the vessel.

New wine needs new wineskin. If you pour the new into the old, it bursts. Prepare yourself.

Scripture · Teaching Text

3. The Widow's Empty Vessels

2 Kings 4:1–4 (KJV)
Now there cried a certain woman of the wives of the sons of the prophets unto Elisha, saying, Thy servant my husband is dead; and thou knowest that thy servant did fear the LORD: and the creditor is come to take unto him my two sons to be bondmen. And Elisha said unto her, What shall I do for thee? tell me, what hast thou in the house? And she said, Thine handmaid hath not any thing in the house, save a pot of oil. Then he said, Go, borrow thee vessels abroad of all thy neighbours, even empty vessels; borrow not a few. And when thou art come in, thou shalt shut the door upon thee and upon thy sons, and shalt pour out into all those vessels, and thou shalt set aside that which is full.

The debtors have come. They are ready to take something precious. The enemy is demanding your marital peace, your marital blessing, your children, your glory, your health. But there is an anointing, and this anointing is going to pay back.

God always gives seed to the sower. He said to Isaac, “You don't need to go and look for help. The help of man is but a little.” Look for what can help you sow, what God can multiply.

Ask yourself: Are you coming with an empty vessel, or a heart full of negativity and bitterness? Empty your heart. Empty your life. Say, “Lord, take over.”

Teaching

6. Present Your Bodies — Total Surrender

“Human effort, status, and worldly pursuit leave an inner dry place that only God can satisfy.” The only One who can satisfy your inner man is God — the joy of the Lord, joy unspeakable, the renewer of your mind, the strength of the spirit.

Surrender the places where you need help: unforgiveness, quick temper, lust, disobedience, anger, self-control. God fills hearts that are fully yielded.

Romans 12:1–8 (KJV)

And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God. For I say, through the grace given unto me, to every man that is among you, not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think; but to think soberly, according as God hath dealt to every man the measure of faith. Romans 15:13 (KJV)

“A lot of us — we don't have patience. We don't allow the Potter to build us, to mould us. You are like a mango fruit that is not ripe. You want it on the silver platter. You want it fast. Come to a place of building. Come to a place where I can build myself in you, and you become like a warrior, a mighty man — like David in the wilderness, empowered by God. No Goliath can stand against you.”

Teaching

8. Release Bitterness — The Joseph Test

“Joseph was embittered. He was full of bitterness — the treatment his brothers took him through, the dream that led him into the house of Potiphar. God said, ‘Until you break away from the realm of bitterness and begin to become an intercessor, a giver, a helper, a covenant walker, you can never ascend into your realm of glory. You can never ascend into the seat of prime minister. You continue to become a prisoner.’”

Job 19:25 (KJV)
For I know that my redeemer liveth, and that he shall stand at the latter day upon the earth.

Job said, Though he slay me, yet will I trust in him (Job 13:15). And when Job prayed for his friends, God restored him double of what he had lost.

The prophet's testimony: “My own friend pastors, my own friends stood up against me. Said all sorts of things because I was making headway — out of jealousy, out of envy. I said, ‘I will never forgive them.’ I was in forty days of fasting and prayer. The Lord said, ‘Begin to release them. Call them and ask of them. Call them and bless them.’ I started weeping. And when I released them, that was the beginning of my breakthrough.”

10. Abigail, Esther, Ruth — The Vessel God Fills

“Esther did not only win the heart of the king by her beauty, but by the way this woman talked to the king. Ruth — when Boaz saw Ruth, it wasn't only her beauty. It was the way she treated Naomi, the mother-in-law. When you do that, I tell you, you will have the kingdom, the whole kingdom, not even the half of it.”

Abigail (1 Samuel 25) — the woman who knew how to talk, who feared God, who kept her hope in God. She married a foolish man, Nabal, a very wealthy but foolish man. She was able to manage him, was able to stay with him until this man died. And David said, “I have to marry this woman.”

The exhortation: “Let everybody say you are full. Let everybody insult you. Continue in the service of God, continue in the fear of God, continue in the love of God. Don't raise yourself above anyone; humble yourself, because God elevates the humble hearts.”

Recall · Say It In Your Own Words

Q1. In your own words, explain why Prophet Prince says you must come to God with an empty vessel, not a full one.

You come empty so God can fill you. If your heart is already full — of bitterness, negativity, self, worldly things, or the old wineskin — the new wine cannot enter. Like Moses on the mountain who was clean and could receive, or the widow whose empty vessels became full of oil, emptiness is the invitation for God's overflow.

Q2. The prophet said Joseph could not ascend to the palace until he broke away from a certain realm. Name it and explain how release brought his elevation.

Joseph had to break away from the realm of bitterness. Until he became an intercessor, a giver, a helper, and a covenant walker — until he chose not to take revenge on his brothers — he would have remained a prisoner. Bitterness would have killed the prophecy given to Abraham. Release brings elevation; prison becomes palace only after the heart is free.

Q3. How does giving (dresses, shoes, offerings, orphanage visits) connect to breaking yokes in this teaching?

Fasting goes with giving. Giving lets something leave your life so something new can enter. It is not prayer alone that breaks yokes — sowing what you already have (clothes, food, cash, kindness) becomes a seed God can bless and multiply. The prophet's testimony: the more he gave away his own dresses and shoes, the more God opened doors for supply.
