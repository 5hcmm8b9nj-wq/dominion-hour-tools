📜 The Teaching Journey · Part 2

Eight Movements Through the Word

Tap each stop on the journey — each opens the exact place Prophet Prince Amo David took us in the Part 2 teaching.

  1. 🕊️

    The Transfer of the Anointing

    Impartation is a transfer. Prophet Prince Amo David said it plainly: “The transfer, the invitation, is a transfer of power, transfer of grace, transfer of anointing. He is going to take from his office, he is going to take from his bank account and give it to you. He is going to make you to have the double.”

    “He is going to make you to have the double.”

    At the Jordan, Elisha did not ask for money or position. He asked for the Spirit — and he asked for double.

    “And it came to pass, when they were gone over, that Elijah said unto Elisha, Ask what I shall do for thee, before I be taken away from thee. And Elisha said, I pray thee, let a double portion of thy spirit be upon me.” 2 Kings 2:9 KJV

    “And he said, Thou hast asked a hard thing: nevertheless, if thou see me when I am taken from thee, it shall be so unto thee; but if not, it shall not be so.” 2 Kings 2:10 KJV

  2. 💧

    The Jordan Divided — Twice

    Elijah took his mantle, wrapped it together, and smote the waters — and they parted. The two of them walked over on dry ground.

    “And Elijah took his mantle, and wrapped it together, and smote the waters, and they were divided hither and thither, so that they two went over on dry ground.” 2 Kings 2:8 KJV

    Then Elijah was taken. Elisha picked up the same mantle, came back to the same Jordan, and cried out: Where is the LORD God of Elijah? The waters parted again. Same God. Second generation.

    “And he took the mantle of Elijah that fell from him, and smote the waters, and said, Where is the LORD God of Elijah? and when he also had smitten the waters, they parted hither and thither: and Elisha went over.” 2 Kings 2:14 KJV

  3. 🤲

    Honor: The Poured-Water Servant

    The first quality that attracts the mantle is honor. When Israel needed a prophet, Elisha was identified not by his gift but by his honor: he was the one which poured water on the hands of Elijah.

    “…Here is Elisha the son of Shaphat, which poured water on the hands of Elijah.” 2 Kings 3:11 KJV

    The Shunammite woman honored the prophet the same way — she built him a chamber, a bed, a table, a stool, and a candlestick.

    “And she said unto her husband, Behold now, I perceive that this is an holy man of God, which passeth by us continually. Let us make a little chamber, I pray thee, on the wall; and let us set for him there a bed, and a table, and a stool, and a candlestick: and it shall be, when he cometh to us, that he shall turn in thither.” 2 Kings 4:9–10 KJV

    Honor is not optional. It carries a promise.

    “Honour thy father and thy mother: that thy days may be long upon the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee.” Exodus 20:12 KJV

  4. 🤝

    Loyal Service: I Will Not Leave Thee

    From Gilgal to Bethel to Jericho to Jordan, Elijah told Elisha to stay behind. Every single time, Elisha refused to leave.

    “And Elijah said unto Elisha, Tarry here, I pray thee; for the LORD hath sent me to Bethel. And Elisha said unto him, As the LORD liveth, and as thy soul liveth, I will not leave thee. So they went down to Bethel.” 2 Kings 2:2 KJV

    Loyal service is not one moment of enthusiasm — it is staying when it becomes inconvenient. The double portion came at the last stop, not the first.

  5. 🛡️

    Defender of the Faith

    Repeat after me: “I am a faith defender. God is building me. The world will hear of me.”

    Elijah stood alone and thought he was the only one left — but God had preserved seven thousand who had not bowed to Baal.

    “And he said, I have been very jealous for the LORD God of hosts: because the children of Israel have forsaken thy covenant, thrown down thine altars, and slain thy prophets with the sword; and I, even I only, am left; and they seek my life, to take it away.” 1 Kings 19:14 KJV

    “Yet I have left me seven thousand in Israel, all the knees which have not bowed unto Baal, and every mouth which hath not kissed him.” 1 Kings 19:18 KJV

    You are not hiding your faith. You are a sign and a wonder in your generation.

    “Behold, I and the children whom the LORD hath given me are for signs and for wonders in Israel from the LORD of hosts, which dwelleth in mount Zion.” Isaiah 8:18 KJV

    “Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.” Hebrews 11:1 KJV

  6. 🦅

    Patience — Wait on the LORD

    Elisha waited. He did not seize the mantle — he waited until it fell. Patience is the fourth quality that qualifies a person for the transfer.

    “But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.” Isaiah 40:31 KJV

    “But let patience have her perfect work, that ye may be perfect and entire, wanting nothing.” James 1:4 KJV

    What God is building in you while you wait is part of the inheritance.

  7. 🚪

    Every Closed Door Opens

    Prophet Prince Amo David declared: “This morning, every stagnation, every closed door, every delay holding you back — break in the name of Jesus. Every dormant prophetic word — activated.”

    “And to the angel of the church in Philadelphia write; These things saith he that is holy, he that is true, he that hath the key of David, he that openeth, and no man shutteth; and shutteth, and no man openeth.” Revelation 3:7 KJV

    “For yet a little while, and he that shall come will come, and will not tarry.” Hebrews 10:37 KJV

    “So shall my word be that goeth forth out of my mouth: it shall not return unto me void, but it shall accomplish that which I please, and it shall prosper in the thing whereto I sent it.” Isaiah 55:11 KJV

    Then take your oil and anoint yourself. “This morning, oil of divine favor speak for you. But not just speak — divine acceleration, allowing you to pass delays.”

    “But my horn shalt thou exalt like the horn of an unicorn: I shall be anointed with fresh oil.” Psalm 92:10 KJV

  8. 🌊

    Walk Over Your Jordan

    Peter heard one word — Come — and walked on the water. That is the picture of walking over your Jordan.

    “And Peter answered him and said, Lord, if it be thou, bid me come unto thee on the water. And he said, Come. And when Peter was come down out of the ship, he walked on the water, to go to Jesus.” Matthew 14:28–29 KJV

    Repeat after me: “I refuse to be lukewarm. I connect to the fire of the Holy Ghost. I will not backslide.”

    “I indeed baptize you with water unto repentance: but he that cometh after me is mightier than I, whose shoes I am not worthy to bear: he shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire.” Matthew 3:11 KJV

    You are not an ordinary person. You carry the power of God, and fresh oil rests upon your head.

    “But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me both in Jerusalem, and in all Judaea, and in Samaria, and unto the uttermost part of the earth.” Acts 1:8 KJV

📝 Reflection

Sit With the Word

These are for your notebook — not for scoring. Write honestly before the Lord.

“Search me, O God, and know my heart: try me, and know my thoughts: And see if there be any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting.” Psalm 139:23–24 KJV

  1. What mantle has God assigned to my life that I have not yet stretched out my hand to receive?
  2. Whom in my life am I called to honor — parents, spiritual fathers and mothers, elders — and where have I dishonored them?
  3. Where have I been a “leaving” servant instead of a “staying” one — quick to walk away when it got difficult?
  4. What faith am I defending publicly? Or am I silent when I should be speaking?
  5. Where is God asking me to wait when everyone around me is rushing?
  6. What closed door in my life has been closed the longest — and am I willing to command it to open now, in the name of Jesus?
  7. Where does God want me to “walk on water” — to step into something impossible at His invitation?
