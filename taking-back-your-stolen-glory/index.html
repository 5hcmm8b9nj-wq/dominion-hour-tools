Journaling Workbook

These prompts are arranged for personal prayer, reflection, and ministry follow-through, with writing space built directly into each module.

1. The Lord My Shield and Glory

Scriptures: Psalm 3:1–3

Lord, You are my shield, my glory, and the lifter of my head. I refuse shame, defeat, and demotion. I arise into the glory You ordained for me.
  • Describe one area of life where your head has been bowed by shame, delay, defeat, or reproach.
  • Rewrite Psalm 3:3 in your own words as a personal confession.
  • Record what it means for the Lord to be both your shield and your glory.

2. Praise That Restores Glory

Scriptures: Psalm 103:1–6

My soul blesses the Lord. He forgives, heals, redeems, and crowns my life with mercy. My glory is being restored.
  • List five benefits of the Lord you can identify in this present season.
  • Write a thanksgiving paragraph beginning, “Lord, I bless You because…”
  • Reflect on how praise shifts your focus from loss to redemption.

3. Cleansing Before Glory

Scripture: 1 John 1:8–9

I confess my sins before the Lord and receive cleansing from all unrighteousness. Every accusation against my destiny is silenced by the blood of Jesus.
  • Write down any area where the Holy Spirit is calling you to repentance.
  • Note how guilt, fear, or condemnation has affected your confidence.
  • Complete this sentence: “Lord, I receive Your cleansing in the area of…”

4. The Holy Spirit Helps Us Pray

Scriptures: Romans 8:26–27

Holy Spirit, help my weakness in prayer and intercede for me according to the will of God concerning my stolen glory.
  • Describe a situation where you do not know how to pray as you should.
  • Write a surrender prayer yielding that burden to the Holy Spirit.
  • Record any insight, impression, or burden that comes while waiting quietly before God.

5. Double Honor and Restoration

Scriptures: Isaiah 61:1–9; Jeremiah 30:17

For every shame I have endured, the Lord gives double honor. He restores my health, heals my wounds, and recovers what was stolen.
  • Identify the areas where shame or loss has touched your life.
  • Describe what “double” restoration would look like in those same areas.
  • Write a decree beginning, “In place of shame, the Lord will give…”

6. Reversing Ichabod

Scripture: 1 Samuel 4:12–22

Every Ichabod over my life, household, and assignment is reversed. The glory of God will not depart from me.
  • Reflect on any season where God’s presence felt distant or your fire felt low.
  • Write what must be restored on your altar of prayer, obedience, and consecration.
  • Complete: “Lord, I choose to value Your presence above…”

7. Crowned with Glory and Dominion

Scripture: Psalm 8:4–6

In Christ, I am crowned with glory and honor. I reject defeat and walk in dominion over every force resisting my destiny.
  • In your own words, describe how God sees you according to Psalm 8.
  • Where have you been living below the level of dominion assigned to you?
  • Write one practical action step that reflects spiritual dominion this week.

8. Manifested Glory and Light

Scriptures: John 2:8–11; Acts 9:3; Genesis 1:1–4

The glory of Jesus will be manifested in my life. Let there be light in every place where my glory has been hidden or covered.
  • Describe any hidden season of preparation you have walked through.
  • Name the areas where you need divine light, clarity, and open manifestation.
  • Write a prayer beginning, “Lord, manifest Your glory in my life through…”

Student Q&A Workbook

This section is prepared for teaching, class review, or follow-up after the Clubhouse session, using direct questions tied to the same Scripture flow.

1. Psalm 3:3 — My Shield and GloryWhat does it mean for God to be your shield, your glory, and the lifter of your head? In what specific area do you need this promise today?
2. Psalm 103:1–6 — Praise and BenefitsList the benefits of the Lord mentioned in this passage. Which one most speaks to the restoration of your glory, and why?
3. 1 John 1:8–9 — Confession and CleansingWhat happens when we confess our sins? Why is cleansing necessary when dealing with destiny, restoration, and glory retrieval?
4. Romans 8:26–27 — The Holy Spirit Helps Us PrayHow does the Holy Spirit help us in weakness? Describe a real situation where you need His intercession now.
5. Isaiah 61 and Jeremiah 30:17 — Double Honor and HealingWhat does God promise in place of shame? What does restoration of wounds look like in practical life and ministry?
6. 1 Samuel 4:12–22 — IchabodWhat does Ichabod represent in this passage? How can a believer guard and honor the presence of God?
7. Psalm 8:4–6 — Crowned with GloryHow has God honored humanity in this text? Where should you begin to live in greater dominion?
8. John 2:11 and Acts 9:3 — Manifested Glory and Divine EncounterHow did Jesus manifest His glory? How can a divine encounter redirect a life and uncover hidden purpose?
9. Deuteronomy 34:7 and Luke 1:13, 19–20 — Strength and Answered PrayerWhat do these passages teach about endurance and delayed prayer? How do they encourage you personally?
10. Genesis 1:1–4 — Let There Be LightName one area of darkness, confusion, or delay where you are praying for God to speak light, and explain what you are expecting Him to do.
