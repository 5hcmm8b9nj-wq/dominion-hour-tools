To the L137 Deliverance Ministry & Dominion Hour Family

With hearts full of gratitude, we honor and thank God for the faithful lives and dedicated service of Pastor Kiky Ocansey of L137 Deliverance Ministry and Prophet Prince Amo David of Dominion Hour with Prophet Prince Amo David.
