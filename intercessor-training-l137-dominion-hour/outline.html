Intercessor Training — The Full Curriculum

Ezekiel 22:30 (KJV) — "And I sought for a man among them, that should make up the hedge, and stand in the gap before me for the land, that I should not destroy it: but I found none."

Purpose

To raise, equip, and mature a company of intercessors who stand in the gap for God's people, partner in prayer with the vision of L137 Deliverance Ministry International and Dominion Hour, and remain grounded in the Word of God (KJV), the finished work of Jesus Christ, and the ministry of the Holy Spirit.

Who this training is for

  • Members of L137 Deliverance Ministry International who feel called to intercession
  • Members of Dominion Hour who serve in or feel drawn to the prayer altar
  • Family prayer leaders, WhatsApp group coordinators, and small-group facilitators
  • Any born-again believer under Pastor Kiky Ocansey's or Prophet Prince Amo David's covering

Module-by-module summary

Module 1 — The Call of the Intercessor

Main scripture: Ezekiel 22:30 · Supporting: Isaiah 59:16; Genesis 18:22–33; 1 Timothy 2:1.

Objectives: Define biblical intercession · Recognise the divine invitation to stand in the gap · Distinguish intercession from personal prayer · Answer the call personally.

Declaration theme: "Here am I, Lord — send me."

Assignment: Write your personal "Here am I" letter to the Lord.

Module 2 — Salvation, Repentance, and the Clean Vessel

Main scripture: 2 Timothy 2:20–21 · Supporting: Psalm 66:18; 1 John 1:9; Isaiah 6:5–8.

Objectives: Confirm salvation through Jesus Christ · Understand why unconfessed sin blocks intercession · Walk in daily repentance and forgiveness.

Declaration theme: "I am a vessel unto honour, sanctified, meet for the Master's use."

Assignment: 7-day repentance and forgiveness journal.

Module 3 — The Blood of Jesus and the Believer's Authority

Main scripture: Revelation 12:11 · Supporting: Hebrews 9:14; Colossians 1:13–14; Luke 10:19.

Objectives: Understand the finished work of the Cross · Plead the blood of Jesus over self, family, ministry · Exercise the authority given in the name of Jesus.

Declaration theme: "I overcome by the blood of the Lamb and the word of my testimony."

Assignment: Daily blood-of-Jesus covering prayer for 14 days.

Module 4 — The Word of God as the Intercessor's Sword

Main scripture: Hebrews 4:12 · Supporting: Ephesians 6:17; Jeremiah 1:12; Isaiah 55:11.

Objectives: Pray the Word, not fear · Build a personal scripture arsenal · Speak KJV scripture as a weapon of warfare.

Declaration theme: "The Word of God is quick, powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword."

Assignment: Build a 30-verse personal warfare bank.

Module 5 — Praying in the Holy Ghost

Main scripture: Jude 1:20 · Supporting: Romans 8:26–27; 1 Corinthians 14:14–15; Acts 2:4.

Objectives: Understand the Holy Spirit as Helper in prayer · Practise praying with the understanding and with the spirit · Yield to prophetic utterance and travail.

Declaration theme: "I build myself up on my most holy faith, praying in the Holy Ghost."

Assignment: 15-minute daily Holy Ghost prayer window for 21 days.

Module 6 — The Prayer Altar: Building and Keeping It

Main scripture: Genesis 8:20 · Supporting: Exodus 27:1–8; Malachi 1:7; Genesis 26:18.

Objectives: Build a personal prayer altar at home · Keep the altar clean, fired, and undefiled · Re-dig broken altars in family lines.

Declaration theme: "The fire on my altar shall never go out."

Assignment: Set up the physical personal prayer altar (5 AM devotional slot recommended).

Module 7 — Fasting for Breakthrough

Main scripture: Isaiah 58:6 · Supporting: Matthew 6:16–18; Matthew 17:21; Ezra 8:21–23.

Objectives: Understand biblical purposes of fasting · Choose a fast pattern · Combine fasting with prayer, Word, and worship.

Declaration theme: "This kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting."

Assignment: Complete a 3-day guided fast.

Module 8 — Spiritual Warfare and the Whole Armour

Main scripture: Ephesians 6:10–18 · Supporting: 2 Corinthians 10:3–5; Luke 10:19; Isaiah 54:17.

Objectives: Identify the enemy's tactics · Put on every piece of armour daily · War from victory, not toward victory.

Declaration theme: "No weapon that is formed against me shall prosper."

Assignment: Daily armour-up prayer for 30 days.

Module 9 — Prophetic Intercession and Divine Acceleration

Main scripture: Habakkuk 2:2–3 · Supporting: 2 Kings 3:16–20; Ezekiel 37:1–10; 1 Kings 18:41–46.

Objectives: Receive and record prophetic direction · Dig ditches by faith before the rain · Prophesy to dry bones and closed situations.

Declaration theme: "The vision is for an appointed time — it will surely come."

Assignment: Begin a prophetic intercession journal.

Module 10 — Interceding for Family, Home, and Generations

Main scripture: Job 1:5 · Supporting: Acts 16:31; Joshua 24:15; Isaiah 49:24–25.

Objectives: Cover spouse, children, extended family in daily prayer · Break generational patterns through the blood of Jesus · Establish family prayer rhythms.

Declaration theme: "As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord."

Assignment: Complete a family covering prayer list (names + specific needs).

Module 11 — Interceding for Pastors, Ministry, and Nations

Main scripture: 1 Timothy 2:1–4 · Supporting: Ephesians 6:19–20; Psalm 122:6; 2 Chronicles 7:14.

Objectives: Cover Pastor Kiky Ocansey and Prophet Prince Amo David daily · Intercede for L137 and Dominion Hour ministry assignments · Stand in the gap for cities and nations.

Declaration theme: "I will not hold my peace, until the salvation thereof go forth as a lamp that burneth."

Assignment: Daily 10-minute pastoral and national covering slot.

Module 12 — The Intercessor's Life: Character, Aftercare, Longevity

Main scripture: Luke 18:1 · Supporting: Galatians 6:9; 1 Corinthians 9:27; Isaiah 40:31.

Objectives: Guard the heart · Practise aftercare · Stay hidden in Christ · Finish well.

Declaration theme: "Men ought always to pray, and not to faint."

Assignment: Write your personal Rule of Life for intercession.

Deliverables in the full pack

  1. Interactive course dashboard (this site)
  2. Workbook (full teaching + KJV in full + reflection + journaling)
  3. Prayer Point Manual
  4. Scripture Companion (KJV, in full)
  5. Declaration Cards
  6. Weekly Assignment sheets (one per module)
  7. Memory Verse cards
  8. Family Prayer Guide
  9. Leader's Guide (for prayer coordinators)
  10. Certificate of Commissioning

Recommended pace

  • 12-week track — one module per week (recommended for new intercessors)
  • 6-week intensive — two modules per week (for existing intercessors upgrading)
  • Weekend retreat — four modules per day over three days (in-person)

Commissioning

Completion is confirmed by all 12 modules studied, all 12 assignments completed, personal Rule of Life submitted, and pastoral confirmation from L137 or Dominion Hour leadership. Upon confirmation, the intercessor receives a digital Certificate of Commissioning and is officially added to the L137 / Dominion Hour Intercessors register.
