Module 02 · L137 Emphasis · Deliverance Groundwork

Salvation, Repentance, and the Clean Vessel

2 Timothy 2:20–21 (KJV) — "But in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also of wood and of earth; and some to honour, and some to dishonour. If a man therefore purge himself from these, he shall be a vessel unto honour, sanctified, and meet for the master's use, and prepared unto every good work."

Module objectives

By the end of Module 2, you will be able to:

  1. Confirm salvation through Jesus Christ as the ground of all intercession.
  2. Understand why unconfessed sin blocks the intercessor.
  3. Walk in daily repentance and unforced forgiveness.
  4. Practise a personal cleansing prayer before every prayer altar.
  5. Recognise the intercessor as a vessel unto honour, sanctified, and meet for the Master's use.

Opening — the ministry standard

Before we teach, we obey the flow that Pastor Kiky Ocansey and Prophet Prince Amo David keep in every service:

  1. Praise and worship
  2. Thanksgiving
  3. Confession of sins
  4. Forgive others
  5. Invite the Holy Spirit
  6. Teaching
  7. Prayers and declarations
  8. Sealing with the blood of Jesus

Do this every time you open this module, whether alone or in a group. Do not skip. Do not reorder.

Teaching

1. You cannot intercede for a house you have not entered

Intercession is a family ministry. You cannot stand in the gap for a family you do not belong to. Before the intercessor stands for others, the intercessor must first stand in Christ.

John 3:3 (KJV)

Jesus answered and said unto him, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God.

The new birth is not a decoration on a religious life. It is the door. If you have never given your life to Jesus Christ, do it today, out loud, in your own voice. Confess Him as Lord. Believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead. You are then born into the family, adopted by the Father, sealed by the Holy Spirit, and legally qualified to intercede.

2. Unconfessed sin blocks the intercessor

Psalm 66:18 (KJV)

If I regard iniquity in my heart, the Lord will not hear me.

Sin does not send God away, but it does close the ear of God to the one who tolerates it. This is not condemnation — it is a mercy. God will not partner with a heart that is hiding rebellion, because to answer such a prayer would confirm the rebellion. The Father loves us too much to co-sign our destruction.

The intercessor's answer is not perfection. The intercessor's answer is quick, honest, thorough repentance.

1 John 1:9 (KJV)

If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.

3. The vessel unto honour

2 Timothy 2:20-21 (KJV)

But in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also of wood and of earth; and some to honour, and some to dishonour. If a man therefore purge himself from these, he shall be a vessel unto honour, sanctified, and meet for the master's use, and prepared unto every good work.

Paul says the difference between a vessel unto honour and a vessel unto dishonour is not the material — it is whether the vessel has been purged. The intercessor is not more talented than others. The intercessor is cleaner. The intercessor is willing to be emptied of every unclean thing so that the Master can use them.

  • Purge — a deliberate emptying of what does not belong
  • Sanctified — set apart, dedicated, no longer common
  • Meet for the master's use — fit for purpose, at hand, ready
  • Prepared unto every good work — carried, not driven, into assignments

4. Isaiah's altar of cleansing

Isaiah 6:5-8 (KJV)

Then said I, Woe is me! for I am undone; because I am a man of unclean lips, and I dwell in the midst of a people of unclean lips: for mine eyes have seen the King, the LORD of hosts. Then flew one of the seraphims unto me, having a live coal in his hand, which he had taken with the tongs from off the altar: And he laid it upon my mouth, and said, Lo, this hath touched thy lips; and thine iniquity is taken away, and thy sin purged. Also I heard the voice of the Lord, saying, Whom shall I send, and who will go for us? Then said I, Here am I; send me.

Isaiah's response to the glory of God was not excitement, it was terror. "Woe is me!" That is the honest response of any real intercessor who sees God clearly. The Lord did not scold Isaiah. He sent a seraph with a live coal from the altar. Only then did Isaiah hear "Whom shall I send?" and only then could he say, "Here am I; send me."

There is no true commissioning without the coal from the altar. The intercessor must let the fire of God touch their lips, their motives, their memories, and their assignments.

5. Forgiveness is not optional

Matthew 6:14 (KJV)

For if ye forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you.

Matthew 6:15 (KJV)

But if ye forgive not men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.

Jesus made forgiveness the hinge of prayer. An intercessor holding a grudge is a stopped-up well. Forgiveness is not agreement with the wrong; it is a legal release of the offender from your court. Give the case to God the Judge, then walk free to intercede for anyone He assigns.

Prayer points

Pray each point aloud. Take at least three minutes on each.

  1. Father, I thank You for the blood of Jesus that speaks better things for me than the blood of Abel, in the name of Jesus. (Hebrews 12:24)
  2. Lord Jesus, I confess You today as my Lord and my Saviour, and I receive Your Spirit into my life afresh, in the name of Jesus. (Romans 10:9)
  3. Father, I repent of every hidden sin, every secret sin, every generational sin, in the name of Jesus. (Psalm 19:12)
  4. Father, I renounce every agreement I have made with darkness — knowingly and unknowingly — in the name of Jesus.
  5. Holy Spirit, purge me with hyssop and I shall be clean, in the name of Jesus. (Psalm 51:7)
  6. Father, I forgive every person who has hurt, betrayed, abused, or abandoned me, in the name of Jesus. (Matthew 6:14)
  7. Father, I release every offence into Your hands, and I refuse to carry it back, in the name of Jesus.
  8. Father, deliver me from bitterness, from the root of bitterness, and from the fruit of bitterness, in the name of Jesus. (Hebrews 12:15)
  9. Lord, purge my lips with the live coal from Your altar, in the name of Jesus. (Isaiah 6:6-7)
  10. Father, sanctify me by Your truth — Your Word is truth, in the name of Jesus. (John 17:17)
  11. Father, seal me, my house, and my prayer altar with the blood of Jesus, in the name of Jesus.
  12. Father, make me a vessel unto honour, prepared unto every good work, in the name of Jesus. (2 Timothy 2:21)

Sealing

I cover this prayer, my mind, my body, my family, and my assignment with the precious blood of Jesus Christ. Every arrow returned, every gate sealed, every ministry protected, in the name of Jesus. Amen.

Memory verse

2 Timothy 2:21 (KJV) — If a man therefore purge himself from these, he shall be a vessel unto honour, sanctified, and meet for the master's use, and prepared unto every good work.

Write it. Speak it. Pray it. Sleep with it. Wake with it.

Reflection questions

  1. Have you personally, in your own words, confessed Jesus as Lord and received Him as Saviour?
  2. Is there any known sin you have been tolerating, minimising, or hiding?
  3. Who is the one person you most struggle to forgive? What would it look like to release them today?
  4. What does "a vessel unto honour" look like in your practical daily life this week?
  5. What must be emptied out of your life before God can fill you afresh?

Weekly assignment — Seven-day repentance and forgiveness journal

For the next seven days, keep a simple prayer journal with three columns each day:

  • Confess — what sin the Holy Spirit convicted you of today
  • Forgive — who you released today, and what you released them from
  • Receive — the specific promise or scripture God gave you in return

End every entry with: "I am a vessel unto honour, sanctified, meet for the Master's use."

Closing prayer

Father, in the name of Jesus Christ, I thank You that the blood of Jesus is enough. Cleanse me tonight. Purge me tonight. Set me apart tonight. I release every offence. I renounce every hidden sin. I say with Isaiah, "Here am I; send me." Fill me with the Holy Ghost, and make me a vessel unto honour, prepared unto every good work. In Jesus' mighty name, Amen.
