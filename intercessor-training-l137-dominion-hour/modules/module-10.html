Interceding for Family, Home, and Generations

Job 1:5 (KJV) — "And it was so, when the days of their feasting were gone about, that Job sent and sanctified them, and rose up early in the morning, and offered burnt offerings according to the number of them all: for Job said, It may be that my sons have sinned, and cursed God in their hearts. Thus did Job continually."

Module objectives

By the end of Module 10, you will be able to:

  1. Cover spouse, children, and extended family in daily prayer.
  2. Break generational patterns through the blood of Jesus.
  3. Establish a family prayer rhythm at home.
  4. Contend for captive children (Isaiah 49:24–25).

Before we teach, we obey the flow that Pastor Kiky Ocansey and Prophet Prince Amo David keep in every service:

  1. Praise and worship
  2. Thanksgiving

Do this every time you open this module, whether alone or in a group. Do not skip. Do not reorder.

Teaching

1. The Job pattern

Job 1:5 (KJV)

And it was so, when the days of their feasting were gone about, that Job sent and sanctified them, and rose up early in the morning, and offered burnt offerings according to the number of them all: for Job said, It may be that my sons have sinned, and cursed God in their hearts. Thus did Job continually.

Job rose early. Job named each child. Job sacrificed for the possibility of hidden sin. He said, "It may be that my sons have sinned, and cursed God in their hearts." This is a father's intercession — proactive, personal, patient, and repeated. "Thus did Job continually."

2. The household salvation promise

Acts 16:31 (KJV)

And they said, Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved, and thy house.

God speaks in households. When one member is saved and truly stands, the promise of household salvation is opened. This does not override anyone's free will — but it gives the interceding member the legal ground to plead for every other member, by name.

3. As for me and my house

Joshua 24:15 (KJV)

And if it seem evil unto you to serve the LORD, choose you this day whom ye will serve; whether the gods which your fathers served that were on the other side of the flood, or the gods of the Amorites, in whose land ye dwell: but as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD.

Joshua did not consult his household. He declared. There is a place where the intercessor speaks for the house — not in tyranny, but in covenant. This declaration is a legal claim in the courts of heaven for every member of that house.

4. Contending for captive children

Isaiah 49:24-25 (KJV)

Shall the prey be taken from the mighty, or the lawful captive delivered? But thus saith the LORD, Even the captives of the mighty shall be taken away, and the prey of the terrible shall be delivered: for I will contend with him that contendeth with thee, and I will save thy children.

God Himself promises to contend with the one who contends with your children. When a child is bound, entangled, prodigal, or under demonic pressure, God is more committed to their rescue than the intercessor is. Pray from that promise — not from fear.

5. Breaking generational patterns

Exodus 20:5-6 (KJV)

Thou shalt not bow down thyself to them, nor serve them: for I the LORD thy God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate me; And shewing mercy unto thousands of them that love me, and keep my commandments.

The Bible is honest: iniquity travels down family lines. But mercy is more powerful than iniquity — mercy is shewn unto thousands. Through the blood of Jesus, the intercessor:

  • Renounces every ancestral covenant that dishonours God
  • Confesses the sins of the fathers (Nehemiah 9:2)
  • Applies the blood of Jesus to the family line
  • Cuts off every generational curse in the name of Jesus (Galatians 3:13)
  • Declares that Christ has redeemed us from the curse

Do not attempt to dig into occult ancestral history for information. God does not require exhaustive knowledge for deliverance. He requires honest repentance and the blood of Jesus applied by faith.

Prayer points

Pray each point aloud. Take at least three minutes on each.

  1. Father, I thank You that You are a covenant-keeping God unto a thousand generations, in the name of Jesus. (Deuteronomy 7:9)
  2. Lord, I stand before You today for every member of my household by name — [insert names], in the name of Jesus. (Job 1:5)
  3. Father, I plead the blood of Jesus over every member of my family, in the name of Jesus.
  4. Father, I confess the sins of my fathers and my father's fathers, known and unknown, in the name of Jesus. (Nehemiah 9:2)
  5. In the name of Jesus, I renounce every ungodly covenant, oath, or dedication in my family line. (Isaiah 28:18)
  6. In the name of Jesus, I break every generational curse — of poverty, sickness, addiction, broken marriages, and untimely death — over my bloodline. (Galatians 3:13)
  7. Lord, You have said even the captives of the mighty shall be taken away — contend today for every captive child in my family, in the name of Jesus. (Isaiah 49:25)
  8. Father, save my spouse — draw them by cords of love, in the name of Jesus. (Hosea 11:4)
  9. Father, save my children — every prodigal come home, in the name of Jesus. (Luke 15:20)
  10. Father, save my parents, my siblings, my in-laws, and my extended family, in the name of Jesus. (Acts 16:31)
  11. Father, deliver my house from every unclean object, every defiled media, and every accursed thing, in the name of Jesus. (Joshua 7:12-13)
  12. Father, establish a family prayer altar in my home, in the name of Jesus.

Sealing

I cover this prayer, my mind, my body, my family, and my assignment with the precious blood of Jesus Christ. Every arrow returned, every gate sealed, every ministry protected, in the name of Jesus. Amen.

Reflection questions

  1. Who is on your family prayer list, by name?
  2. Which generational pattern do you most need broken in Jesus' name?
  3. Do you rise early to intercede for your household (Job 1:5)?
  4. Is your house a house of prayer? If not, what needs to change today?
  5. Which captive child does God want you to contend for this month?

Weekly assignment — Complete a family covering prayer list

Create a family covering prayer list in your notebook. For each person:

  1. Full name
  2. Relationship
  3. Salvation status (saved / unsaved / prodigal / unknown)
  4. Specific need — salvation, healing, deliverance, direction, marriage, finances
  5. One Scripture promise for them
  6. One prayer point tailored to them

Pray through the whole list at least three times per week. Update the list every month. Do not share individual names publicly.

Closing prayer

Father, in the name of Jesus, thank You for my household. I stand for every name today. I plead the blood of Jesus over my spouse, my children, my parents, my siblings, and my extended family. I break every generational curse. I renounce every ungodly covenant. I contend for every captive child. As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord. In Jesus' mighty name, Amen.
