Module 07 · Both Ministries · Prayer & Fasting

Fasting for Breakthrough

Isaiah 58:6 (KJV) — "Is not this the fast that I have chosen? to loose the bands of wickedness, to undo the heavy burdens, and to let the oppressed go free, and that ye break every yoke?"

Module objectives

By the end of Module 7, you will be able to:

  1. Understand the biblical purposes of fasting.
  2. Choose a fast pattern suited to your body, work, and calling.
  3. Combine fasting with prayer, Word, and worship — not with weight loss.
  4. Break every yoke through the fast God has chosen.

Opening — the ministry standard

Before we teach, we obey the flow that Pastor Kiky Ocansey and Prophet Prince Amo David keep in every service:

  1. Praise and worship
  2. Thanksgiving
  3. Confession of sins
  4. Forgive others
  5. Invite the Holy Spirit
  6. Teaching
  7. Prayers and declarations
  8. Sealing with the blood of Jesus

Do this every time you open this module, whether alone or in a group. Do not skip. Do not reorder.

Teaching

1. The fast God has chosen

Isaiah 58:6 (KJV)

Is not this the fast that I have chosen? to loose the bands of wickedness, to undo the heavy burdens, and to let the oppressed go free, and that ye break every yoke?

God defines the fast He honours: it looses the bands of wickedness, undoes heavy burdens, sets the oppressed free, and breaks every yoke. Fasting that only shrinks the waistline while leaving the neighbour hungry, the marriage bitter, and the temper unbroken is not the fast God chose.

2. Fast unto the Lord, not unto men

Matthew 6:16-18 (KJV)

Moreover when ye fast, be not, as the hypocrites, of a sad countenance: for they disfigure their faces, that they may appear unto men to fast. Verily I say unto you, They have their reward. But thou, when thou fastest, anoint thine head, and wash thy face; That thou appear not unto men to fast, but unto thy Father which is in secret: and thy Father, which seeth in secret, shall reward thee openly.

Jesus assumes we will fast. He says "when ye fast," not "if." But He forbids theatre. Do not announce it. Do not perform it. Do it before the Father who sees in secret.

3. Some yokes only break with fasting

Matthew 17:21 (KJV)

Howbeit this kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting.

There are stubborn spirits in family lines, over marriages, in cities, and over gifts that will not yield to prayer alone. Fasting weakens the flesh, sharpens the spirit, quiets the noise, and forces every appetite to bow to the Spirit of God. Then and there, the yoke breaks.

4. A national example

Ezra 8:21-23 (KJV)

Then I proclaimed a fast there, at the river of Ahava, that we might afflict ourselves before our God, to seek of him a right way for us, and for our little ones, and for all our substance. For I was ashamed to require of the king a band of soldiers and horsemen to help us against the enemy in the way: because we had spoken unto the king, saying, The hand of our God is upon all them for good that seek him; but his power and his wrath is against all them that forsake him. So we fasted and besought our God for this: and he was intreated of us.

Ezra proclaimed a fast for a right way, for the little ones, and for their substance. When you fast, do not only ask for personal blessings; carry your family, your church, your nation.

5. Choosing a fast pattern

There is no single Christian fasting formula. Common biblical patterns include:

  • Sunrise-to-sunset — no food from dawn until evening, water permitted (Judges 20:26)
  • Daniel fast — no meat, no wine, no delicacies (Daniel 1:12; 10:3)
  • 3-day fast — no food (Esther 4:16). Water only unless health requires otherwise
  • Longer fasts (7, 21, 40 days) — undertaken only with pastoral guidance, medical review, and the leading of the Spirit
  • Corporate fast — the whole ministry fasting together for a common purpose (Joel 2:15)

If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, diabetic, on essential medication, elderly, or seriously ill, speak to your pastor and to a Christian medical professional before beginning any food fast. Consider a media, sugar, or entertainment fast instead.

6. What the fast should not be

  • Not a weight-loss programme
  • Not a bargain with God — He is not for hire
  • Not a competition — do not compare your fast to another's
  • Not a badge — never post it, never brag about it
  • Not an excuse to be rude — keep your heart tender

Prayer points

Pray each point aloud. Take at least three minutes on each. Prayer points to be prayed during and after each fast.

  1. Father, I thank You for the grace to fast, in the name of Jesus.
  2. Lord, teach me to fast the fast that You have chosen, in the name of Jesus. (Isaiah 58:6)
  3. Father, loose every band of wickedness over my life through this fast, in the name of Jesus.
  4. Father, undo every heavy burden You have placed on my heart, in the name of Jesus.
  5. Father, set every oppressed member of my family free through this fast, in the name of Jesus.
  6. Father, break every yoke of poverty, sickness, delay, and confusion over my life, in the name of Jesus.
  7. Lord, cleanse my body, purify my mind, and sharpen my spirit through this fast, in the name of Jesus.
  8. Father, deliver me from every stubborn spirit that only comes out by prayer and fasting, in the name of Jesus. (Matthew 17:21)
  9. Father, hear the cry of L137 and Dominion Hour intercessors as we fast together, in the name of Jesus. (Ezra 8:23)
  10. Father, keep my mouth from complaining, my heart from boasting, and my face from being disfigured, in the name of Jesus. (Matthew 6:16)
  11. Lord, let this fast produce lasting fruit — not just a feeling, in the name of Jesus. (Isaiah 58:8)
  12. Father, close every gate of the enemy that this fast has forced open, and cover the ground with the blood of Jesus, in the name of Jesus.

Sealing

I cover this prayer, my mind, my body, my family, and my assignment with the precious blood of Jesus Christ. Every arrow returned, every gate sealed, every ministry protected, in the name of Jesus. Amen.

Reflection questions

  1. Do you approach fasting as a discipline, as a bargain, or as a weapon?
  2. Which fast pattern fits your body and your calling this season?
  3. Which yoke has resisted prayer alone in your life, and needs a fast?
  4. Who else is God asking you to fast for?
  5. Do you have pastoral and, where needed, medical clearance before any extended fast?

Weekly assignment — Complete a 3-day guided fast

This week, undertake a 3-day fast — pattern as pastorally appropriate for your health. Suggested framework:

  • Day 1 — Repentance and cleansing. Use Module 2 prayer points.
  • Day 2 — Warfare and breakthrough. Focus on the one yoke you most need broken.
  • Day 3 — Intercession for others. Cover pastors, family, ministries, and nations.

Break the fast quietly with a small meal. Do not celebrate the fast; celebrate the Lord of the fast. Log the outcomes in your notebook, and share only what your leaders need to hear.

Closing prayer

Father, in the name of Jesus, I choose the fast that You have chosen. Loose every band of wickedness. Undo every heavy burden. Set every oppressed one free. Break every yoke. Keep my mouth clean, my heart tender, and my body submitted. Let this fast bear lasting fruit for the kingdom, in Jesus' mighty name, Amen.
