Praying in the Holy Ghost

Jude 1:20 (KJV) — "But ye, beloved, building up yourselves on your most holy faith, praying in the Holy Ghost."

Module objectives

By the end of Module 5, you will be able to:

  1. Understand the Holy Spirit as the intercessor's Helper.
  2. Distinguish praying with the understanding from praying with the spirit.
  3. Yield to prophetic utterance and travail as the Spirit leads.
  4. Practise a daily rhythm of praying in the Holy Ghost.

Opening — the ministry standard

Before we teach, we obey the flow that Pastor Kiky Ocansey and Prophet Prince Amo David keep in every service:

  1. Praise and worship
  2. Thanksgiving
  3. Confession of sins
  4. Forgive others
  5. Invite the Holy Spirit
  6. Teaching
  7. Prayers and declarations
  8. Sealing with the blood of Jesus

Do this every time you open this module, whether alone or in a group. Do not skip. Do not reorder.

Teaching

1. Building yourself up

Jude 1:20 (KJV)

But ye, beloved, building up yourselves on your most holy faith, praying in the Holy Ghost.

The intercessor cannot pour out what has not been built up. Jude commands the believer to build himself up on his most holy faith by praying in the Holy Ghost. This is not a technique — it is an atmosphere. Praying in the Holy Ghost is prayer that is initiated, sustained, and energised by the Spirit of God.

2. The Spirit helps our infirmities

Romans 8:26-27 (KJV)

Likewise the Spirit also helpeth our infirmities: for we know not what we should pray for as we ought: but the Spirit itself maketh intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered. And he that searcheth the hearts knoweth what is the mind of the Spirit, because he maketh intercession for the saints according to the will of God.

Every intercessor at some point runs out of words. The mind reaches its limit. The Spirit does not. The Holy Ghost makes intercession according to the will of God, and does so through us. This is a great mercy: you do not have to know exactly what to pray. You have to yield.

3. With the spirit and with the understanding

1 Corinthians 14:14-15 (KJV)

For if I pray in an unknown tongue, my spirit prayeth, but my understanding is unfruitful. What is it then? I will pray with the spirit, and I will pray with the understanding also: I will sing with the spirit, and I will sing with the understanding also.

Paul teaches both. "I will pray with the spirit, and I will pray with the understanding also." Neither replaces the other. The intercessor prays in tongues to build up faith and to pray beyond what the mind grasps, and prays in known language to declare truth, to instruct the spirit, and to teach others.

4. Filled and refilled

Acts 2:4 (KJV)

And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance.

Ephesians 5:18 (KJV)

And be not drunk with wine, wherein is excess; but be filled with the Spirit.

The Greek in Ephesians 5:18 is present continuous: "be being filled" with the Spirit. The Baptism of the Holy Spirit is a doorway; being filled is a way of life. Every intercessor lives a lifestyle of leaking and refilling.

5. Travail — when words fail

Galatians 4:19 (KJV)

My little children, of whom I travail in birth again until Christ be formed in you.

There are seasons when intercession moves beyond words into groans, tears, and prophetic pregnancy. Do not despise it. Do not perform it. When the Spirit leads you there, yield with reverence, then return to the Word to nurse the promise.

Teaching insert — DIPN 7-Key Framework for Effective Intercession

Reverend Dinna Osayi, founder of Deborah Intercessory Prayer Network (DIPN), teaches a seven-key framework that fits neatly under Praying in the Holy Ghost. We reference it here because it summarises the New Testament pattern in seven verses. Study each key, speak each verse, and let the Holy Spirit weave them into your daily altar.

  1. Pray God's will — Matthew 6:10, "Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven."
  2. Pray in faith — Mark 11:24, "What things soever ye desire, when ye pray, believe that ye receive them, and ye shall have them."
  3. Pray persistently — Luke 18:1, "Men ought always to pray, and not to faint."
  4. Pray in agreement — Matthew 18:19, "If two of you shall agree on earth as touching any thing that they shall ask, it shall be done for them of my Father which is in heaven."
  5. Pray with thanksgiving — Philippians 4:6, "Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God."
  6. Pray in the Spirit — Ephesians 6:18, "Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit."
  7. Intercede for all people — 1 Timothy 2:1, "I exhort therefore, that, first of all, supplications, prayers, intercessions, and giving of thanks, be made for all men."

Prayer points

Pray each point aloud. Take at least three minutes on each. If you have received the Baptism of the Holy Spirit and speak in other tongues, spend at least 10 minutes praying in tongues before continuing.

  1. Father, I thank You for the promise of the Holy Ghost, poured out on all flesh, in the name of Jesus. (Acts 2:17)
  2. Lord Jesus, baptise me afresh with the Holy Ghost and with fire, in the name of Jesus. (Matthew 3:11)
  3. Holy Spirit, teach me to pray as I ought, in the name of Jesus. (Romans 8:26)
  4. Father, fill my mouth with utterance from heaven, in the name of Jesus. (Acts 2:4)
  5. Lord, deliver me from dry, dead, formal prayer, in the name of Jesus.
  6. Holy Spirit, I yield my mind, my tongue, and my will to You now, in the name of Jesus.
  7. Father, let rivers of living water flow out of my belly, in the name of Jesus. (John 7:38)
  8. Lord, quicken my mortal body by Your Spirit, in the name of Jesus. (Romans 8:11)
  9. Holy Spirit, birth every prayer that must be prayed through me this season, in the name of Jesus. (Galatians 4:19)
  10. Father, help me pray in agreement, in unity with other intercessors, in the name of Jesus. (Matthew 18:19)
  11. Father, teach me to intercede for all people, for kings, and for all in authority, in the name of Jesus. (1 Timothy 2:1-2)
  12. Father, keep me being filled — refilled daily — with the Holy Ghost, in the name of Jesus. (Ephesians 5:18)

Sealing

I cover this prayer, my mind, my body, my family, and my assignment with the precious blood of Jesus Christ. Every arrow returned, every gate sealed, every ministry protected, in the name of Jesus. Amen.

Memory verse

Reflection questions

  1. Have you personally received the Baptism of the Holy Spirit? If not, will you ask today?
  2. What blocks you from yielding freely to the Holy Ghost in prayer?
  3. How much of your prayer time is with the understanding, and how much is with the spirit?
  4. Where in the DIPN 7-key framework are you strongest? Where are you weakest?
  5. How will you keep yourself being filled this week?

Weekly assignment — 21 days of Holy Ghost prayer window

For the next 21 days, set aside a 15-minute Holy Ghost prayer window at the same time each day (recommended: 5 AM or immediately after your morning devotional). During this window:

  1. Begin by thanking the Father for the gift of the Holy Ghost.
  2. Pray in tongues for as long as the Spirit leads.
  3. Then pray with the understanding — a specific matter the Spirit brings to your mind.
  4. Close by declaring one verse from Module 4's warfare bank.

Keep a short log: what did the Holy Spirit bring to prayer that you would not have brought on your own?

Closing prayer

Father, in the name of Jesus, thank You for the promise of the Holy Ghost. Fill me now. Fill my mouth. Fill my spirit. Fill my prayer altar. Teach me to pray in the Holy Ghost daily. Build me up on my most holy faith. Let rivers of living water flow out of my belly, in Jesus' mighty name, Amen.
