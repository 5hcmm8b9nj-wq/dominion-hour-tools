Module 06 · L137 Emphasis · Altars & Foundations

The Prayer Altar — Building and Keeping It

Genesis 8:20 (KJV) — "And Noah builded an altar unto the LORD; and took of every clean beast, and of every clean fowl, and offered burnt offerings on the altar."

Module objectives

By the end of Module 6, you will be able to:

  1. Build a personal prayer altar at home.
  2. Keep the altar clean, fired, and undefiled.
  3. Re-dig broken altars in family lines.
  4. Understand the difference between a Christian prayer altar and pagan altars.

Opening — the ministry standard

Before we teach, we obey the flow that Pastor Kiky Ocansey and Prophet Prince Amo David keep in every service:

  1. Praise and worship
  2. Thanksgiving
  3. Confession of sins
  4. Forgive others
  5. Invite the Holy Spirit
  6. Teaching
  7. Prayers and declarations
  8. Sealing with the blood of Jesus

Do this every time you open this module, whether alone or in a group. Do not skip. Do not reorder.

Teaching

1. Every generation of God's people built altars

Genesis 8:20 (KJV)

And Noah builded an altar unto the LORD; and took of every clean beast, and of every clean fowl, and offered burnt offerings on the altar.

Genesis 12:7 (KJV)

And the LORD appeared unto Abram, and said, Unto thy seed will I give this land: and there builded he an altar unto the LORD, who appeared unto him.

Noah, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob — every patriarch who walked with God built altars. An altar is not the place where God begins; it is the place where a person answers Him with a lifestyle of surrender.

The Christian's altar is not a shrine and not a ritual. It is a set-apart place, a set-apart time, and a set-apart heart. God meets us anywhere; God stays where we build.

2. The pattern of the brazen altar

Exodus 27:1-8 (KJV)

And thou shalt make an altar of shittim wood, five cubits long, and five cubits broad; the altar shall be foursquare: and the height thereof shall be three cubits. And thou shalt make the horns of it upon the four corners thereof: his horns shall be of the same: and thou shalt overlay it with brass. And thou shalt make his pans to receive his ashes, and his shovels, and his basons, and his fleshhooks, and his firepans: all the vessels thereof thou shalt make of brass. And thou shalt make for it a grate of network of brass; and upon the net shalt thou make four brasen rings in the four corners thereof. And thou shalt put it under the compass of the altar beneath, that the net may be even to the midst of the altar. And thou shalt make staves for the altar, staves of shittim wood, and overlay them with brass. And the staves shall be put into the rings, and the staves shall be upon the two sides of the altar, to bear it. Hollow with boards shalt thou make it: as it was shewed thee in the mount, so shall they make it.

God gave Moses a very detailed pattern. Every intercessor learns the same principle: God cares about how the altar is built. Ours is now a spiritual altar (Romans 12:1), but the pattern still teaches us:

  • It is foursquare — orderly, balanced, honest
  • It has horns — points of authority and grip
  • It is portable but heavy — you carry it, but it must have weight
  • It has fire that never goes out

3. Fire on the altar must never go out

Leviticus 6:13 (KJV)

The fire shall ever be burning upon the altar; it shall never go out.

The priest's job was not to start new fire every day. The priest's job was to keep the fire burning. This is the intercessor's daily task. Add wood — Word, worship, obedience, testimony. Remove ashes — old offences, dead habits, cold traditions.

4. Polluted altars, and how to cleanse them

Malachi 1:7 (KJV)

Ye offer polluted bread upon mine altar; and ye say, Wherein have we polluted thee? In that ye say, The table of the LORD is contemptible.

It is possible to have a working altar that God calls polluted. This happens when we offer God our leftovers, or when we bring compromised material to the altar — bitterness, entertainment defilement, hidden sin, occult items in the home.

The cleansing is not with salt baths. It is with:

  • The blood of Jesus applied by faith
  • Repentance from what defiled the altar
  • Removing every unclean object from the physical space
  • Rededicating the space and time to the Lord
  • Praying through until the fire returns

5. Re-digging broken family altars

Genesis 26:18 (KJV)

And Isaac digged again the wells of water, which they had digged in the days of Abraham his father; for the Philistines had stopped them after the death of Abraham: and he called their names after the names by which his father had called them.

Isaac's inheritance included the altars of his father. Enemies had filled them with earth. Isaac re-dug them by the same names. Many intercessors are called to re-dig altars in their family line — the altar of a praying grandmother, the altar of a lost parent, the altar of a marriage that once carried God. Do not try to invent new altars while ignoring old ones the Lord has given you to restore.

Prayer points

Pray each point aloud. Take at least three minutes on each.

  1. Father, I thank You for calling me to be a house of prayer, in the name of Jesus. (Matthew 21:13)
  2. Lord, I repent of every polluted offering I have laid on my altar, in the name of Jesus. (Malachi 1:7)
  3. Lord, I renounce every ungodly altar my ancestors or I have built, knowingly or unknowingly, in the name of Jesus.
  4. In the name of Jesus, I dismantle every ungodly altar assigned to my life, my family, or my ministry. (Judges 6:25)
  5. Father, cleanse the space of my home with the blood of Jesus, from the roof to the foundation, in Jesus' name. (Exodus 12:13)
  6. Father, remove every unclean object from my house — reveal to me anything that must go, in Jesus' name.
  7. Lord, consecrate my personal prayer altar — the time, the place, the posture — in the name of Jesus.
  8. Father, kindle the fire on my altar, and let it burn continually, in the name of Jesus. (Leviticus 6:13)
  9. Lord, I re-dig every righteous altar of prayer my ancestors built for You, in the name of Jesus. (Genesis 26:18)
  10. Father, teach me the rhythm of morning and evening sacrifice, in the name of Jesus. (Exodus 29:38-39)
  11. Lord, connect my personal altar with the corporate altars of L137 and Dominion Hour, in the name of Jesus.
  12. Father, keep my altar fire burning even when I am weak, in the name of Jesus.

Sealing

I cover this prayer, my mind, my body, my family, and my assignment with the precious blood of Jesus Christ. Every arrow returned, every gate sealed, every ministry protected, in the name of Jesus. Amen.

Reflection questions

  1. Where in your home is your prayer altar? If you don't have one, where will you set it up?
  2. What time of day will you consecrate as your altar hour? (Recommended: 5 AM.)
  3. What is currently polluting your altar — an object, a habit, a relationship, a compromise?
  4. Whose family altar are you called to re-dig?
  5. Who will you invite to build a shared family altar with you?

Weekly assignment — Set up your personal prayer altar

This week, set up a personal prayer altar in your home. Practical steps:

  1. Choose a physical location — a corner, a chair, a small table. It does not need to be elaborate.
  2. Place only these items: your Bible (KJV), a notebook, a pen, optionally a communion element and anointing oil kept in a clean container.
  3. Remove any object in the room that dishonours God — occult books, defiled art, unclean media.
  4. Consecrate the space with a short prayer and by pleading the blood of Jesus.
  5. Commit to a fixed daily altar hour (recommended: 5 AM).
  6. Keep an altar log — a simple record of what you prayed, what God said, and what you saw happen.

Send a photo of your altar (facing away from private areas) to your intercessor coordinator only if pastoral leadership requests it.

Closing prayer

Father, in the name of Jesus, I thank You for the privilege of building an altar unto You. I consecrate the place, the time, and my heart. I plead the blood of Jesus over this altar. Kindle the fire and let it never go out. Re-dig every righteous altar of my father's house. Dismantle every ungodly altar. Let every generation after me find that You have been enthroned in this home. In Jesus' mighty name, Amen.
