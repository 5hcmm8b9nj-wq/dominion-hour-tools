Module 08 · L137 Emphasis · Deliverance Warfare

Spiritual Warfare and the Whole Armour

Ephesians 6:10–18 (KJV) — "Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might. Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil."

Module objectives

By the end of Module 8, you will be able to:

  1. Identify the enemy's real tactics (Ephesians 6:12).
  2. Put on the whole armour of God every day.
  3. War from victory, not toward victory.
  4. Bind and loose in the name of Jesus, biblically and soberly.

Opening — the ministry standard

Before we teach, we obey the flow that Pastor Kiky Ocansey and Prophet Prince Amo David keep in every service:

  1. Praise and worship
  2. Thanksgiving
  3. Confession of sins
  4. Forgive others
  5. Invite the Holy Spirit
  6. Teaching
  7. Prayers and declarations
  8. Sealing with the blood of Jesus

Do this every time you open this module, whether alone or in a group. Do not skip. Do not reorder.

Teaching

1. Know your real enemy

Ephesians 6:10-18 (KJV)

Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might. Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil. For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places. Wherefore take unto you the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand. Stand therefore, having your loins girt about with truth, and having on the breastplate of righteousness; And your feet shod with the preparation of the gospel of peace; Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked. And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God: Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit, and watching thereunto with all perseverance and supplication for all saints.

The Bible names the intercessor's real enemy plainly: principalities, powers, rulers of the darkness of this world, and spiritual wickedness in high places. Not your husband. Not your children. Not your co-worker. Not the government official. Behind the flesh-and-blood face is a spirit that must be exposed and disarmed.

2. Every piece is a person

  1. Loins girt with truth — no compromise, no double-mindedness
  2. Breastplate of righteousness — Christ our righteousness, over our heart
  3. Feet shod with the preparation of the gospel of peace — ready to bring good news
  4. Shield of faith — active, raised, quenching every fiery dart
  5. Helmet of salvation — protecting the mind, the identity, the future
  6. Sword of the Spirit — the Word of God, in the mouth, in season
  7. All prayer and supplication in the Spirit — the atmosphere of every piece

The armour is not a metaphor for confidence. It is Christ Himself. "Put ye on the Lord Jesus Christ" (Romans 13:14). Every morning, dress deliberately, out loud.

3. Weapons that are not carnal

2 Corinthians 10:3-5 (KJV)

For though we walk in the flesh, we do not war after the flesh: (For the weapons of our warfare are not carnal, but mighty through God to the pulling down of strong holds;) Casting down imaginations, and every high thing that exalteth itself against the knowledge of God, and bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ.

The intercessor's weapons are not shouting, cursing, insult, or performance. They are mighty through God to pull down strongholds. The main battleground is the mind — arguments, imaginations, high things that exalt themselves against the knowledge of God. Every thought must be brought captive to the obedience of Christ.

4. No weapon shall prosper

Isaiah 54:17 (KJV)

No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper; and every tongue that shall rise against thee in judgment thou shalt condemn. This is the heritage of the servants of the LORD, and their righteousness is of me, saith the LORD.

Weapons will still be formed against you — God did not promise the enemy would stop working. God promised the weapons would not prosper. Standing intercessors keep standing until the weapon is exposed, disarmed, and returned to the sender.

5. Bind and loose — soberly

Matthew 18:18 (KJV)

Verily I say unto you, Whatsoever ye shall bind on earth shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever ye shall loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven.

The authority to bind and loose is real, but it is not a game. Bind spirits by name where the Word gives them names — spirit of fear, spirit of infirmity, spirit of heaviness. Loose the ministry of angels, the peace of God, the joy of the Lord. Do not invent creative names for demons; the Bible gives us all we need.

Prayer points

Pray each point aloud. Take at least three minutes on each. Suitable for daily armour-up prayer.

  1. Father, I thank You for the whole armour of God, in the name of Jesus. (Ephesians 6:11)
  2. I gird my loins with truth today — no compromise, no double-mindedness, in the name of Jesus.
  3. I put on the breastplate of righteousness — Christ my righteousness, over my heart, in the name of Jesus.
  4. I shod my feet with the preparation of the gospel of peace, in the name of Jesus.
  5. I take up the shield of faith, and I quench every fiery dart of the wicked, in the name of Jesus.
  6. I put on the helmet of salvation, and I guard my mind with the mind of Christ, in the name of Jesus. (Philippians 2:5)
  7. I take the sword of the Spirit, which is the Word of God, and I put it in my mouth today, in the name of Jesus.
  8. I pray always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit, in the name of Jesus. (Ephesians 6:18)
  9. In the name of Jesus, I pull down every stronghold of the enemy in my life. (2 Corinthians 10:4)
  10. In the name of Jesus, I cast down every imagination that exalts itself against God.
  11. In the name of Jesus, I bring every thought captive to the obedience of Christ.
  12. In the name of Jesus, I bind every spirit of fear, infirmity, heaviness, and confusion assigned to my life today. (Matthew 18:18)
  13. In the name of Jesus, I loose the ministry of angels, the peace of God, and the joy of the Lord over my household. (Hebrews 1:14; Philippians 4:7; Nehemiah 8:10)
  14. Father, no weapon that is formed against me shall prosper, in the name of Jesus. (Isaiah 54:17)
  15. Father, I stand — and having done all, I stand — in the name of Jesus. (Ephesians 6:13)

Sealing

I cover this prayer, my mind, my body, my family, and my assignment with the precious blood of Jesus Christ. Every arrow returned, every gate sealed, every ministry protected, in the name of Jesus. Amen.

Reflection questions

  1. Which piece of armour do you consistently forget to put on?
  2. Where has the enemy targeted your mind lately? What Scripture answers it?
  3. Have you been fighting a person, when the real enemy was a spirit behind them?
  4. Do you war from victory (Christ has won) or toward victory (I hope I win)?
  5. Which stronghold in your life needs pulling down this month?

Weekly assignment — 30 days of armour-up prayer

For the next 30 days, first thing every morning, pray the armour-up prayer aloud. Say each piece by name, put it on with your hand as a symbol of faith:

  1. Loins girt with truth
  2. Breastplate of righteousness
  3. Feet shod with the gospel of peace
  4. Shield of faith
  5. Helmet of salvation
  6. Sword of the Spirit — pick up your Bible physically
  7. Prayer and supplication in the Spirit

Keep a short log of every fiery dart quenched during the day.

Closing prayer

Father, in the name of Jesus, I thank You for the victory won on the Cross. I put on the whole armour of God today. Strengthen me in the power of Your might. Silence every voice of the enemy. Sharpen my sword. Cover my mind. Anchor my feet. I stand — and having done all, I stand — in Jesus' mighty name, Amen.
