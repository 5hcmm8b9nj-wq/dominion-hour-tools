Module 11 · Both Ministries · Covering Assignment

Interceding for Pastors, Ministry, and Nations

1 Timothy 2:1–4 (KJV) — "I exhort therefore, that, first of all, supplications, prayers, intercessions, and giving of thanks, be made for all men; For kings, and for all that are in authority; that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and honesty. For this is good and acceptable in the sight of God our Saviour; Who will have all men to be saved, and to come unto the knowledge of the truth."

Module objectives

By the end of Module 11, you will be able to:

  1. Cover Pastor Kiky Ocansey and Prophet Prince Amo David in daily prayer.
  2. Intercede for L137 and Dominion Hour ministry assignments.
  3. Stand in the gap for cities, Ghana, USA, Israel, and the nations.
  4. Refuse to hold peace until salvation goes forth as a lamp that burneth.

Opening — the ministry standard

Before we teach, we obey the flow that Pastor Kiky Ocansey and Prophet Prince Amo David keep in every service:

  1. Praise and worship
  2. Thanksgiving
  3. Confession of sins
  4. Forgive others
  5. Invite the Holy Spirit
  6. Teaching
  7. Prayers and declarations
  8. Sealing with the blood of Jesus

Do this every time you open this module, whether alone or in a group. Do not skip. Do not reorder.

Teaching

1. First of all

1 Timothy 2:1-4 (KJV)

I exhort therefore, that, first of all, supplications, prayers, intercessions, and giving of thanks, be made for all men; For kings, and for all that are in authority; that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and honesty. For this is good and acceptable in the sight of God our Saviour; Who will have all men to be saved, and to come unto the knowledge of the truth.

Paul commands the church's frontline assignment: supplications, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgivings — for all men, for kings, and for all in authority. This is not politics. This is discipleship. The intercessor stands between the throne of God and the seats of earthly authority.

2. Cover your pastors

Ephesians 6:19-20 (KJV)

And for me, that utterance may be given unto me, that I may open my mouth boldly, to make known the mystery of the gospel, For which I am an ambassador in bonds: that therein I may speak boldly, as I ought to speak.

Paul asked believers to pray for him. Not because he was weak, but because he was on assignment. Every pastor in an anointed pulpit is a target. The intercessor's assignment is to cover them — their utterance, their family, their body, their finances, their prophetic accuracy, their integrity, their protection, and their joy.

Practical daily covering for L137 and Dominion Hour:

  • Pastor Kiky Ocansey — utterance, health, family, integrity, protection, L137 assignments
  • Prophet Prince Amo David — utterance, health, family, integrity, protection, Dominion Hour assignments
  • Their spouses and children
  • Their leadership teams and armour-bearers
  • Their intercessor teams (including you)
  • Every service — before, during, after
  • Every deliverance session — participants and ministers

3. For cities and nations

Psalm 122:6 (KJV)

Pray for the peace of Jerusalem: they shall prosper that love thee.

2 Chronicles 7:14 (KJV)

If my people, which are called by my name, shall humble themselves, and pray, and seek my face, and turn from their wicked ways; then will I hear from heaven, and will forgive their sin, and will heal their land.

The intercessor is a national and a global citizen. Pray for the peace of Jerusalem — always. Pray for your city — where you live, work, worship. Pray for the nations both ministries are sent to reach — Ghana, USA, the UK, and beyond. Pray for revival, repentance, righteousness, and healing of the land.

4. No holding peace, no rest

Isaiah 62:1 (KJV)

For Zion's sake will I not hold my peace, and for Jerusalem's sake I will not rest, until the righteousness thereof go forth as brightness, and the salvation thereof as a lamp that burneth.

The intercessor has a mouth that will not hold peace and a body that will not fully rest until the salvation of God goes forth. This is not restlessness — it is holy urgency. It is Zion's cry through your lips.

Prayer points

Pray each point aloud. Take at least three minutes on each.

  1. Father, I thank You for Pastor Kiky Ocansey and for L137 Deliverance Ministry International, in the name of Jesus.
  2. Father, cover Pastor Kiky, her family, her home, her health, her finances, and her assignments with the blood of Jesus, in the name of Jesus.
  3. Father, sharpen the utterance of Pastor Kiky, and let every word she speaks come to pass, in the name of Jesus. (Jeremiah 1:9)
  4. Father, protect Pastor Kiky from every arrow, every witchcraft, every conspiracy, and every accusation, in the name of Jesus. (Isaiah 54:17)
  5. Father, raise armour-bearers, intercessors, helpers, and financial partners around Pastor Kiky, in the name of Jesus.
  6. Father, I thank You for Prophet Prince Amo David and for Dominion Hour, in the name of Jesus.
  7. Father, cover Prophet Prince, his family, his home, his health, his finances, and his prophetic accuracy, in the name of Jesus.
  8. Father, sharpen the prophetic utterance of Prophet Prince, and let every word he speaks in Your name come to pass, in the name of Jesus. (1 Samuel 3:19)
  9. Father, protect Prophet Prince from every arrow, every accusation, every distraction, and every plot, in the name of Jesus.
  10. Father, raise armour-bearers, intercessors, helpers, and financial partners around Prophet Prince, in the name of Jesus.
  11. Father, let every service — L137 and Dominion Hour — be a service of deliverance, salvation, healing, and impartation, in the name of Jesus.
  12. Father, I pray for the president and every ruler of my nation — grant them wisdom, righteousness, and the fear of God, in the name of Jesus. (1 Timothy 2:2)
  13. Father, pour out Your Spirit upon Ghana, upon the United States, and upon every nation these ministries are sent to reach, in the name of Jesus. (Joel 2:28)
  14. Father, I pray for the peace of Jerusalem — Your covenant land, Your covenant people, in the name of Jesus. (Psalm 122:6)
  15. Father, heal my land — humble our hearts, forgive our sins, in the name of Jesus. (2 Chronicles 7:14)

Sealing

I cover this prayer, my mind, my body, my family, and my assignment with the precious blood of Jesus Christ. Every arrow returned, every gate sealed, every ministry protected, in the name of Jesus. Amen.

Reflection questions

  1. How consistently do you cover your pastors in daily prayer? What needs to change?
  2. Which nation is God putting on your heart to intercede for this season?
  3. Do you refuse to hold your peace — or have you gone quiet?
  4. What can you fast for concerning your city?
  5. Whom else is God asking you to cover — a leader, an armour-bearer, a missionary?

Weekly assignment — Daily 10-minute pastoral + national covering slot

Add a fixed 10-minute pastoral and national covering slot to your daily prayer rhythm. Suggested breakdown:

  1. 2 minutes — Pastor Kiky Ocansey and L137
  2. 2 minutes — Prophet Prince Amo David and Dominion Hour
  3. 2 minutes — your local government leaders
  4. 2 minutes — your nation (Ghana, USA, UK, wherever you live)
  5. 1 minute — the peace of Jerusalem
  6. 1 minute — nations under persecution or crisis

This slot can be during your commute, your walk, or your evening. Log the topics for one full month, and note the answers as they come.

Closing prayer

Father, in the name of Jesus, I thank You for Pastor Kiky Ocansey, for Prophet Prince Amo David, for L137, for Dominion Hour, for my city, my nation, and Your covenant people Israel. I stand in the gap for each one. I plead the blood of Jesus over every pastor, every servant, every service, every soul. I will not hold my peace and I will not rest, until the salvation of God goes forth as a lamp that burneth. In Jesus' mighty name, Amen.
