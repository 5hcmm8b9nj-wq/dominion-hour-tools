Module 03 · L137 + Dominion Hour · Foundation of Authority

The Blood of Jesus and the Believer's Authority

Revelation 12:11 (KJV) — "And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death."

Module objectives

By the end of Module 3, you will be able to:

  1. Understand the finished work of the Cross as the ground of every prayer.
  2. Plead the blood of Jesus over self, family, ministry, and assignments.
  3. Exercise authority in the name of Jesus without fear or presumption.
  4. Distinguish authority from noise — quiet authority, backed by heaven.

Opening — the ministry standard

Before we teach, we obey the flow that Pastor Kiky Ocansey and Prophet Prince Amo David keep in every service:

  1. Praise and worship
  2. Thanksgiving
  3. Confession of sins
  4. Forgive others
  5. Invite the Holy Spirit
  6. Teaching
  7. Prayers and declarations
  8. Sealing with the blood of Jesus

Do this every time you open this module, whether alone or in a group. Do not skip. Do not reorder.

Teaching

1. The blood speaks

Hebrews 12:24 (KJV)

And to Jesus the mediator of the new covenant, and to the blood of sprinkling, that speaketh better things than that of Abel.

Abel's blood cried from the ground for justice. Jesus' blood cries from the mercy seat for redemption. The blood is not passive — it speaks. When the intercessor pleads the blood of Jesus, he is not begging; he is presenting a legal claim in a court where the verdict has already been won.

2. Overcoming by the blood

Revelation 12:11 (KJV)

And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death.

Three weapons in one verse:

  1. The blood of the Lamb — the legal ground of victory
  2. The word of their testimony — their agreement, spoken aloud, with what the blood has already done
  3. They loved not their lives unto the death — surrender, willingness to pay the price of standing

Every serious intercessor learns to combine all three. The blood alone is enough — but the intercessor's mouth activates its testimony in his own case.

3. Purged conscience, restored access

Hebrews 9:14 (KJV)

How much more shall the blood of Christ, who through the eternal Spirit offered himself without spot to God, purge your conscience from dead works to serve the living God?

The blood does not only forgive sin. It purges the conscience — the internal witness that accuses you before God. When the enemy whispers, "You cannot pray, look at what you did last week," the intercessor answers, "The blood of Christ has purged my conscience from dead works. I stand."

4. The name that is above every name

Philippians 2:9-11 (KJV)

Wherefore God also hath highly exalted him, and given him a name which is above every name: That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of things in heaven, and things in earth, and things under the earth; And that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.

Luke 10:19 (KJV)

Behold, I give unto you power to tread on serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy: and nothing shall by any means hurt you.

The authority is delegated. It is not yours by nature; it is yours by name. When you say, "In the name of Jesus," you invoke the finished work, the risen Lord, and the seated Christ. Every knee must bow — because the name has been given, and the price has been paid.

5. The believer's redemption

Colossians 1:13-14 (KJV)

Who hath delivered us from the power of darkness, and hath translated us into the kingdom of his dear Son: In whom we have redemption through his blood, even the forgiveness of sins.

1 Peter 1:18-19 (KJV)

Forasmuch as ye know that ye were not redeemed with corruptible things, as silver and gold, from your vain conversation received by tradition from your fathers; But with the precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot.

You have already been translated out of the power of darkness. Intercession is not moving toward victory — it is enforcing a victory Christ has already won. The intercessor stands on ground already claimed.

Prayer points

Pray each point aloud. Take at least three minutes on each.

  1. Father, I thank You for the precious blood of Jesus Christ, shed for me on the Cross, in the name of Jesus. (1 Peter 1:19)
  2. Lord, I plead the blood of Jesus over my body — head, mind, eyes, ears, mouth, heart, and every organ, in the name of Jesus.
  3. Lord, I plead the blood of Jesus over my family — spouse, children, parents, siblings — one by one, in the name of Jesus.
  4. Lord, I plead the blood of Jesus over my home, my property, and every gate of my life, in the name of Jesus. (Exodus 12:13)
  5. Lord, I plead the blood of Jesus over Pastor Kiky Ocansey, his family, and L137 Deliverance Ministry International, in the name of Jesus.
  6. Lord, I plead the blood of Jesus over Prophet Prince Amo David, his family, and Dominion Hour, in the name of Jesus.
  7. Father, silence every voice of the accuser by the blood of the Lamb, in the name of Jesus. (Revelation 12:10)
  8. In the name of Jesus, I bind every spirit of fear, torment, and confusion assigned to my prayer life. (Matthew 18:18)
  9. In the name of Jesus, I loose the ministry of angels to protect me and my household. (Hebrews 1:14)
  10. In the name of Jesus, I cast down every imagination and every high thing that exalts itself against the knowledge of God. (2 Corinthians 10:5)
  11. Father, cover my prayer altar with the blood of Jesus, in the name of Jesus.
  12. Father, teach me quiet authority — no showmanship, no noise, only faith in the name and the blood, in the name of Jesus.

Sealing

I cover this prayer, my mind, my body, my family, and my assignment with the precious blood of Jesus Christ. Every arrow returned, every gate sealed, every ministry protected, in the name of Jesus. Amen.

Reflection questions

  1. Do you pray from victory, or toward victory? What would change if you truly believed the victory was already won?
  2. Over what specific area of your life do you need to plead the blood of Jesus this week?
  3. Where has the enemy accused you? How does the purged conscience answer him?
  4. What is the difference between authority and noise?
  5. Whose face is God asking you to cover with the blood daily?

Weekly assignment — Fourteen days of blood-of-Jesus covering prayer

For the next fourteen days, at the start of each morning devotional, pray a specific blood-of-Jesus covering prayer over:

  1. Yourself — body, soul, spirit
  2. Your spouse and children (or immediate family)
  3. Your home, workplace, and property
  4. Pastor Kiky Ocansey and L137 Deliverance Ministry International
  5. Prophet Prince Amo David and Dominion Hour
  6. Your city and nation

Keep a simple checklist. Notice which areas feel hardest to cover — that is likely where the enemy has been contesting. Bring those to your intercessor coordinator or leader in prayer.

Closing prayer

Father, I thank You for the precious blood of Jesus Christ. I plead the blood over my life, my family, my pastors, and my ministry assignment. Every accusation silenced, every legal ground cancelled, every gate sealed, in Jesus' name. I stand today in the authority of the name that is above every name. I am not moving toward victory — I am enforcing the victory of the Cross. In Jesus' mighty name, Amen.
