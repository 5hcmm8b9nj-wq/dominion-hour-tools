Module 1 — The Call

  1. Father, I thank You for choosing me before the foundation of the world, in the name of Jesus. (Ephesians 1:4)
  2. Father, I repent of every time I heard Your call and refused it, in the name of Jesus. (1 John 1:9)
  3. Father, I forgive every person who wounded me and caused me to withdraw from prayer, in the name of Jesus. (Matthew 6:14)
  4. Holy Spirit, I invite You now — teach me to intercede, in the name of Jesus. (Romans 8:26)
  5. Lord, open my ears to hear Your call clearly today, in the name of Jesus. (1 Samuel 3:9)
  6. Lord, purge my lips with the live coal from Your altar, in the name of Jesus. (Isaiah 6:6–7)
  7. Lord, I say with Isaiah: "Here am I, send me" — commission me now, in the name of Jesus. (Isaiah 6:8)
  8. Father, position me in the gap where the hedge is broken in my family, in the name of Jesus. (Ezekiel 22:30)
  9. Father, position me in the gap for L137 Deliverance Ministry International and Pastor Kiky Ocansey, in the name of Jesus.
  10. Father, position me in the gap for Dominion Hour and Prophet Prince Amo David, in the name of Jesus.
  11. Father, deliver me from every spirit of slumber and prayerlessness, in the name of Jesus. (Romans 11:8)
  12. Father, kindle the fire of intercession on the altar of my heart, and let it never go out, in the name of Jesus. (Leviticus 6:13)

Sealing

I cover this prayer, my mind, my body, my family, and my assignment with the precious blood of Jesus Christ. Every arrow returned, every gate sealed, every ministry protected, in the name of Jesus. Amen.
