Add-On · Three-Day Retreat

L137 / Dominion Hour Intercessors Camp

Isaiah 58:6 (KJV) — "Is not this the fast that I have chosen? to loose the bands of wickedness, to undo the heavy burdens, and to let the oppressed go free, and that ye break every yoke?"

Purpose

A three-day retreat that gathers commissioned intercessors of L137 Deliverance Ministry International and Dominion Hour for concentrated prayer, fasting, worship, and impartation. Modelled after biblical patterns of set-apart consecration and after Camp Deborah under Reverend Dinna Osayi (Deborah Intercessory Prayer Network), while remaining fully under the covering of Pastor Kiky Ocansey and Prophet Prince Amo David.

Who it is for

  • Intercessors who have completed all 12 modules of this training
  • Prayer coordinators and armour-bearers within L137 and Dominion Hour
  • Family prayer leaders whose covering pastors approve them for the retreat
  • Any born-again believer whose pastor recommends them for a season of deeper consecration

Format

  • Duration: 3 days — Friday evening arrival, Sunday evening close
  • Pattern: Fasting (pattern chosen with pastoral and medical review — dry fast only for those with pastoral clearance and medical fitness), worship, teaching, prayer sessions, corporate deliverance, communion
  • Location options: Physical camp, church premises, home hosted, or fully online (Zoom + WhatsApp)
  • Rhythms: Morning altar (5 AM), mid-morning teaching, mid-day prayer, afternoon rest and Bible reading, evening worship and deliverance, night watch (optional)

Three-day schedule

Day 1 — Consecration

Main scripture: Isaiah 6:5–8. Emphasis: Cleansing, repentance, forgiveness, altar rebuild.

  • 05:00 — Personal altar
  • 08:00 — Corporate praise and worship
  • 09:00 — Teaching: Modules 1–3 recap (The Call · The Clean Vessel · The Blood of Jesus)
  • 11:00 — Corporate prayer of cleansing and consecration
  • 13:00 — Silence and Bible reading (Isaiah 6, Psalm 51)
  • 15:00 — Prophetic ministry: coal from the altar
  • 18:00 — Communion
  • 19:00 — Corporate deliverance session
  • 21:00 — Sealing prayer and rest

Day 2 — Warfare

Main scripture: Ephesians 6:10–18. Emphasis: Whole armour, warfare prayer, breaking generational patterns.

  • 05:00 — Personal altar
  • 08:00 — Praise and worship, then armour-up prayer
  • 09:00 — Teaching: Modules 4, 6, 8 recap (Word · Altar · Warfare)
  • 11:00 — Warfare prayer session — binding, loosing, pulling down strongholds
  • 13:00 — Rest and Bible reading (Psalm 91, Ephesians 6)
  • 15:00 — Family and generational prayer
  • 18:00 — Corporate declaration session
  • 19:00 — Prophetic intercession night — Habakkuk 2, Ezekiel 37
  • 22:00 — Optional watch (Matthew 26:41)

Day 3 — Commissioning

Main scripture: Isaiah 62:1. Emphasis: Pastors, ministries, cities, nations, and lifelong commissioning.

  • 05:00 — Personal altar
  • 08:00 — Break of the fast with a simple meal, thanksgiving, testimony
  • 10:00 — Teaching: Modules 11 and 12 recap (Pastors, Ministry, Nations · The Intercessor's Life)
  • 12:00 — Pastoral covering session — praying over Pastor Kiky, Prophet Prince, both ministries
  • 14:00 — Prayer for cities and nations (Ghana, USA, Israel, wherever intercessors serve)
  • 16:00 — Presentation of personal Rule of Life; pastoral confirmation
  • 17:00 — Corporate commissioning: laying on of hands, prophetic release, sealing
  • 18:30 — Closing communion, worship, farewell blessing

Preparation checklist for every camper

  1. Pastoral approval to attend
  2. Medical review of any planned fast, especially for pregnancy, diabetes, chronic conditions, or essential medication
  3. KJV Bible (physical), notebook, pen
  4. Personal warfare bank (from Module 4)
  5. Personal family covering list (from Module 10)
  6. Personal Rule of Life draft (from Module 12) — brought for confirmation
  7. Modest, comfortable clothing suitable for kneeling, worship, and rest
  8. Water, electrolytes as pastorally advised
  9. Phone silenced or handed in for the duration of teaching and prayer sessions

What the camp is not

  • Not a competition or an endurance contest
  • Not a public event — attendance is by pastoral approval only
  • Not a platform for personal prophecies to strangers
  • Not a substitute for pastoral or medical care
  • Not for children — a separate family-focused day is planned for children and youth

Ministry standards on-site (non-negotiable)

  • KJV Bible only; every scripture read in full
  • Every prayer session follows the 8-step deliverance flow (praise, thanksgiving, confession, forgive, invite the Holy Spirit, teaching, prayers and declarations, sealing with the blood of Jesus)
  • All physical elements (oil, water, communion) framed only through the finished work of Christ
  • No salt baths, no ancestral veneration, no covenant-of-salt language
  • No new-age, no divination, no astrology, no random unnamed rituals
  • Everything under pastoral covering; personal prophetic words tested and confirmed
  • Every gathering closes with sealing by the blood of Jesus

After the camp — aftercare protocol

  1. Break the fast gently with a small, healthy meal
  2. Hydrate, rest, sleep well the following night
  3. Read the Word deliberately for the next 7 days
  4. Report to your intercessor coordinator with any burden you carried out of the camp
  5. Continue your Rule of Life daily rhythms
  6. Seek Christian counsel or medical support if you notice persistent heaviness, insomnia, or physical symptoms
  7. Rejoice — you have entered the standing company of L137 and Dominion Hour intercessors
Commissioning declaration (spoken over every camper on Day 3)

By the authority of the Lord Jesus Christ, and under the covering of Pastor Kiky Ocansey of L137 Deliverance Ministry International and Prophet Prince Amo David of Dominion Hour, we commission you as an intercessor of these ministries. You are called to stand in the gap. You are qualified by the blood of Jesus. You are filled with the Holy Ghost. You are covered, sealed, and sent. Go and stand — and having done all, stand. In Jesus' mighty name, Amen.
