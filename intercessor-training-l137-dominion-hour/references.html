Appendix · Reputable Christian Reference

Reference Ministries & Recommended Reading

You are part of a wider stream of praying men and women across the body of Christ. This page lists reputable Christian ministries and books that align with L137 and Dominion Hour intercession — for study, reference, and encouragement.

Ministry standard for this page

All references listed here are historic, reputable, Christ-centred ministries and authors. No New Age, no Reddit, no divination, no aunty-flo-style sources. Where a ministry teaches on any subject outside this page's focus, we do not endorse everything published — only the intercession content noted here. Always test every teaching by the KJV Bible.

Contemporary intercessor ministry — Africa

Deborah Intercessory Prayer Network (DIPN)

Founder: Reverend Dinna Ololade Osayi (wife of Apostle Arome Osayi, Remnant Christian Network).
Focus: Women-focused intercessor training and equipping — unbroken intercession, retreats, citywide women's prayer crusades.
Signature gatherings: Camp Deborah (annual 3-day dry fast), Lioness Conference, The Ingathering, DIPN prayer camps (Kenya and beyond).
Signature framework: Seven keys to effective intercession — praying God's will, praying in faith, persistent prayer, praying in agreement, praying with thanksgiving, praying in the Spirit, interceding for all people. See Module 5 for the full teaching insert.
Teaching channel: Women Aflame TV (YouTube, Telegram).

Remnant Christian Network (RCN)

Founder / Set Man: Apostle Arome Osayi.
Headquarters: Makurdi, Benue State, Nigeria.
Intercessor-focused streams: Rise of Intercessors (teaching series and gatherings), Teach Us to Pray Conference, The Intercessor series, The Skill of an Intercessor.
Teaching schools inside RCN: School of the Prophets, School of Wisdom, School of Faith, School of Priests, School of Ministry.

Contemporary intercessor ministry — Global (Christian)

International House of Prayer Kansas City (IHOPKC)

Founder: Mike Bickle. Established continuous worship-and-prayer model, night-and-day prayer, prophetic history teaching.

Generals International

Founders: Mike and Cindy Jacobs. Signature teaching on strategic-level intercession, prayer for nations, and the church's prayer mandate. Cindy Jacobs' Possessing the Gates of the Enemy is a standard training text for intercessors.

Dutch Sheets Ministries — Give Him 15

Founder: Dutch Sheets. Author of Intercessory Prayer, one of the most widely used intercession books in the global church. Daily Give Him 15 devotional focuses on 15-minute intercession windows.

Derek Prince Ministries

Founder: the late Derek Prince. Author of Shaping History Through Prayer and Fasting, a foundational teaching on how prayer and fasting move nations.

Aglow International

Founded: 1967. Global women's intercession network with the freely available Eight Keys to Intercession teaching PDF.

Intercessors for America / Intercessors for Nigeria / Intercessors for the Nations

Country-focused intercession fellowships that publish weekly prayer targets and training resources. Reputable and Bible-anchored.

James Goll — God Encounters Ministries

Focus: Prophetic intercession. His Powerful Prophetic Intercession course (12 lessons) is a well-known reference for prophetic prayer.

The Prayer Course (24-7 Prayer)

Founder: Pete Greig. A freely available 8-session teaching course walking through the Lord's Prayer as a template for a life of prayer.

Recommended reading — Core intercession books

  1. Dutch Sheets — Intercessory Prayer. The single most widely used modern intercession textbook. Foundations, authority, standing in the gap.
  2. Cindy Jacobs — Possessing the Gates of the Enemy. Practical training for the intercessor, including strategic-level and personal intercession.
  3. Derek Prince — Shaping History Through Prayer and Fasting. Foundational on how prayer and fasting move nations.
  4. Mike Bickle — Growing in Prayer. Sustained personal prayer life for a lifetime.
  5. Wesley Duewel — Mighty Prevailing Prayer. Classic on persevering intercession.
  6. E. M. Bounds — Power Through Prayer. Short, sharp, historic Christian classic on the prayer life of the minister.
  7. Andrew Murray — With Christ in the School of Prayer. 19th-century classic — Christ's own lessons on prayer.
  8. Leonard Ravenhill — Why Revival Tarries. Prophetic call to travailing intercession and revival.

A word from L137 and Dominion Hour

Every ministry listed here has its own doctrinal emphasis. We reference them because they honour the Lord Jesus Christ, the authority of Scripture, and the ministry of intercession. Where any teaching from these ministries appears to conflict with the KJV Bible or with the ministry standards of L137 or Dominion Hour, the KJV Bible and our covering leadership have the final word. Read widely, but drink deeply from the Word.
