Ministry standards

What every intercessor keeps

The standard flow

  1. Praise and worship
  2. Thanksgiving
  3. Confession of sins
  4. Forgive others
  5. Invite the Holy Spirit
  6. Teaching / Word
  7. Prayers and declarations
  8. Sealing with the blood of Jesus

Non-negotiables

  • KJV Bible only, every scripture in full
  • Christian sources only — no New Age, no Reddit
  • All authority through Jesus Christ and His finished work
  • All glory to God the Father alone
  • Physical elements (oil, water, communion) framed only through the finished work of the Cross
