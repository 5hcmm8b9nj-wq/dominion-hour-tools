About the Prophet

A vessel called to teach, deliver, and restore dominion.

Prophet Prince Amo David ministers from Accra, Ghana, with a global voice that reaches believers across the diaspora. His ministry, Dominion Hour, carries a mandate to teach the Word with prophetic clarity, bring believers into deliverance, and stir hearts back to the secret place of intercession.

Each service is rooted in Scripture (KJV), seasoned with revelation, and committed to producing fruit that remains — free from doctrines of men, mysticism, or the spirit of this age. Whether through Clubhouse, the upcoming online school, or one-on-one prayer, the heart of this ministry is the same: Jesus Christ, and Him crucified.

Teaching Pillars

The three streams of Dominion Hour.

Every service flows from one of three streams — and together they form the rhythm of this ministry.

i.

Prophetic Teaching

Scripture-first teaching that unpacks the prophetic from a sound Word foundation — never apart from the cross of Christ, never apart from the KJV witness of Scripture.

Word · Revelation
ii.

Deliverance

Practical, biblical deliverance — pulling down strongholds, breaking generational covenants, and walking believers into freedom through the blood and name of Jesus.

Freedom · Warfare
iii.

Intercession

Spirit-led prayer for families, nations, and personal breakthrough. Standing in the gap — early-morning intercession that has marked this ministry from the beginning.

Prayer · Watch
