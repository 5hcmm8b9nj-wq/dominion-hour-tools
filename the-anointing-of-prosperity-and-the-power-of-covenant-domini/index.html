What this teaching says in one paragraph

There is an anointing called the anointing of prosperity. When it settles on a believer, that life becomes like a tree planted by the rivers of water — fruitful in its season, its leaf never withering, and whatsoever it doeth prospers. Prosperity is not money alone: it is to thrive and flourish in spirit, in body, in marriage, in career and in ministry. The oil is poured on the head because the head carries destiny. And behind every lasting harvest stands covenant — the covenant that made Isaac reap a hundredfold in a famine, that preserved David's kingdom, and that saved Rahab's entire household. Ask for the oil, keep the covenant, guard the anointing, and go forward to possess.

    The eight-panel story

    One panel answers one question. Use your own supplied photographs for the believer — same hairstyle, clothing, skin tone, age and expression in every panel. God is never given a human face: only light, rays of glory and radiance.

    Closing of the story

    Prayer: Lord, anoint my head with fresh oil, and let my cup run over. Prosper the work of my hands and keep me in covenant with You all the days of my life.

    Declaration: By the reason of the anointing of prosperity upon my head, I am moving forward. I am advancing. I am prospering in all areas of my life.

    Memory verse: "But thou shalt remember the Lord thy God: for it is he that giveth thee power to get wealth, that he may establish his covenant which he sware unto thy fathers, as it is this day." (Deuteronomy 8:18)

    Reflection: What is the pot of oil already in my hand that God is asking me to bring out?
