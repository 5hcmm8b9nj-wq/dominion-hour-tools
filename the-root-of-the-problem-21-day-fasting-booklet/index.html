Welcome to the Fast

Some battles do not bow to ordinary effort. Stubborn spiritual obstacles, generational yokes, and hard situations demand an upgraded spiritual authority — the kind that comes only through prayer and fasting.

This booklet is designed to be easy to read. Big letters. Short paragraphs. One Scripture at a time. Whether you struggle to read long passages, whether you are new to the faith, or whether you simply want a companion for the fast — take one page at a time and let the Word of God do the work.

Read one day at a time. Pray each Scripture aloud. Speak the declaration. Write your reflection. Let heaven touch the root.

Before You Begin

What is fasting?

A tool to subdue the flesh, show humility, and align with God's purpose — not a way to force God or impress people.

Why fast with prayer?

Fasting without prayer is only hunger. A true fast joins hunger with active prayer, deep worship, and the Word of God.

What breaks the yoke?

The anointing breaks the yoke (Isaiah 10:27). Fasting sharpens our sensitivity to the Holy Spirit so His authority operates freely through us.

Guard your heart.

Do not fast to impress people. Fast to humble yourself before God, to seek His face, and to break every chain.

Today's Lesson

Jesus told His disciples plainly: certain kinds of bondage will not move by ordinary effort. They require the combined weight of prayer and fasting. Today we humble ourselves and say, Lord, teach us to fast the fast that breaks every kind.

Prayer

Father, I come before You in the name of Jesus. I humble myself before You today. Teach me to fast in a way that pleases You, and let this fast release the authority I need to see every stubborn thing bow.

Repeat After Me
This kind goes out — by prayer and fasting. In Jesus' name. Amen.
