The Word Spoken Over You

Why should you bother yourself about negative thoughts or what someone thinks about you?

Your sister Shelia has been coming to the room also to pray. Do you know the spiritual effects of prayer and the Word of God? Maybe if it wasn’t for the prayers, your situation would have been worse.

The rooms are Holy Ghost filled and the Word of God is being spoken every time.

The Bible says that David surrendered with a group of people, and he trained them to become mighty men.

God is not just delivering you from demonic spirits and attacks — He is building capacity in you, which is the Word and prayer, to deliver your family from bondage.

Key Lessons From This Word
1. Negative thoughts and opinions are not your assignment
The enemy uses the opinions of people and the noise of your own mind to pull you off the altar. You are not required to carry what someone thinks of you. Your assignment is to keep praying, keep standing on the Word.
2. Someone is praying you through
Sister Shelia coming into the room to pray is not small — it is covering. The intercession of others is one of the reasons your situation is not worse. Thank God for the ones He has stationed around you.
3. The room is Holy Ghost filled
Where the Word of God is spoken continually, the atmosphere shifts. You are not fighting in a dry place — you are being sustained in a Holy Ghost saturated environment.
4. David gathered a company and trained them
David did not fight Saul’s battles alone. He surrendered with a group, trained them, and they became mighty men. You are being gathered and trained the same way.
5. God is building capacity, not just delivering you
Your deliverance is not the end goal. God is depositing the Word and prayer inside you so that you can turn around and deliver your own family from bondage. You are being made a deliverer.
Prayer

Father, in the name of Jesus, I thank You for every prayer that has been prayed over my life. Thank You for my sister Shelia, for Prophet Prince, and for every intercessor You have stationed around me.

I release my mind from every negative thought and from the opinion of every person. I refuse to carry what You have not asked me to carry.

Fill this room, fill my ears, fill my mouth with Your Word. Build capacity in me by prayer and by the Word, that I would rise as a mighty one for the deliverance of my whole family. In Jesus’ name, amen.
