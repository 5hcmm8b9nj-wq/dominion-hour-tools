Practicum & Assessment Rubric

Same rubric structure for both schools. Differences noted where they exist.

How a student passes

To pass any course in either school, a student must meet all four bars:

  1. Attendance — 80% minimum.
  2. Weekly workbook completion — every module submitted, no exceptions.
  3. Practicum log — for each course, a set number of supervised or logged actions.
  4. Term review — one written test and one oral review per course.

Per-course practicum requirements

Course 1 — Foundations, Redemption & the (Holy / Prophetic) Spirit

  • 28-day prayer log
  • 28-day Word-study log
  • 12 worship entries
  • Attendance record: local church + 12 ministry services + 4 small-group meetings
  • Written recitation of the seven redemption realities with KJV verses
  • Memorized Luke 1:37 (L137) or Luke 10:19 (Dominion Hour) — oral

Course 2 — Ministry Leadership & Ethics

  • Publishing-Policy practicum: correctly clean a raw transcript with 5 deliberate violations (5/5 pass)
  • Aftercare log: shadow one aftercare follow-up
  • Safeguarding walk-through: identify when to refer to a qualified pastor or medical professional
  • Two short papers: one on manifestations & aftercare, one on workplace discernment

Course 3 — Warfare & Covenant Ministry

  • 10 supervised deliverance sessions (reference codes only, never names)
  • Written case notes for each session using the L137 8-step flow
  • One "strongman map" showing the hierarchy pattern the student encountered
  • One "altar map" showing an altar the Lord led the student to break

Course 4 — Soul Healing / Restoration & Personal Transformation

  • Personal bloodline map (private)
  • 21-day dream & vision journal
  • Personal Legacy Breakdown entries (private track)
  • One completed prophetic-declaration workbook
  • One completed 21-day Root of the Problem fast log

Course 5 — Stewardship, Family Altar & Ministry Income

  • Written personal budget
  • 30-day tithe log
  • 30-day family-altar log
  • Personal ministry-integrity statement (signed by the pastor)

Weekly workbook page — same in every module

Every workbook page uses the same 4-part structure:

  1. What Scripture plainly says — the KJV text (full, not snippets), and 2–3 sentences on what it clearly states.
  2. What the minister taught — a summary of Pastor Kiki's or Prophet Prince's teaching on the passage.
  3. Prayer response — a 3–5 minute personal prayer the student prays and logs.
  4. Declaration — the corporate declaration of the week, spoken aloud, dated.

Grading

GradeRequirement
Pass with distinction90–100% across all four bars
Pass80–89%
ContinueBelow 80% — student retakes the course before advancing

Confidentiality

  • All practicum records are private.
  • Reference codes replace names in every log.
  • The pastor (Pastor Kiki for L137, Prophet Prince for Dominion Hour) is the only reviewer of practicum work outside the student.
  • Practicum records are never used as testimony content without written consent.

Graduation & Impartation

  • L137 — graduation service at an L137 gathering, laying-on-of-hands by Pastor Kiki Ocansey. Certificate signed "L137 Deliverance Ministry International / Luke 1:37 KJV / For with God nothing shall be impossible."
  • Dominion Hour — graduation service at a Dominion Hour gathering, laying-on-of-hands by Prophet Prince Amo David. Certificate signed under Dominion Hour with Prophet Prince Amo David. Never under "Dominion Hour with Prophet Prince Amo David."
