Five courses. Same shape for both schools.

The school runs on five compulsory courses. Each of our schools maps its teachings into the same five slots.

Course 1

Foundations, Redemption & the Spirit

Ordinances of intimacy, redemption realities, authority of the believer.

Course 2

Ministry Leadership & Ethics

Pastoral care, aftercare, safeguarding, publishing policy.

Course 3

Practice & Warfare

Strongman, altars, hidden enemies, covenants, dominion authority.

Course 4

Soul Healing & Personal Transformation

Bloodline, mind renewal, dreams, restoration of destiny.

Course 5

Stewardship & Family Altar

Biblical wealth, family altar, ministry income with integrity.

Program tracks

Three admission tracks — pick the one that fits the season the student is in. All three end in an impartation service.

Track A

Foundations Intensive

12 weeks. All five courses, condensed. Weekly workbook, no practicum requirement, certificate of foundation.

Track B

Diploma in Formation & Warfare

24 weeks. Full five courses, full workbook, full practicum. Diploma with impartation.

Track C

Full School

52 weeks / 4 terms. Practicum with 10 supervised deliverance sessions, prophetic practicum, graduation and commissioning.

Non-negotiables in both schools

  • KJV Bible only, every scripture quoted in full — no snippets.
  • The L137 8-step deliverance flow taught to every student, never reordered.
  • No new age, no Reddit, no pop-astrology sources. Reputable Christian ministries only.
  • Publishing policy — names, family names, personal prophecies, and manifestations removed before any content goes public.
  • Confidentiality — practicum records use reference codes, never names.
  • The four ordinances of intimacy — prayer, Word study, worship, corporate gathering — required weekly.
