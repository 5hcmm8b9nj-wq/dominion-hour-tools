Module 2 — Redemption Realities & Authority of the Believer

Second module of Course 1 in both schools.

Course: Foundations, Redemption & the Holy Spirit (L137) / Foundations, Redemption & the Prophetic Spirit (Dominion Hour)

Length: 3 lessons

L137 anchor scripture: Luke 1:37 KJV — "For with God nothing shall be impossible."

Dominion Hour anchor scripture: Luke 10:19 KJV — "Behold, I give unto you power to tread on serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy: and nothing shall by any means hurt you."

Course outcome

By the end of this module, the student can:

1. State what Christ finished on the cross and what that means for the believer today. 2. Explain the difference between position (who you are in Christ) and practice (how you walk it out). 3. Speak the authority of the believer over sickness, demons, and impossible situations — from Scripture, not from feeling.

Lesson 1 — What Christ Finished

Anchor scripture — quoted in full KJV:

John 19:30 — "When Jesus therefore had received the vinegar, he said, It is finished: and he bowed his head, and gave up the ghost."
Colossians 2:13–15 — "And you, being dead in your sins and the uncircumcision of your flesh, hath he quickened together with him, having forgiven you all trespasses; blotting out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us, which was contrary to us, and took it out of the way, nailing it to his cross; and having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it."
Hebrews 9:12 — "Neither by the blood of goats and calves, but by his own blood he entered in once into the holy place, having obtained eternal redemption for us."

What Scripture plainly says

  • Jesus said "It is finished" — completed, not delayed (John 19:30).
  • On the cross He spoiled principalities and powers and made an open show of them (Colossians 2:15).
  • His blood obtained eternal redemption — one time, not repeatable (Hebrews 9:12).

What we teach — the seven redemption realities

Every deliverance minister must be able to recite these:

1. Forgiveness of sin — Ephesians 1:7 2. Reconciliation to the Father — 2 Corinthians 5:18–19 3. Adoption as sons — Galatians 4:4–7 4. New creation identity — 2 Corinthians 5:17 5. Righteousness as a gift — Romans 5:17 6. Healing — 1 Peter 2:24 7. Deliverance from the power of darkness — Colossians 1:13

Repeat-after-me declaration

"It is finished. The blood of Jesus has spoken. I am forgiven, reconciled, adopted, made new, made righteous, healed, and delivered. Principalities and powers are spoiled. In Jesus' name, Amen."

Weekly assignment

  • Write out each of the seven redemption realities in your own hand.
  • Under each, write the KJV verse in full.
  • Say each one aloud every morning for 7 days.

Lesson 2 — Who You Are Now (Identity in Christ)

Anchor scripture — quoted in full KJV:

2 Corinthians 5:17 — "Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new."
Ephesians 2:4–6 — "But God, who is rich in mercy, for his great love wherewith he loved us, even when we were dead in sins, hath quickened us together with Christ, (by grace ye are saved;) and hath raised us up together, and made us sit together in heavenly places in Christ Jesus."
1 Peter 2:9 — "But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people; that ye should shew forth the praises of him who called you out of darkness into his marvellous light."

What Scripture plainly says

  • The believer is a new creature — not improved, not repaired, new (2 Corinthians 5:17).
  • The believer is seated with Christ in heavenly places — right now, not one day (Ephesians 2:6).
  • The believer is a royal priesthood, out of darkness (1 Peter 2:9).

What we teach — position before practice

You do not fight for victory. You fight from victory. Deliverance ministry is not the believer trying to become something; it is the believer walking out what they already are.

Three phrases every student must know the difference between:

  • "I am trying to be free" — wrong. Christ finished the work.
  • "I am waiting to be free" — wrong. The blood has already been shed.
  • "I am walking out my freedom" — right. This is the whole assignment.

Repeat-after-me declaration

"I am in Christ. Old things are passed away. All things are become new. I am seated with Him in heavenly places. I am a royal priesthood. I walk out of darkness into His marvellous light. In Jesus' name, Amen."

Weekly assignment

  • Every morning for 7 days, look in the mirror and confess your new-creation identity aloud from 2 Corinthians 5:17.
  • Journal: where in your life have you been fighting for victory instead of from victory?

Lesson 3 — The Authority of the Believer

Anchor scripture — quoted in full KJV:

Luke 10:19 — "Behold, I give unto you power to tread on serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy: and nothing shall by any means hurt you."
Mark 16:17–18 — "And these signs shall follow them that believe; In my name shall they cast out devils; they shall speak with new tongues; They shall take up serpents; and if they drink any deadly thing, it shall not hurt them; they shall lay hands on the sick, and they shall recover."
Matthew 28:18–20 — "And Jesus came and spake unto them, saying, All power is given unto me in heaven and in earth. Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost: Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen."
Luke 1:37 — "For with God nothing shall be impossible."

What Scripture plainly says

  • The believer has power over all the power of the enemy — not some, not most, all (Luke 10:19).
  • The signs of the believer include casting out devils, speaking with new tongues, laying hands on the sick (Mark 16:17–18).
  • All authority in heaven and earth belongs to Jesus, and He commissioned it into the church (Matthew 28:18–20).
  • Nothing shall be impossible with God (Luke 1:37).

What we teach — the L137 8-step deliverance flow, applied

In every deliverance encounter, this is the order — never skipped, never reordered:

1. Praise and worship — establish the atmosphere; God inhabits praise. 2. Thanksgiving — remember what He has already done. 3. Confession of sins — 1 John 1:9. Clean the legal ground. 4. Forgive others — Mark 11:25. Close the courtroom. 5. Invite the Holy Spirit — He is the deliverer; we are the vessel. 6. Teaching — the Word does the work (Ephesians 5:26). 7. Deliverance prayers and declarations — spoken in Jesus' name, from authority. 8. Sealing — cover the person with the blood of Jesus.

Repeat-after-me declaration

"In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, I take my authority. I tread on serpents and scorpions. All the power of the enemy is under my feet. Nothing shall by any means hurt me. With God nothing shall be impossible. In Jesus' name, Amen."

Weekly assignment

  • Memorize Luke 10:19 KJV and Luke 1:37 KJV word-for-word.
  • Pray the L137 8-step flow every day this week.
  • Log which step you find easiest and which you find hardest.

Module 2 practicum check

Before advancing to Module 3, a student must submit:

  • The seven redemption realities written out with full KJV verses under each
  • 7 days of the L137 8-step flow logged
  • Luke 10:19 and Luke 1:37 recited from memory in front of a peer or small-group leader
