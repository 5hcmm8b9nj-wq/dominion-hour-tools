Module 1 — The Ordinances of Intimacy

Opening module of Course 1 in both schools. Opening module for both schools and re-anchored in the deliverance-ministry tradition of L137 and Dominion Hour.

Course: Foundations, Redemption & the Holy Spirit (L137) / Foundations, Redemption & the Prophetic Spirit (Dominion Hour)

Length: 4 lessons (one per week for 4 weeks, or one per day in the 12-week Foundations Intensive)

Anchor scripture: John 15:4–5 KJV — "Abide in me, and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit of itself, except it abide in the vine; no more can ye, except ye abide in me. I am the vine, ye are the branches: He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing."

Course outcome

By the end of this module, the student can:

1. Explain what an "ordinance of intimacy" is and why it is non-negotiable for a deliverance minister. 2. Keep a daily prayer log, a daily Word-study log, and a weekly worship log. 3. Name the four ordinances from memory. 4. Show 28 days of unbroken practice before advancing to Module 2.

Why this module comes first

You cannot cast out what you have not overcome. You cannot break yokes you have not renounced. And you cannot minister deliverance from a distance — deliverance ministry flows out of intimacy with Jesus Christ. Every teaching Pastor Kiki Ocansey has ever released, and every prophetic word Prophet Prince Amo David has ever spoken, sits on top of a life built on these four ordinances.

Lesson 1 — A Strategic and Systemic Prayer Life

Anchor scripture — quoted in full KJV:

Luke 18:1 — "And he spake a parable unto them to this end, that men ought always to pray, and not to faint."
1 Thessalonians 5:17 — "Pray without ceasing."
Ephesians 6:18 — "Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit, and watching thereunto with all perseverance and supplication for all saints."

What Scripture plainly says

  • Prayer is not optional; it is a commandment (Luke 18:1).
  • Prayer is meant to be continuous, not occasional (1 Thessalonians 5:17).
  • Prayer must be strategic ("with all prayer and supplication") and Spirit-led (Ephesians 6:18).

What we teach

A "strategic and systemic" prayer life has three parts:

1. A fixed altar — a set time and place. Pastor Kiki teaches 5 AM; Prophet Prince opens Clubhouse at 8:00 AM Eastern. Pick yours and do not move it. 2. A fixed pattern — the L137 8-step deliverance flow becomes the daily template:

- Praise and worship

- Thanksgiving

- Confession of sins

- Forgive others

- Invite the Holy Spirit

- Word / teaching

- Prayer and declarations

- Sealing with the blood of Jesus 3. A fixed record — a prayer journal. The mind forgets; the page remembers.

Repeat-after-me declaration

"I will pray without ceasing. My altar is set. My time is fixed. My tongue is trained. I will not faint. In Jesus' name, Amen."

Weekly assignment

  • Pray at the same time every day for 7 days.
  • Log start time, end time, and one thing the Holy Spirit said.
  • Bring your log to the next class.

Lesson 2 — A Systemic Word Study Life

Anchor scripture — quoted in full KJV:

Joshua 1:8 — "This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success."
2 Timothy 2:15 — "Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth."
Psalm 1:1–3 — "Blessed is the man that walketh not in the counsel of the ungodly, nor standeth in the way of sinners, nor sitteth in the seat of the scornful. But his delight is in the law of the Lord; and in his law doth he meditate day and night. And he shall be like a tree planted by the rivers of water, that bringeth forth his fruit in his season; his leaf also shall not wither; and whatsoever he doeth shall prosper."

What Scripture plainly says

  • The Word must stay in your mouth, not just in your Bible (Joshua 1:8).
  • Study is a labor, not a hobby (2 Timothy 2:15).
  • The blessed man is the one who meditates day and night (Psalm 1:2).

What we teach — the three ways to study the Bible

A three-fold Bible-study pattern:

1. Book-by-book — read one book of the Bible cover to cover. Start with the Gospel of John. 2. Chapter-by-chapter — take one chapter, verse by verse, note every promise, command, warning, and person. 3. Topical — pick one theme (deliverance, faith, healing, prayer, righteousness) and read every verse the Spirit leads you to on that theme.

Reading rule for both schools: KJV only, in full. No snippets. No paraphrases. No commentary before the text. Read the Scripture first — let it speak — then reach for the teaching.

Repeat-after-me declaration

"The Word of God shall not depart out of my mouth. I meditate day and night. My leaf shall not wither. In Jesus' name, Amen."

Weekly assignment

  • Read one Gospel this week (Matthew, Mark, Luke, or John — student's choice).
  • Write down five verses that jumped out.
  • Under each verse, answer: what is God saying, and what am I to do?

Lesson 3 — Worship

Anchor scripture — quoted in full KJV:

John 4:23–24 — "But the hour cometh, and now is, when the true worshippers shall worship the Father in spirit and in truth: for the Father seeketh such to worship him. God is a Spirit: and they that worship him must worship him in spirit and in truth."
Psalm 22:3 — "But thou art holy, O thou that inhabitest the praises of Israel."
Hebrews 13:15 — "By him therefore let us offer the sacrifice of praise to God continually, that is, the fruit of our lips giving thanks to his name."

What Scripture plainly says

  • Worship is what the Father seeks (John 4:23).
  • God inhabits the praises of His people (Psalm 22:3).
  • Praise is a sacrifice — it costs something — and it is meant to be continuous (Hebrews 13:15).

What we teach

Worship is not a music genre; it is a posture. Three practical rules:

1. Private worship comes before public worship. If you cannot worship alone in your bedroom at 5 AM, you have nothing to bring to the Clubhouse room at 8 AM. 2. Worship is not entertainment. No performance. No showmanship. Just the fruit of your lips giving thanks to His name. 3. Worship is warfare. When Jehoshaphat sent the singers ahead of the army, the ambushments began (2 Chronicles 20:22). Worship dismantles altars.

Repeat-after-me declaration

"I am a true worshipper. I worship in spirit and in truth. My praise is a sacrifice. My worship is warfare. In Jesus' name, Amen."

Weekly assignment

  • Set aside 15 minutes of private worship, three times this week.
  • Sing without music at least once.
  • Note in your journal: what did the Holy Spirit do during that time?

Lesson 4 — The Corporate Gathering of Believers

Anchor scripture — quoted in full KJV:

Hebrews 10:24–25 — "And let us consider one another to provoke unto love and to good works: Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as the manner of some is; but exhorting one another: and so much the more, as ye see the day approaching."
Acts 2:42 — "And they continued stedfastly in the apostles' doctrine and fellowship, and in breaking of bread, and in prayers."
Matthew 18:20 — "For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them."

What Scripture plainly says

  • Do not forsake the assembling of yourselves together (Hebrews 10:25).
  • The early church continued steadfastly in four things: doctrine, fellowship, breaking of bread, prayers (Acts 2:42).
  • Christ Himself is in the midst wherever two or three gather in His name (Matthew 18:20).

What we teach

The four corporate gatherings expected of every student:

1. Your local church. No online school replaces the local body. 2. Your ministry's live services — L137 (Monday, Wednesday, Saturday) or Dominion Hour Clubhouse (Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, 8:00 AM Eastern). 3. A weekly small group — WhatsApp prayer group, home cell, or Cave of Adullam prayer watch. 4. Corporate fasting seasons — the 21-day Root of the Problem fast (joint) and the Sure Mercies fasting booklet (Dominion Hour) are standing entries.

Repeat-after-me declaration

"I will not forsake the assembly. I gather with the saints. I am part of a body. In Jesus' name, Amen."

Weekly assignment

  • Attend your local church this Sunday.
  • Attend at least three live ministry services (L137 or Dominion Hour).
  • Join or lead one small-group prayer session.

Module 1 practicum check

Before advancing to Module 2 (Redemption Realities & Authority of the Believer), a student must submit:

  • 28 days of prayer log (date, time, one Spirit-impression per day)
  • 28 days of Word-study log (Scripture read, one verse of note per day)
  • 12 worship entries across 4 weeks
  • Attendance record: local church + 12 ministry services + 4 small-group meetings

Nothing else opens until Module 1 closes. The principle: no impartation without immersion.
