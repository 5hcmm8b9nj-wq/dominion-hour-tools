Module — Biblical Stewardship (L137) / Kingdom Stewardship (Dominion Hour)

Capstone module of Course 5 in both schools.

Course: Stewardship, Family Altar & Ministry Income (L137) / Kingdom Stewardship & Family Governance (Dominion Hour)

Length: 4 lessons

Anchor scriptures — quoted in full KJV:

Deuteronomy 8:18 — "But thou shalt remember the Lord thy God: for it is he that giveth thee power to get wealth, that he may establish his covenant which he sware unto thy fathers, as it is this day."
3 John 1:2 — "Beloved, I wish above all things that thou mayest prosper and be in health, even as thy soul prospereth."
Malachi 3:10 — "Bring ye all the tithes into the storehouse, that there may be meat in mine house, and prove me now herewith, saith the Lord of hosts, if I will not open you the windows of heaven, and pour you out a blessing, that there shall not be room enough to receive it."
2 Corinthians 9:7 — "Every man according as he purposeth in his heart, so let him give; not grudgingly, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver."

Course outcome

By the end of this module, the student:

1. Understands biblical wealth is for covenant, not consumption. 2. Runs a household budget aligned with Scripture. 3. Gives biblically without hype, manipulation, or seed-faith pressure. 4. Knows how a Kingdom ministry funds itself with integrity.

Lesson 1 — Wealth Is a Tool, Not a God

What Scripture plainly says

  • Wealth is given by God to establish His covenant, not for personal luxury (Deuteronomy 8:18).
  • The love of money is a root of evil, not money itself (1 Timothy 6:10).
  • You cannot serve God and mammon (Matthew 6:24).

What we teach

  • Prosperity is not the goal; covenant is the goal.
  • Money is a tool for advancing the Kingdom, caring for family, blessing the poor, and building the church.
  • A minister with disordered finances has disordered ministry.
Repeat-after-me declaration: "Money serves me; I do not serve money. I remember the Lord my God, who gives me power to get wealth to establish His covenant. In Jesus' name, Amen."

Lesson 2 — The Tithe and the Offering

What Scripture plainly says

  • The tithe belongs to the Lord (Leviticus 27:30).
  • Bring the whole tithe into the storehouse, and He will open the windows of heaven (Malachi 3:10).
  • Every man gives as he purposes in his heart, not by pressure (2 Corinthians 9:7).

What we teach

  • The tithe is 10% of income, brought to the local church (the storehouse).
  • The offering is separate from the tithe — it is what you purpose in your heart.
  • Both are worship, not tax.
  • What we do not teach: manipulation, seed-faith hype, "sow $1,000 for a miracle," or any scheme that pressures the poor to give what they do not have.
Repeat-after-me declaration: "I tithe with joy. I give as I purpose in my heart, not grudgingly, not of necessity. God loves a cheerful giver, and I am one. In Jesus' name, Amen."
Weekly assignment
  • List your last month's income.
  • Calculate the tithe.
  • Journal: is it going to the storehouse, on time, in full?

Lesson 3 — The Family Altar and Household Governance

What Scripture plainly says

  • Joshua declared, "as for me and my house, we will serve the Lord" (Joshua 24:15).
  • Whoever provides not for his own house has denied the faith (1 Timothy 5:8).
  • Train up a child in the way he should go (Proverbs 22:6).

What we teach — the family altar (drawn from your Kinyanjui Family Altar project)

1. Fix a time — daily if possible, weekly minimum. 2. Fix an anchor Scripture — one for the family, printed on the wall. 3. Fix a rhythm — praise, Word, prayer, declarations, sealing. 4. Involve every member — spouse, children, whoever is under your roof. 5. Keep a family prayer journal.

Household finance rules

  • Live on less than you earn.
  • Save before you spend.
  • Give before you save.
  • Owe no man anything but love (Romans 13:8).
Weekly assignment
  • If you have a family, run the family altar three times this week.
  • If you live alone, run a personal altar five times.
  • Journal what changed.

Lesson 4 — Ministry Income with Integrity

What Scripture plainly says

  • The labourer is worthy of his hire (Luke 10:7).
  • Not for filthy lucre, but of a ready mind (1 Peter 5:2).
  • Provide things honest in the sight of all men (Romans 12:17).

What we teach

Ministers of L137 and Dominion Hour follow these guardrails:

  • Never charge for deliverance ministry.
  • Never charge for prayer.
  • Never make the poor feel small for giving small.
  • Publish giving accounts openly (like the L137 country-specific giving cards).
  • Publish outreach — like the Ghana and Jamaica children's home work — so every giver sees where the money went.
  • Separate personal finance from ministry finance.

Ministry funding streams your school will teach:

1. Freewill offerings — the primary stream. 2. Partnership — monthly partners who commit to the mission. 3. Resource sales — books, courses, workbooks (never deliverance itself). 4. Sponsored outreach — orphanage, prison, hospital ministry, sponsored by donors.

Repeat-after-me declaration: "I will not merchandise the gospel. I will not sell what the Lord gave freely. I will handle every naira, cedi, and dollar with integrity, before God and men. In Jesus' name, Amen."
Weekly assignment
  • Draft your personal ministry-integrity statement (5 sentences maximum).
  • Bring it to your pastor for review.

Module practicum check

Before graduation, a student must submit:

  • A written personal budget
  • A tithe log for the last 30 days
  • A signed family-altar log for 30 days
  • A personal ministry-integrity statement
