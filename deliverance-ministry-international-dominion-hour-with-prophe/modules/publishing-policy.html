Module — Publishing Policy for Deliverance Ministers

Middle module of Course 2 in both schools. Adapted from Pastor Kiki Ocansey's L137 Publishing Policy and Prophet Prince Amo David's Dominion Hour transcript rule.

Course: Deliverance / Prophetic Ministry Leadership & Ethics

Length: 1 lesson + 1 practicum

Anchor scripture — quoted in full KJV:

Proverbs 11:13 — "A talebearer revealeth secrets: but he that is of a faithful spirit concealeth the matter."
Proverbs 25:9–10 — "Debate thy cause with thy neighbour himself; and discover not a secret to another: Lest he that heareth it put thee to shame, and thine infamy turn not away."

Course outcome

By the end of this module, the student can look at any piece of ministry content — a Clubhouse recording, a WhatsApp voice note, a Zoom replay, a personal testimony — and know exactly what may be published and what must be removed.

Why this module exists

Deliverance ministry deals with people at their most vulnerable. Manifestations, family sin patterns, sexual bondage, prophecies over specific people, testimonies of demonic encounters — these are ministry moments, not content. A minister who cannot keep a secret has no business publishing.

Both L137 and Dominion Hour have written policies. This module teaches them.

Rule 1 — Preserve exactly

The following must never be altered when publishing:

  • The biblical teaching, as delivered
  • Every scripture, in full KJV — no snippets, no paraphrases
  • Every corporate declaration, exactly as spoken
  • Every corporate prayer
  • Every deliverance principle and teaching illustration
  • Doctrine and meaning

You may not "improve" the pastor. If Pastor Kiki said "sons of anak" you do not change it to "sons of Anak" without asking. If Prophet Prince said "Pp." at the end of a word, you do not remove it.

Rule 2 — Remove before publication

The following must always come out of any public deliverable:

  • Personal names — Pastor's family, friends, anyone spoken in the room
  • Family names
  • Personal prophecies over named individuals
  • Individual deliverance ministry (what the pastor did with one specific person)
  • Manifestations (screaming, coughing, vomiting, shaking)
  • Personal counseling exchanges
  • Sensitive personal testimonies
  • Confidential ministry conversations
  • Dates and locations of individual encounters

Rule 3 — Two versions live side by side

Every teaching produces two documents:

1. Master Transcript (private) — the pastor's own copy. Never leaves the ministry. 2. Clean Public Transcript — the anonymized version. Every downstream deliverable is drawn from this one.

Rule 5 — When in doubt, ask the pastor

If you are not sure whether something can be published, the answer is no until the pastor says yes. Not "probably fine." Not "it was mostly public anyway." Ask.

Rule 6 — Anonymized testimonies

When a personal testimony is essential to the teaching, the minister may choose one of two options:

  • Option A (default): remove the testimony entirely.
  • Option B (only with explicit permission): anonymize — no names, no country, no relationships, no dates. Never publish both the name and the event.

Rule 7 — Physical elements

Oil, water, communion may be referenced only through Christ's finished work. Never as a ritual on their own. No salt baths. No covenant-of-salt ritual language. This is Pastor Kiki's standing instruction for L137 materials, and Dominion Hour follows the same guardrail.

Rule 8 — Sources you may not use

  • Reddit
  • New Age / new-age doctrine
  • Pop-astrology / "Auntie Flow" / "Sharman" type sites
  • Any non-Christian religious source

Rule 9 — Sources you may use

  • The KJV Bible (primary)
  • Reputable Christian ministries (Impact Christian Books, Eckhardt Ministries, Kenneth Hagin Ministries, John Wesley writings, Charles Spurgeon, etc.)
  • Cross-references within Scripture
  • The pastor's own teaching library

Anonymization checklist (used every time)

Before a document goes public, run this checklist. If any box cannot be ticked, the document is not ready.

  • ☐ All personal names removed or replaced with role words ("a sister," "a brother," "a member")
  • ☐ All family names removed
  • ☐ All personal prophecies removed
  • ☐ All references to specific manifestations removed
  • ☐ All specific dates of personal encounters removed
  • ☐ All locations of personal encounters removed
  • ☐ All confidential ministry conversations removed
  • ☐ Full KJV verses used — no snippets
  • ☐ Ministry attribution correct (Pastor Kiki Ocansey · L137 / Dominion Hour with Prophet Prince Amo David)
  • ☐ Footer correct
  • ☐ Reviewed by the pastor before publication

Practicum

The student is given a raw transcript containing five deliberate policy violations. The student must produce a Clean Public Transcript and correctly identify all five removals. A passing score is 5/5.

Repeat-after-me declaration

"I will not be a talebearer. I will conceal what the Lord has told me to conceal. I will preserve the teaching, protect the vulnerable, and honor the pastor. I will publish nothing that has not been approved. In Jesus' name, Amen."
