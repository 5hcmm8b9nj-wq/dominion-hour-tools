Practicum, assessment & commissioning

Every student, in every course, submits a weekly workbook page with the same four-part structure — what Scripture plainly says · what the minister taught · prayer response · declaration. Full details in the assessment rubric.

  • 10 supervised deliverance sessions (reference codes only, never names)
  • 30-day aftercare tracking on every session
  • Written + oral term review at the end of every course
  • Graduation & Impartation — laying-on-of-hands by Pastor Kiki Ocansey. Certificate signed "L137 Deliverance Ministry International / Luke 1:37 KJV / For with God nothing shall be impossible."
