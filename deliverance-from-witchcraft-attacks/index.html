About this teaching

Delivered on the third Friday of the church's 21-day corporate fast, this session exposes the operations of witchcraft against a believer's finances, marriage, health, ministry, and destiny — and walks the church through the biblical response: repentance, forgiveness, renunciation, breaking of covenants, deliverance from agents, healing and restoration, and sealing in the blood of Jesus.

The teaching also includes the prophetic instruction for the upcoming anointing service, guidance on dream attacks, and the corporate seven-fold "Holy Ghost fire!" declaration standing on Psalm 18:12-14. All twenty corporate declarations are preserved verbatim.

All personal ministry — roll-call, names of congregants, private prophecies, and individual counselling — has been removed from every public asset so this pack is safe to share widely for study and prayer. The complete private transcript remains internal to the ministry.

Ministry standard

  • KJV only. Every Scripture written out in full.
  • Verbatim declarations. Preserved exactly as delivered.
  • Standard deliverance flow. Praise · Thanksgiving · Confession · Forgive · Invite the Holy Spirit · Teach · Pray and declare · Seal in the blood.
  • No New Age, no occult sources. Christian sources only.
