Before you begin

You have just come out of a deliverance session. The Word teaches that after the strongman is bound and the goods are spoiled, the house must be swept clean and filled — otherwise seven worse spirits may return (Matthew 12:43–45). This companion is that sweeping and filling.

Move through the nine stations in order. Speak every declaration aloud, and read its verse aloud as well. The Word is the sword; the declaration is the swing. Tap each line as you say it.

  • Every declaration is anchored in a full KJV verse right beneath it.
  • All scripture is King James Version, written out in full.
  • Nothing here replaces your pastor’s spiritual authority — this is companion material for private prayer.

Sealed and Standing 🕊️

You have swept the house and filled it with the Word. Rest now. Behold, he that keepeth Israel shall neither slumber nor sleep (Psalm 121:4, KJV).
