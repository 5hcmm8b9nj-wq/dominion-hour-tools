About Prophet Prince Amo David

A prophetic and teaching grace.

PA
Prophet Prince Amo David
Prophetic Teacher · Equipping Vessel
Accra, Ghana

With over two decades of ministry experience, Prophet Prince Amo David carries a distinct prophetic and teaching grace that releases deliverance, revelation, and the uncompromised Word of God with clarity and power.

He is recognized for his equipping mantle — raising believers into a deeper understanding of their identity in Christ, and activating them into a life of effective prayer, spiritual authority, and consistent victory. Through sound doctrine, prophetic insight, and strategic warfare prayer, his ministry brings transformation, restoration, and spiritual growth to individuals and communities.

Prophet Prince Amo David is passionate about establishing believers in the grace of God, the love of Christ, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit. His teachings emphasize not only receiving deliverance, but remaining free and living a disciplined, kingdom-centered life.

Ministry Focus

What the Prophet carries.

Five streams flow out of the Dominion Hour mantle — each one designed to take the believer from receiving to releasing.

Prophetic teaching and divine revelation
Deliverance and spiritual warfare
Equipping believers for authority and dominion
Teaching grace, identity, and spiritual maturity
Empowering believers to live in sustained victory
Power Statement
A prophetic teacher and equipping vessel, Prophet Prince Amo David is called to raise a generation of believers who walk in power, authority, and dominion through Jesus Christ — established in truth, walking in freedom, and living in victory.
