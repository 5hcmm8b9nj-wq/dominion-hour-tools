About L137 Deliverance Ministries

A ministry born in the fire.

L137 Deliverance Ministries International, led by Pastor Kiki Ocansey, is a midnight prayer line standing on Luke 1:37 — "For with God nothing shall be impossible." We carry every kind of warfare prayer to the throne and speak in tongues of fire as the Holy Spirit gives utterance.

Our model is the Garden of Gethsemane — Jesus pressing in until heaven answered (Matthew 26:36–46) — and the two witnesses of Revelation 11:3–5, fire proceeding out of their mouths to devour their enemies by the power of the Holy Ghost.

We share the Word as the Spirit leads, lift up prayer requests by name, and intercede for one another until breakthrough comes. No spectacle. No striving. Just the family of God, contending together at midnight.

"For with God nothing shall be impossible." — Luke 1:37 (KJV)
How We Pray

Three movements, every midnight.

1

The Word

We open in scripture. Pastor Kiki shares the word as the Spirit leads, anchoring the prayer line in the truth that frames the night.

2

Warfare & Tongues of Fire

We pray every type of warfare prayer and speak in tongues of fire — modeled after Revelation 11:3–5 — until the atmosphere shifts.

3

Prayer Requests & Agreement

We lift up the family by name, standing in agreement on the requests submitted that week. We do not leave anyone behind.
