How to use

Three ways these workbooks are being used

Pastor Kiki shares these freely so the body of Christ can pray in agreement — alone, with family, and across our WhatsApp and Clubhouse rooms.

1

Personal warfare

Print a copy. Set aside an hour. Move slowly through each section out loud — repentance first, then declarations, then communion.

2

Family altar

Open the workbook with your household. Read one section together each evening for a week. Let the children declare too.

3

WhatsApp & Clubhouse

Share the PDF in your prayer group. Pray a single section together on a voice note or live room. Carry the declarations through the week.
