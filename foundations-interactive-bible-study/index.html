Theological theme

Foundations — from Jericho's ruins to
the city whose builder is God

An interactive study that maps historical biblical narratives — the lineage of Terah, the fall and rebuilding of Jericho, David's cry when foundations collapse, Paul's masterbuilder theology — against the deeper theme of spiritual architecture and generational legacy.

“For other foundation can no man lay than that is laid, which is Jesus Christ.”

— 1 Corinthians 3:11 (KJV)

How to use this study

  1. Timeline — walk the Foundations story from Babel to the eternal city, filtered by era or theme.
  2. Commentary — search reputable Christian commentary (Barnes, Ellicott, Cambridge, Benson, Matthew Henry) by phrase, verse, or theme.
  3. Verse Compare — read the same passage side-by-side in KJV, NKJV, ESV, NIV, and WEB.
  4. Journal — record insights on how each narrative speaks to legacy and spiritual architecture. Export your entries anytime.
