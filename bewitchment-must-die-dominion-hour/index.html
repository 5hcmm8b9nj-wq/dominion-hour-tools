Module 01 · Praise & Thanksgiving

Begin at His Gates

Every deliverance service opens with worship. We do not rush to warfare — we first ascend His hill with thanksgiving.

📖 Scripture — Psalm 95:1–6 (KJV)

"O come, let us sing unto the LORD: let us make a joyful noise to the rock of our salvation. Let us come before his presence with thanksgiving, and make a joyful noise unto him with psalms. For the LORD is a great God, and a great King above all gods. In his hand are the deep places of the earth: the strength of the hills is his also. The sea is his, and he made it: and his hands formed the dry land. O come, let us worship and bow down: let us kneel before the LORD our maker."

✦ Teaching

The Lord deserves our thanksgiving. He shares glory with no man. So we come to worship — the Almighty, the Immortal, the Invisible, the Ancient of Days, the Rock of Ages.

Thank Him for: the gift of life, His protection, His provision, His preservation, watching over your steps, and His Word — "Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path."

Module 02 · Confession & Cleansing

Silence the Accuser

Before we command anything on the enemy's side, we plead the blood over our own hearts. The mouth of the accuser is closed by the blood.

📖 Scripture — Revelation 12:10–11 (KJV)

"And I heard a loud voice saying in heaven, Now is come salvation, and strength, and the kingdom of our God, and the power of his Christ: for the accuser of our brethren is cast down, which accused them before our God day and night. And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death."

📖 Scripture — 1 John 1:8–9 (KJV)

"If we say that we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us. If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness."

✦ Practice

Plead the blood. Ask the Lord to sanctify, wash, and make you whole again. Let the blood silence the voice of the accuser — the one that accuses day and night before the throne. Bind every negative presence, every negative energy that has entered your garden. Consume every spell through the fire.

Module 04 · What Is Bewitchment?

Naming the Enemy

You cannot destroy what you cannot define. Bewitchment operates because it stays hidden.

Invisible

Spiritual power operating out of view of the natural eye.

Manipulation

You are being moved — but not by God, and not by your own will.

Deception

To be misled — running after a lie away from God's truth.

Control

Demonic control disguised as circumstance, opinion, or fate.

⚠ Scope of the Attack

Witchcraft can destroy a family, a community, a nation. Through the witchcrafts of Jezebel, Israel entered famine — dryness over the land, no rain, national and economic crisis.

Some territories belong to witchcraft manipulation. Anything planted or established there struggles unless God has given an upper hand to break through the demonic atmosphere. Businesses and ministries fail to make headway because the people in that area have been bewitched with spells.

⚠ Simon the Sorcerer — Acts 8:9–11 (KJV)

"But there was a certain man, called Simon, which beforetime in the same city used sorcery, and bewitched the people of Samaria, giving out that himself was some great one: To whom they all gave heed, from the least to the greatest, saying, This man is the great power of God. And to him they had regard, because that of long time he had bewitched them with sorceries."

✦ Why Cities Fall

Sorcery is a higher form of witchcraft. Simon bewitched an entire city — young and old, from the least to the greatest. When bewitchment stays long enough, everyone starts calling the wrong power "the great power of God."

📖 The eyes must open — 2 Corinthians 4:4 (KJV)

"In whom the god of this world hath blinded the minds of them which believe not, lest the light of the glorious gospel of Christ, who is the image of God, should shine unto them."

Module 05 · Scriptural Foundation

What God Has Said About Enchantment

Six scriptures form the wall of fire around every deliverance from bewitchment.

📖 Numbers 23:23–24 (KJV) — The Anchor Verse

"Surely there is no enchantment against Jacob, neither is there any divination against Israel: according to this time it shall be said of Jacob and of Israel, What hath God wrought! "There shall not be found among you any one that maketh his son or his daughter to pass through the fire, or that useth divination, or an observer of times, or an enchanter, or a witch, or a charmer, or a consulter with familiar spirits, or a wizard, or a necromancer."

📖 Job 5:12 (KJV) — He Frustrates the Crafty

"He disappointeth the devices of the crafty, so that their hands cannot perform their enterprise."

📖 Proverbs 23:6 (KJV) — Do Not Eat Her Bread

"Eat thou not the bread of him that hath an evil eye, neither desire thou his dainty meats."

📖 Isaiah 6:1 (KJV) — Vision Above Every Throne

"In the year that king Uzziah died I saw also the Lord sitting upon a throne, high and lifted up, and his train filled the temple."

Module 07 · Prophetic Direction

Anointing Oil with Garlic

A prophetic instruction under the finished work of Christ — not ritual on its own. Faith in Jesus is what acts through it.

🕊 Why This Direction

The Lord instructed Moses how to prepare anointing oil so that whatever is used will bring testimony and deliverance. Olive oil represents divine blessing and purity. Fresh garlic — added under the blood of Jesus — activates the oil against witchcraft attack. This is faith in Christ operating through obedience to prophetic instruction. It is not a ritual on its own.

Preparation

1
Pure olive oil

Anointing oil — the base.

2
Fresh garlic

Not powdered. Crush if needed.

3
Soak 3 days

Let it steep until it is strong.

4
Pray & consecrate

Turn it into the blood of Jesus by faith.

Anointing — For the Body

A
Head

Crown and forehead.

B
Palms

Both hands, then wrists.

C
Feet

Soles and ankles.

D
Wedding ring

If married — anoint band and finger.

Anoint gently — do not overdo it.

The Cleansing Bath — 3 Evenings

1. In your bucket, add water and salt.
2. Add some of the oil-garlic mixture.
3. Pray as you bathe:

"Lord, I wash myself from every attack of witchcraft. Everything that has brought lack and want in my life, Lord, wash it away in the name of Jesus Christ."

4. Repeat for three consecutive evenings — starting tonight.

🔥 The Prayer Over the Oil
"I turn it into the blood of Jesus. I bless it for you. Let it perform signs and wonders. Let it break every evil covenant in your life. Let this anointing break every chronic yoke, every unbreakable yoke, from today. The anointing breaks the yoke."
