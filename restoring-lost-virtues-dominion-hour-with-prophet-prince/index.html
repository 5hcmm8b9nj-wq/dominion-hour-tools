The big idea

Virtue can be exchanged. Virtue can be recovered.

Virtue is a divine ability — the blessing of the Lord that makes rich and adds no sorrow. It heals the sick, breaks yokes, opens doors, and singles you out for favour. When virtue leaves through sin, contamination, satanic transactions, sex, food from an evil eye, wrong friendship, or a demonic gift, life becomes empty and difficult. The same Jesus from whom virtue went out into the woman with the issue of blood still restores lost virtues today. By repentance, faith, the Word, and the blood of the covenant, whatever the enemy took is recovered.
