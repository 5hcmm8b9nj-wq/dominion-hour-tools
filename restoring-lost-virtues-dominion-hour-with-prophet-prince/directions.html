Prophetic directions

How to walk this out

The practical instructions Prophet Prince released in this service — the 21-day fast, anointing oil, Saturday deliverance service, Clubhouse schedule, WhatsApp Scriptures, guarded almsgiving, tithe and sowing, and how to guard your senses through this season.

Direction 01
Fasting

21-day fast beginning Monday. Fast Monday to Friday each week; eat on weekends and resume on Monday. Keep to your normal prayer time. In the closing seven days Prophet Prince and Woman of God Pastor Kiky lead a consistent morning and evening prayer.

Direction 02
Anointing oil

Take your anointing oil during the teaching. Pray over it and anoint yourself as declarations are released — marking yourself with the blood and the oil for a turnaround.

Direction 03
Saturday deliverance service

Meet on Saturday for the Deliverance Service with Prophet Prince and Woman of God Pastor Kiky. 12:00 midnight BST · 11:00 PM GMT · 7:00 PM EST · 6:00 PM CST · 5:00 PM MST · 4:00 PM PST.

Direction 04
Clubhouse schedule

Live on Clubhouse — Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday at 8:00 AM EST / 1:00 PM GMT. Come with an open heart; the 8:30–9:00 AM window is preparation, expectation, Scripture and prayer.

Direction 05
WhatsApp scriptures

Scriptures will be sent through the WhatsApp platform during the fast — meditate on them daily.

Direction 06
Almsgiving with discernment

Bless orphanages and the poor with a pure heart. Refuse gifts, meals, or transactions given with a hidden intention to exchange your virtue. If a gift is contaminated, return it or lay it before the Lord.

Direction 07
Sowing & tithing

Give your tithe as a covering over the 90 percent; give as the Lord leads with faith, not for show. Vain glory and love of money must not touch the servant of God.

Direction 08
Guard your senses

Do not eat the bread of him that hath an evil eye (Proverbs 23:6). Discern before you eat, before you sleep with anyone, before you accept a gift, and before you receive counsel.

Direction 09
Live in the Word

Read Psalm 103, Psalm 51, Psalm 119:18, Mark 5:25–34 and Luke 8:43–48 daily through this season. Faith comes by hearing the word — refined through anointed vessels.
