Section 1 · Sermon Snapshot

What this service was about

Prophet Prince Amo David opened with thanksgiving and declared that a season of breakthrough had arrived — over marriage, ministry, career, finances, health, and spiritual life. The teaching moves through two anchor scriptures — Luke 5:1–11 (Peter’s empty nets and the great catch) and 2 Samuel 5:17–25 (David at Baal‑Perazim) — and ends with a prophetic cup‑of‑water and salt instruction for restoration and “taste” of life.

Key promises spoken over the room

  • Your breakthrough is here.
  • Your bank account, your baskets, your storehouses will be full.
  • The Lord will fill your life with peace, joy, favor, and blessing.
  • Every altar, every wall of resistance is broken today.
  • Whatever the enemy is sitting on — your promotion, glory, contracts, money, doors, peace — the Lord will remove them.

Section 2 · What Is a Breakthrough

Prophet Prince’s working definition

“A breakthrough is a sudden and powerful act of God that removes a major barrier, transforms an impossible problem. A breakthrough is a sudden and powerful act of God that removes major barriers.”

He continued: the Hebrew and Greek words behind “breakthrough” carry the sense of break out, bust out, or tear down a wall. God is often seen as the Breaker who opens a way through closed doors.

Where breakthrough is needed

  • Marriage & relationship
  • Career & business
  • Finances & storehouses
  • Ministry & spiritual growth
  • Health, healing, and deliverance from sickness
  • Family lines, father’s house, mother’s house
  • Legal matters and long‑standing court cases
Section 3 · Anchor Scripture I

Luke 5 — Empty nets, then a net‑breaking catch

Prophet Prince re‑read the account of Peter, who had toiled all night and taken nothing, and then at the word of Jesus let down his net for a draught. This is the primary picture the whole service is built on: “Your season of waiting for that breakthrough is now over.”

Luke 5:1–11 (KJV)

1And it came to pass, that, as the people pressed upon him to hear the word of God, he stood by the lake of Gennesaret, 2And saw two ships standing by the lake: but the fishermen were gone out of them, and were washing their nets. 3And he entered into one of the ships, which was Simon’s, and prayed him that he would thrust out a little from the land. And he sat down, and taught the people out of the ship. 4Now when he had left speaking, he said unto Simon, Launch out into the deep, and let down your nets for a draught. 5And Simon answering said unto him, Master, we have toiled all the night, and have taken nothing: nevertheless at thy word I will let down the net. 6And when they had this done, they inclosed a great multitude of fishes: and their net brake. 7And they beckoned unto their partners, which were in the other ship, that they should come and help them. And they came, and filled both the ships, so that they began to sink. 8When Simon Peter saw it, he fell down at Jesus’ knees, saying, Depart from me; for I am a sinful man, O Lord. 9For he was astonished, and all that were with him, at the draught of the fishes which they had taken: 10And so was also James, and John, the sons of Zebedee, which were partners with Simon. And Jesus said unto Simon, Fear not; from henceforth thou shalt catch men. 11And when they had brought their ships to land, they forsook all, and followed him.

What the Prophet drew out

  • Peter’s hard work through the night produced nothing — until the word of Jesus came.
  • Breakthrough is not about working harder. It is about the supernatural power, favor, and anointing of the Holy Ghost changing the narrative.
  • When the word of God is applied, the net breaks — a “net‑breaking miracle,” a boat‑filling, partner‑calling catch.
  • “At thy word” is the posture of faith the whole room was invited into.
Section 4 · Anchor Scripture II

2 Samuel 5 — David at Baal‑Perazim

Prophet Prince turned to David’s first battles as newly anointed king. The Philistines heard he had been anointed and came up to look for him. David inquired of the Lord, and God handed them into his hands “like a breakthrough of water.” Then they came up a second time and God gave a different strategy — circle behind, and wait for the sound in the mulberry trees.

2 Samuel 5:17–25 (KJV)

17But when the Philistines heard that they had anointed David king over Israel, all the Philistines came up to seek David; and David heard of it, and went down to the hold. 18The Philistines also came and spread themselves in the valley of Rephaim. 19And David enquired of the Lord, saying, Shall I go up to the Philistines? wilt thou deliver them into mine hand? And the Lord said unto David, Go up: for I will doubtless deliver the Philistines into thine hand. 20And David came to Baal‑perazim, and David smote them there, and said, The Lord hath broken forth upon mine enemies before me, as the breach of waters. Therefore he called the name of that place Baal‑perazim. 21And there they left their images, and David and his men burned them. 22And the Philistines came up yet again, and spread themselves in the valley of Rephaim. 23And when David enquired of the Lord, he said, Thou shalt not go up; but fetch a compass behind them, and come upon them over against the mulberry trees. 24And let it be, when thou hearest the sound of a going in the tops of the mulberry trees, that then thou shalt bestir thyself: for then shall the Lord go out before thee, to smite the host of the Philistines. 25And David did so, as the Lord had commanded him; and smote the Philistines from Geba until thou come to Gazer.

What the Prophet drew out

  • The Philistines rise when you rise. They only came looking for David after he was anointed king. Fresh anointing draws fresh opposition.
  • Enquire of the Lord — twice. Same enemy, different strategy. Don’t assume yesterday’s tactic is today’s instruction.
  • Baal‑Perazim means the Lord of Breakthroughs. When God breaks through, it bursts like water — nothing stops it.
  • Burn the idols. After the victory, David destroyed what the enemy left behind. Break every altar, image, and secret device.
  • Wait for the sound. When you hear the sound in the mulberry trees, the Lord is already going ahead of you.
Section 5 · Core Themes

Six threads running through the whole teaching

1. Breakthrough is the sovereign act of God, not the reward of hard work

“It is not about hard working. People are working hard, doing things the hard way — but it is the word of God that will make the way soft.” Supernatural favor, supernatural anointing, supernatural power change the narrative.

2. Anointing precedes attack — the Philistines only came when David was made king

Because the enemy hears the announcement of your elevation, they set traps, hide in strongholds, and try to block your promotion. But the Lord hands them into your hand.

3. Commitment to the altar unlocks the door

The prophet illustrated this with a young man who said, “I was looking for a room to pray,” and stayed committed until the prophecy of travel and open doors was fulfilled. Commitment to the place of prayer opens the door of breakthrough.

4. Breakthrough bursts like water — it cannot be stopped

Baal‑Perazim: “as the breach of waters.” When the release comes it floods every dry place — finances, marriage, ministry, health.

5. Overflow instead of emptiness

“Fill the empty vessels.” The oil that Elisha poured, the water Jesus turned to wine, the widow’s jars — the Lord is filling every empty place to the brim so no demonic wind can blow you away.

6. Salt and taste — restored meaning and preservation

Ye are the salt of the earth. When the salt loses its taste it is good for nothing. The prophetic instruction with salt in the water declares that life — marriage, ministry, calling — becomes tasteful, meaningful, and preserved again.

Section 7 · Prophetic Instruction

The cup, the water, and the salt

✧

Prophet Prince gave a two‑part prophetic instruction — first with a cup of water, then with salt — to seal the breakthrough decree in a tangible act of faith. Do this reverently, understanding that the water and salt are not power; the Lord Jesus is the Power. They are a point of contact for your faith.

  1. Prepare. Take a bowl with a clean drinking glass inside it, and a bottle of clean water. Sit at your altar of prayer.
  2. Pour the water into the glass slowly and pray as you pour: “Lord, as I pour this water back into the glass, restore my marriage. Restore my star, my glory. Restore my anointing.”
  3. Keep pouring until it overflows into the bowl. Say: “My bowl is overflowing. Let there be an overflow — anointing, favor, grace, wisdom, healing, prosperity.”
  4. Speak Isaiah‑like fullness: “Fill the empty vessels. Let the Lord open the floodgates of heaven into my life from today.”
  5. Add a little salt to the water and declare: “From today, my life shall be tasteful. My taste, my glory has been restored. My life shall be meaningful, in the name of Jesus.” (Matthew 5:13)
  6. Wash your face and hands with the water (or, if you cannot, mark your forehead with the sign of the cross of Calvary using the water). Declare: “Just as Jesus washed their feet and they were changed — from today, I am changed for good.”
  7. Close with thanksgiving. Thank God for the breakthrough already received, the anointing, the grace, and the doors that are now opening.
