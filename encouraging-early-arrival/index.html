Encouraging Early Arrival
to the Clubhouse Room

A pastoral host guide with Scripture-rooted words for the 8:30–9:00 AM gathering window — warm, expectant, and Spirit-focused.

I.The Heart Behind the Words

Since the room is online and listeners are anonymous, the tone must be warm, expectant, and Spirit-focused — never scolding. Three principles guide every word spoken in the 8:30–9:00 AM window:

Principle One

Reward the early ones. Speak to those already present as if they made the right choice — because they did.

Principle Two

Build hunger for what is coming. Hint at what God is about to release through the Prophet.

Principle Three

Never shame the latecomers. Instead, invite them deeper each time someone new joins.

II.The Host Script

A timed flow the host can follow each morning — opening welcome, mid-window encouragement, final gathering, and gentle welcome for those trickling in after 9:00 AM.

8:30 AM · Opening Welcome

Good morning, family. Welcome into Dominion Hour. We are so glad you pressed in this morning. Wherever you are joining us from — your kitchen, your car, your bedroom, another country — the moment you entered this room, you stepped onto holy ground.

We are gathering quietly before the Prophet begins to minister. This is our preparation time.

"Keep thy foot when thou goest to the house of God, and be more ready to hear, than to give the sacrifice of fools." — Ecclesiastes 5:1 (KJV)

So take a deep breath. Quiet your heart. Get your Bible. Get your pen. God is about to speak.

8:40 AM · Building Expectation

Family, if you just joined us — welcome. You are right on time. Heaven has been waiting for you.

As we wait on the Prophet, let me encourage you: the ones who come early are the ones who catch the first drop of the anointing.

"O God, thou art my God; early will I seek thee: my soul thirsteth for thee, my flesh longeth for thee in a dry and thirsty land, where no water is." — Psalm 63:1 (KJV)

Something is being released this morning. Don't miss the beginning. Stay in the room. Invite someone. The Prophet will begin shortly.

8:50 AM · Final Gathering

We are minutes away, family. If you are just walking in — welcome home. Pull up a chair in the spirit. Get quiet.

To everyone who came early this morning: thank you for honoring the presence of God. You set the atmosphere for what God is about to do.

"My voice shalt thou hear in the morning, O Lord; in the morning will I direct my prayer unto thee, and will look up." — Psalm 5:3 (KJV)

You looked up this morning. You showed up. Now get ready to receive.

After 9:00 AM · Welcoming Those Trickling In

Welcome, welcome — whoever just joined, you are in the right place. Don't scroll. Don't leave. Stay right here. What God is releasing this morning is for you. Rewind in your spirit — the Prophet is ministering.

Or alternatively:

To the one who just walked in — God knew you were coming. Your timing is not late; it is ordained. Settle in. Receive.

III.Recurring Phrases That Build Culture

Repeat these week after week until they become part of the room's identity. Consistent language shapes the atmosphere.

  • The early ones catch the first drop of the anointing.
  • Heaven has been waiting for you.
  • You didn't stumble into this room — you were drawn.
  • Wherever you are joining us from, you just stepped onto holy ground.
  • Get your Bible. Get your pen. God is about to speak.
  • This is Dominion Hour — where dominion is restored.

IV.Monthly Teaching Moment

Every few weeks, take 2–3 minutes before the Prophet begins to teach why coming early matters — without naming or shaming anyone.

Teaching · 2–3 minutes

Family, let me share something on my heart. In the natural, when we love someone, we show up early. We don't want to miss a moment. The same is true in the spirit.

When we come early to meet with God, we are saying, "Lord, You are worth my time. You are worth my attention. I don't want to miss what You are saying today."

"Prepare to meet thy God." — Amos 4:12 (KJV)

Preparation is a posture. It says, God, I honor You. And when we honor God with our time, He honors us with His presence.

V.Pre-Service Declaration

Lead the room in this declaration before the Prophet begins to minister. The congregation repeats each line aloud.

Repeat After Me

I honor the presence of God this morning.

I am not here by accident.

I came expecting to receive.

Every spirit of distraction, delay, and slumber
is broken off me now, in the name of Jesus.

My ears are open. My heart is open. My spirit is open.

Lord, speak — Your servant heareth.

Amen.

VI.A Word on the Online Room

Since we cannot see who is coming and going, we shepherd the atmosphere — not the attendance.

Name the atmosphere, not the attendance.

Instead of "some of you are late," say "the atmosphere in this room is shifting." Instead of "people keep leaving," say "the ones who stay will carry something home." This keeps the focus on God's presence — not on people's behavior.
