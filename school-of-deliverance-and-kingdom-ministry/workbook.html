HOW TO USE THIS WORKBOOK

  1. Print or open one Weekly Page before every class.
  2. Fill Sections 1–3 during the class.
  3. Fill Sections 4–10 within 48 hours after the class.
  4. Bring the completed page to your cell group.
  5. Submit to your Cell Mentor before the next class.

A page is counted complete only when every section has been filled and the Scriptures written out in full KJV in your own hand. Writing the verse is part of the learning.
