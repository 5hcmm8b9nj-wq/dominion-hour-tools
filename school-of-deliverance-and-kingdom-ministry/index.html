Three program lengths

Track A

12 Weeks

Foundations Intensive

For new believers and anyone needing personal freedom before ministering to others. One live class each week plus a prayer watch.

Certificate of Deliverance Foundations

Track B

24 Weeks

Two Terms · Deliverance School

Term One forms the minister. Term Two deploys them. For committed members preparing to serve on a prayer or deliverance team.

Diploma in Deliverance Ministry

Track C

52 Weeks

Four Terms · Full School of Ministry

Nature of Christ, Wisdom of Christ, Power of Christ, then Deployment. For ministers-in-training, pastors, intercessors and team leaders.

Certificate in Practical Deliverance Ministry

The five compulsory courses

  1. PNEPneumatology and Redemption RealitiesWho we became in Christ, and the Person, work, gifts and fruit of the Holy Spirit.
  2. DELDeliverance and Spiritual WarfareSetting captives free through the name, blood and authority of Jesus Christ.
  3. PTRPersonal TransformationGrowth of the total man, so the vessel is fit for the anointing.
  4. LEDKingdom LeadershipLeading people, teams and online rooms in order and in servanthood.
  5. FINKingdom Finance and StewardshipGod’s principles for provision, integrity and Kingdom purpose.

The standard class flow

  1. 1Praise and Worship
  2. 2Thanksgiving
  3. 3Confession of Sins
  4. 4Forgive Others
  5. 5Invite the Holy Spirit
  6. 6Teaching
  7. 7Deliverance Prayers and Declarations
  8. 8Sealing — cover with the blood of Jesus

No step is skipped or reordered, in any class, in any track.

Three pillars of Kingdom discipleship

The Nature of Christ

Cultivating the character of Christ by the Word and the supply of the Spirit.

The Wisdom of Christ

Sound doctrine and exposition into the principles of the Kingdom.

The Power of Christ

Empowering transformed believers to become witnesses.

Structure drawn from the Koinonia School of Ministry. Doctrine, Scripture, prayers and declarations are drawn from the Bible itself.
