PART 1 — THE MODEL WE ARE BUILDING FROM

What the Koinonia School of Ministry actually is

Stated plainly, so students know what is borrowed structure and what is our own teaching.

Duration. KSOM runs a six-month curriculum (Koinonia School of Ministry).

Five compulsory courses. Pneumatology · Leadership · Ministry · Personal Transformation · Finance (Koinonia School of Ministry).

Two-fold strategy. Apostle Joshua Selman described the school's method at a KSOM graduation as, first, mentorship — "a systemic strategy that helps to build people methodically" through the five courses — and second, impartation, because "no matter how knowledgeable an individual is you will need empowerment to be fruitful" (KSOM Graduation & Impartation).

Three pillars of Kingdom discipleship.
- The Nature of Christ — cultivating Christ's character by the Word and the supply of the Spirit
- The Wisdom of Christ — sound doctrine and exposition into Kingdom principles
- The Power of Christ — empowering transformed believers to be witnesses
(Koinonia School of Ministry)

Admission posture. Must be born again; a genuine hunger to grow; a lifestyle consistent with Christian values; willingness to dedicate time to classes, assignments, and ministry activities; every applicant prayerfully considered on spiritual readiness and moral standing (KSOM Admissions).

Stated mandate. To raise and release Kingdom Ambassadors "equipped to transform society through sound doctrine, spiritual intelligence, and Christlike character" (Koinonia School of Ministry).

What we keep, and what we change

Kept from the KSOM model
- Five compulsory course pillars
- Mentorship + Impartation as the two-fold method
- The three pillars: Nature · Wisdom · Power of Christ
- Term structure, practicum, and graduation
- Prayerful, character-based admission rather than academic admission

Changed for our two ministries
- Fully online (KSOM currently does not offer virtual learning — KSOM Admissions)
- The Ministry course is expanded into a full Deliverance and Spiritual Warfare pillar, since that is the mandate of both L137 and Dominion Hour
- No educational diploma requirement — our school is open to any born-again believer who can attend and complete assignments
- Every class follows the L137 standard deliverance flow
- Every Scripture is quoted in full KJV, never referenced only

Important distinction. The five-course framework, the three pillars, and the mentorship/impartation method are the teaching model of Apostle Joshua Selman's KSOM, not commands of Scripture. The doctrine, Scriptures, prayers, and declarations inside our syllabus are drawn from the Bible itself.

COURSE 1 — PNEUMATOLOGY AND REDEMPTION REALITIES

Code: PNE

Definition. An exposition into who we became in Christ, and the Person, work, gifts, and fruit of the Holy Spirit.

Why it comes first. Nobody can cast out a devil they are still legally under. Deliverance begins with redemption.

Learning outcomes. By the end the student can:
- Explain the finished work of Christ and the believer's legal standing
- Distinguish the Holy Spirit's voice from soul, flesh, and familiar spirits
- Operate in the gifts of the Spirit with order and accountability
- Teach the new birth to a new convert without error

Core units
1. The New Birth and the Blood of Jesus
2. The Finished Work — what Calvary legally accomplished
3. The Person of the Holy Spirit, not merely the power
4. The Baptism of the Holy Ghost and speaking with new tongues
5. The Nine Gifts of the Spirit and their governance
6. The Fruit of the Spirit as the proof of the Spirit
7. Discerning of spirits — the deliverance minister's primary gift
8. Sonship, authority, and the believer's seat in Christ

COURSE 2 — DELIVERANCE AND SPIRITUAL WARFARE

Code: DEL

Definition. The biblical ministry of setting captives free from demonic oppression, bondage, covenant, curse, and altar — through the name, blood, and authority of Jesus Christ.

Why it is expanded. In KSOM the pillar is simply "Ministry." For L137 and Dominion Hour, deliverance is the ministry.

Learning outcomes. By the end the student can:
- Define demonic oppression biblically and refuse to over-diagnose
- Lead a person through repentance, forgiveness, renunciation, and breaking
- Conduct a full deliverance session following the standard flow
- Provide aftercare so that the person stays free
- Recognise when to refer to pastoral or qualified medical care

Core units
1. The ministry of Jesus as the pattern of deliverance
2. The hierarchy of darkness — Ephesians 6:12 mapping
3. Legal grounds: sin, unforgiveness, covenant, curse, vow, dedication, trauma

8. Renunciation, breaking of covenants, and pulling down of altars
9. Manifestations, order in the room, and protecting the dignity of the person

11. What deliverance is not — never a substitute for repentance, obedience, medical care, or discipleship

PART 4 — THE STANDARD CLASS FLOW

Every single class, in every track, follows the L137 standard deliverance flow. No step is skipped or reordered.

1. Praise and Worship — 10 minutes
2. Thanksgiving — 5 minutes
3. Confession of Sins — 5 minutes
4. Forgive Others — 5 minutes
5. Invite the Holy Spirit — 5 minutes
6. Teaching — 45–60 minutes
7. Deliverance Prayers and Declarations — 20–30 minutes
8. Sealing (cover with the blood of Jesus) — 5 minutes

Then: assignment given · recording and pack released · workbook page completed before next class.

Total session length: 2 hours (Track A and B) · 2.5 hours (Track C).

PART 12 — SAFEGUARDS FOR AN ONLINE DELIVERANCE SCHOOL

Non-negotiable. Read aloud at orientation and at the start of each term.

  1. No public deliverance of an individual on a recorded call. Personal ministry happens in a private, supervised room.
  2. Two-minister rule. No student ever ministers one-to-one alone, especially across genders or to minors.
  3. Nothing leaves the room. No screenshots, no recordings, no names, no forwarding.
  4. No diagnosis by assumption. We do not declare that a symptom, illness, or dream definitely proves demonic activity. We pray, we discern, we test by the Word.
  5. Medical care is honoured. Nobody is ever told to stop medication, skip treatment, or refuse a doctor. Where mental or physical health is involved, refer to qualified care alongside prayer.
  6. No ritual objects. Oil, water, and communion are used only as they point to Christ's finished work — never as power in themselves. No salt baths. No covenant-of-salt language.
  7. No fear-based teaching, no manipulation, no financial pressure.
  8. All authority is delegated. Acts 19:15-16 stands as our warning.

1. Key Biblical Findings

Training and equipping believers is a direct biblical command, not a modern innovation. Paul instructed Timothy to commit what he heard to faithful men who would teach others also, and Ephesians places teachers in the church specifically "for the perfecting of the saints, for the work of the ministry."

Ignorance destroys people. Hosea 4:6 is not about lack of zeal but lack of knowledge — which is exactly why a structured school matters more in deliverance ministry than almost anywhere else.

Jesus trained before He deployed. Mark 3:14-15 shows the order: He ordained the twelve that they should be with him, and then that He might send them forth. Being with Him came before casting out devils. Our Term One (Nature of Christ) follows that order deliberately.

Deliverance is the ministry of Jesus continued. Luke 4:18, Mark 16:17, and 1 John 3:8 make deliverance a normal part of the gospel, not a fringe specialty.

Authority is delegated, never assumed. Luke 10:19 grants real authority; Acts 19:15-16 shows what happens to those who use the name of Jesus without belonging to Jesus. Character training is therefore not optional in a deliverance school.

Freedom must be maintained or it is lost. Matthew 12:43-45 is the entire biblical basis for our aftercare and discipleship requirement. A school that only casts out and never disciples leaves people worse.

Order protects people. 1 Corinthians 14:40 governs our safeguards, our two-minister rule, and our refusal to conduct personal deliverance on a public recorded call.

Character precedes power. Galatians 5:22-23 and 1 Timothy 3:2-3 mean the fruit of the Spirit is the qualifying evidence, not the gifts alone.

On the KSOM model itself: the five courses, three pillars, and mentorship-plus-impartation strategy are the teaching structure of Apostle Joshua Selman's Koinonia School of Ministry (Koinonia School of Ministry; KSOM Graduation & Impartation). They are a wise, proven framework — but they are a human framework. Scripture alone is the authority for everything taught inside it.

3. Practical Christ-Centred Guidance

Start with Track A. Do not launch the 52-week school first. Run one 12-week pilot cohort, keep it small, fix what breaks, then scale. A school that collapses in month three damages trust.

Free the students before you deploy them. Week 10 of Track A and Term One of Track C exist for this reason. A bound minister multiplies bondage.

Protect the vulnerable more than you protect the platform. The safeguards in Part 12 are worth more than any number of enrolments. Keep the two-minister rule absolutely.

Keep the two ministries distinct in publishing. L137 material goes public after anonymising. Dominion Hour transcripts stay private, with only approved derivatives released. Build that into the platform permissions, not just into policy.

Teach doctrine before demonology. Term Two comes before Term Three on purpose. Students who learn warfare before they learn the Word become sensational, fearful, and unteachable.

Say "I don't know" freely. Train students to distinguish what Scripture plainly says from what a minister teaches, and to hold the second loosely.

Never separate deliverance from discipleship. Matthew 12:43-45 makes aftercare a doctrinal requirement, not a courtesy. Every graduate must be able to disciple, not only to cast out.

Honour medicine and honour prayer. Nurses, doctors, and counsellors in your congregations are a gift. Refer without shame.

Build the workbook habit from Week 1. One page a week for 52 weeks becomes a personal deliverance manual the student keeps for life.
