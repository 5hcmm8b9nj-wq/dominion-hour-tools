Our Mandate

A Ministry of the Spirit and of Power

Five pillars guide the work the Lord has set before this house. Each one is rooted in Scripture and confirmed by the testimony of changed lives.

Prophetic Ministry

Hearing God's voice clearly and declaring His counsel over nations, families, and individual destinies through tested, biblically-aligned prophecy.

Deliverance & Freedom

Casting out demonic strongholds, breaking generational curses, and restoring souls into the perfect liberty that is in Christ Jesus.

Intercession & Prayer

Standing in the gap day and night through corporate prayer, fasting, and night watches — partnering with Heaven to shift atmospheres.

Biblical Teaching

Sound expository teaching of the Word of God — building disciples who are mature, discerning, and equipped for every good work.

Discipleship

Raising sons and daughters who walk in spiritual maturity, holiness, and the fear of the Lord — established in their kingdom assignment.

Global Outreach

Carrying the gospel of the Kingdom across nations through digital ministry, crusades, conferences, and on-the-ground mission work.

Prophet Prince Amo David

A vessel set apart for the prophetic and deliverance ministry

Prophet Prince Amo David is the founder and presiding voice of Dominion Hour Ministries International — a ministry birthed out of years of consecration, prayer, and divine encounters. Called from his youth and tested through seasons of fire, he carries a sharp prophetic edge balanced with a pastor's heart.

His ministry has been marked by accurate prophetic words, mighty deliverances, healings, and the restoration of broken lives and destinies. Through digital platforms, live services, and on-ground gatherings, the Prophet reaches souls across continents — declaring that no demon, curse, or yoke can stand where the name of Jesus is exalted.
